### Changed
- **On a phone, the 360°'s tile rises in a sheet from the bottom; the satellites' words are short** (SKY5, #1257; `docs/design/console.md`, « Au téléphone, une feuille qui monte du bas »). Jonathan's choices (27/11, on a mockup). The sheet opens to half the screen, the sky dimmed above it; dragged up, nearly all of it (92 %); dragged down past a third, or by a quick swipe, it closes. Its whole head is dragged, its content scrolls inside it to its very end (its height is what is seen of it). ✕, Escape or a tap on the dimmed sky close it; its ✕ takes the keyboard's focus when it rises. The satellites count what the modules' blocks count, in short words: « 8 VMs · 4 to judge », « 13 VMs · 2 acted », « 13 kept · 1 restoring ». The help line says on a phone what happens there. A desktop is unchanged. Fixed with it, as Jonathan found them on Imladris: the satellites keep their gap from the ring as the centre grows (they went out to the systems' orbit, their words under its bodies), and the table no longer jumps back to the top at each refresh (the focus given back to the view's button scrolled the column up to it).
