Jonathan's choices (27/11, on a mockup). The sheet opens to half the screen, the sky dimmed above it; dragged up, nearly all of it (92 %); dragged down past a third, or by a quick swipe, it closes. A desktop is unchanged: its card floats as before, its ✕ floating, no head (measured against main).
