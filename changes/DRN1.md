### Fixed
- **A session cut still running on Azure is followed, never read as an empty output** (DRN1, #677; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La coupure suivie sur Azure »; `docs/azure.md`, L17). On the bench on 09/11, under the quarantine, the cut of an autonomous isolation took 302 s on Azure (Started, Accepted, Succeeded); the watch waited 300 s, read what the last poll said, no output, and declared the isolation unverified (« sortie de Run Command illisible ») while the NSG held and the cut succeeded 2 s later.
  - The choice: the isolation is verified on the NSG, as before, and the cut is followed apart. The VM's worker still waits 300 s: a 600 s wait would hold every later signal of that VM for 10 min, and the reassertion's thread when it puts an isolation back. Past it, the cut is `pending` and its poller kept by the watch; the cycle keeps the NSG's verdict, its notification and `glorfindel list` say the cut is pending.
  - Every pass of the reassertion reads the Run Command's status again on Azure, under the VM's lock. While it runs, one card « coupure en attente » (`drain_pending`, action `review_drain`): no decision, nothing to approve. Once the sessions are cut, that card closes with « isolement vérifié ». Refused, failed or with sessions left, a `verification_failed` card says « coupure refusée » or « sessions restées ouvertes ». Still running 30 min after it was asked, or no longer followed (the watch restarted, or another process isolated the VM: `glorfindel respond`; the state's `asked_at` is the drain's nonce), it says « coupure jamais aboutie », the sessions « peut-être pas coupées ». The card is written before the state: a card refused (a full disk), the drain is read again at the next pass. With the reassertion off (`GLORFINDEL_REASSERT_INTERVAL_S=0`), nothing reads a pending cut again: the cycle is not verified, « coupure en attente, non suivie ». The cycle's own card, for a cut that ended within 300 s, says the same cases.
  - The cut's cards have their own id (`reassert-drain:…`, a separator no Azure VM name holds) and never fold into another card of the reassertion on the VM, nor the reverse, nor group with it in the console. The continuation token of the poller is never written to disk: `~/.glorfindel` is writable by the console and the bot, and a forged token would make the watch poll a chosen URL with its ARM token. A VM released while its card waits closes the card. For BL1, a cut that ends badly after the isolation was verified reads « contenu, puis l'isolation n'a pas tenu », never « contenu seul ».
  - The restore's neutralization still waits 600 s (152 s measured under the quarantine). Not done, its status is read on Azure and said (`run_command_pending`, `run_command_status`); it is never read as neutralized, nor as refused.
  - The quarantine's rules 110 (DNS) and 111 (IMDS) are untouched: the hypothesis that they hold the guest agent is strongly supported, not told apart, and the test that would settle it is Jonathan's to run on the bench.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_drain_follow.py`, on the in-memory Azure (a Run Command held running, then ended by the test); the invariant machine gains L4 (`guest_agent_held`, `run_commands_end`: a cut still running is said waiting, one that ended is read).
