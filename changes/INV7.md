### Fixed
- **The invariant machine's operator puts back, as designed, the deleted NSG of a NIC left bare whose original nothing records, before the release** (INV7, #1099; `docs/design/preuves.md`). The 0.6.5 release gate (seed 132150572, 2 000 sequences) failed I7 on a sequence that is red on v0.6.4 too: the isolation code has not changed since, and the new seed drew it. The quarantine NSG was in a group the configuration named at the time; the configuration then named no group; a NIC of the VM was left bare by someone, its original lost, and its NSG deleted by the customer. The release refuses an original that a quarantine NSG outside the configured group names, and says how to put the NSG back by hand. That refusal is unchanged and still holds. The oracle was wrong. Its operator skipped such a NIC in its first step because the NSG was gone. It then wrote the original on every quarantine NSG of ours, including the one the release never follows there, and it took no second round. Now the operator recreates the NSG from the design and puts it on the NIC first. Glorfindel cannot know that this NIC was ever in quarantine, and what the release says of every other NIC is checked as before.
