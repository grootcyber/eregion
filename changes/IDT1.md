### Added
- **Entra ID and the control plane are always on the infrastructure map while their rules run, as a VM is** (IDT1, #1182; `docs/design/console.md`, « Entra ID et le plan de contrôle sur la carte »). Jonathan's choices on a mockup (25/11). Their tile only came with a card, then went: its absence said nothing.
  - Shown as soon as a rule of their source (`azure_entra`, `azure_activity`) is enabled and bound to a backend, first in the row, linked to DETECT only.
  - Their health on their dot: red, a rule's poll or the read of one of their tables in error; grey as soon as one of their tables has had no log for the watch's threshold (`log_freshness` in `/api/watch/status`, LOGF1) — the rule of the watch's `logs_silent` card: a fresh table never hides a silent one (Jonathan, 25/11); green otherwise. The folded line names the table silent the longest, or, when calm, shows the oldest log. A watch before LOGF1: green or red, never grey. A read that hangs (`read_at` older than 30 min): grey, never a stale green.
  - Their tables: the field's of their plane (`plane`, LOGF1b), else Entra ID's five tables by name, or AzureActivity. A table put off by the watch's budget (`deferred`) is no failed read: its last log counts, and never read yet it is neither red nor grey.
  - Unfolded as a VM's identity block: the tenant (masked), the workspace, when the watch read its logs, each table's last log, each rule's title (RTT1a, now served by `/api/config`) and state, the managed identities' certificates set aside (MID1) — the ages shown whatever the dot.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_watched_tiles.py`, whose health rule runs the page's own functions in Node or a headless Chromium (`tests/unit/js_engine.py`, the suite's first); mutants M7601–M7610, M7649, M7650, M7659 and M7660 (`scripts/mutants/IDT1.py`).
