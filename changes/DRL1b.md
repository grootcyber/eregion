### Added
- **The weekly drill judged on Imladris: each occurrence's verdict by the code, the absence alarm, the bot's thread and the console's « Weekly drill » line** (DRL1b, #613; `docs/design/drill-banc.md` §4.2, §13). The watch's pull lists the drill's two blobs in the exercise channel (`exercise_channel.pull_drill`): the operator's permit is judged by that listing, never by a copy nor by `drill.json`; the pulse is read by its one reader and kept with it, in one file written whole. Every minute the watch judges the last occurrence (`glorfindel/drill_watch.py`) against the drill's copy in its own image — never the instance's layer —, Orodruin's status of the request and the run's report, the proof as C1 counts it (detected, the decision attached): `proof`, `missed`, `unknown`, `refused`, `skipped`, `stopped`, `not_run`, `rearm_expected`, `self_disarmed`, `channel_unread`, each with the alert and the mention §4.2 gives it. The absence alarm (`not_run`, two hours after the hour, or the pulse older than eight days) is armed only when the channel was listed, the permit is in it, the pulse says armed and both fingerprints are this host's copy's; a channel unread says so without arming anything, a fingerprint Imladris does not have says « re-arm expected ». A drill never armed, disarmed or killed says nothing, between the permit removed and the pulse written too (a grace); a pull that stops is said, never a quiet state without end. The bot posts each verdict once in its own thread (« drill · <name> »), its text through `chat_text.py`, mentioning only the role configured and only where §4.2 does. The console shows the line « Weekly drill » on Dagorlad (`GET /api/dagorlad/drill`), and the tile of the last proof says when it comes from the drill, with the stalest technique beside it. The decision reads none of it (`_build_user_message()` unchanged, to the byte). Nothing is armed: the arming stays the operator's.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_drill_watch.py`, `tests/unit/test_dagorlad_blind.py`. Mutants M5861–M5880 (`scripts/mutants/DRL1b.py`).
