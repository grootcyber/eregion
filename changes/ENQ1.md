- **Design: a model that reasons over a knowledge base that learns, for the enquiry before a decision — the ransomware sheet first, then the note** (ENQ1, #1040; `docs/design/enquete-raisonnee.md`). Read in the code: the enquiry is a switch on three column names, six fixed queries; six of the nine data-plane rules get no enquiry; under Linux two of the three disk-write queries cannot return anything, and the model reads « no result », as an absence.
  - The ransomware sheet: nine hypotheses (what to read, why, what confirms or refutes the attack), each with its sources (ATT&CK T1486 and T1490, Microsoft Learn) and the bench measurements already made, dated. The bench measurement of 12/11 fixes two of them: the minute average separates the backup spike of 10/11 from the bench attack; the host metric agrees with the guest within 15 % and so separates nothing. Case by case, what the sheet would have changed: since the rule's ceiling, the model no longer decides the isolation on this rule; the gain is the card, and the ceiling stays.
  - The base: a seed reviewed in the repository, its changes in the watch's state, a weight per source (the bench, a human verdict, the dossier, the model never alone), and the asymmetry: what tightens applies, what relaxes waits for a human; neither ever touches a guard or a ceiling. Who can teach the base what, and with what effect.
  - The closed catalogue (`lire(id)`: the model never chooses a query, a value or a VM), six states of a reading that are never confused (rows, empty, not applicable, pending, failed, refused), the mandatory readings of the grave techniques, the budget, the enquiry once and three draws on the same evidence, the citations checked by the code, the guards (the telemetry guard extended; the new guard as an extension of the snapshot-only floor, a card that proposes to contain, never an action raised).
  - The measure: one harness session for the enquiry and the precedents of MEM2 (five configurations, seven cases, a planted log line), then one end-to-end run for both; sixteen hypotheses to measure, the lots ENQ2 to ENQ6 with MEM2 and MEM-H, and nine questions to the maintainer.
