### Changed
- **The watch's loop is out of the CLI, its state in objects, each part reached alone by a test; a ceiling on the big files that can only go down** (WCH1, #1019; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Où vit la watch »). `glorfindel watch` was one function of 947 lines in `glorfindel/cli.py`, with 32 nested functions and its state in closures no test reached alone. It is now a package, `glorfindel/watch/`, split by concern — the startup in its order (`loop.py`, `startup.py`), runs/ read, admitted and bounded (`runs_channel.py`), a VM's queue and its thread (`dispatch.py`), the minute's tasks (`housekeeping.py`), the passes one at a time (`passes.py`), the heartbeats (`beat.py`), the rules, discovery and the startup audit (`rules.py`) — and nothing changed in what the watch does: the same lines said, the same signals and threads, in the same order. `glorfindel watch` stays the command; it hands its console and its displays to `glorfindel.watch.loop.run`. `scripts/check_paragraphs.py --code` finds every line of the old file again, `self.` and comment marks aside; what changed form is a closure's cell become an attribute, a call given its console, a thread started by `Solo`, the housekeeping's ten guarded calls become one table.
  - The guard of size (`tests/unit/test_file_sizes.py`): a ceiling per big file — `glorfindel/cli.py`, `glorfindel/agent.py`, `eregion_console/api.py`, `glorfindel/bot.py` — set at its size after this lot. A file over its ceiling fails; a file more than 100 lines under it fails too, until its ceiling comes down (the ratchet). No module of `glorfindel/watch/` beyond 300 lines, no function nor method beyond 50.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_watch_loop.py` (the tick and its cadences, a task that fails, the channel's room, a VM's queue and its thread, the passes one at a time, the heartbeats, the rules kept when what follows them fails, what the startup says), `tests/unit/test_file_sizes.py`; the tests that ran the whole watch patch its new place. Mutants M7005–M7019 (`scripts/mutants/WCH1.py`); the 38 mutants anchored in the old `watch()` moved with it, the same defect at its new place.
