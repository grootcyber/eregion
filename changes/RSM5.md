### Changed
- **The reassertion's table is its reference, read again on today's code** (RSM5, #1037; `docs/design/reaffirmation.md`; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Réaffirmation »; `docs/historique.md`, « La réaffirmation, lot par lot »). The six paragraphs of the Glorfindel doc on the reassertion (29 KB, lot after lot since L5, RSM1's link included) become a summary of what it does, verified in the code, and a link to the table, which has been the reference since the maintainer's decision of 21/11 (RSM1, Q1). Their history moves to the history page, word for word. Every row of the table now cites its `file:line` in the current code, its rows without one included. The table also writes down what RSM3 and REL068b added and it lacked:
  - a perimeter pair unread where it would be put back is deferred, not spent;
  - a put-back never lands on an NSG other than the one its entry records;
  - the `perimeter_off` card closes once the pair reads on the subnet's NSG again, its mark set or not.
  - It corrects what it said wrong: a perimeter block alone whose cards or perimeter cannot be read gets an `unverified` card, not « on ne sait pas »; L1 judges a block on a reassigned address by content since RSM2a, no longer by name. And it says what it left out: the card of a VM skipped pass after pass stays pending across a restart, until the VM is skipped as often again.
  - The rule « a change to the reassertion, to its code or to its oracle, updates the table in the same pull request » is written in `CLAUDE_GLORFINDEL.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`. `GLORFINDEL_REASSERT_INTERVAL_S` is listed in `docs/operations.md`. No code changes.
  - Tests: `python scripts/check_paragraphs.py --old origin/main:docs/glorfindel.md` finds every paragraph again; `tests/unit/test_docs_budget.py`, `test_doc_anchors.py`, `test_host_name.py`, `test_release.py`, `test_manuel.py`, `test_french_output.py`.
