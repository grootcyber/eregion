### Changed
- **The 360°'s lines follow the legend** (LEG2, #1244; `docs/design/console.md`, « Les fils de la 360° suivent la légende »). Jonathan's choice (26/11): a card waiting for a human is drawn in its action's colour, dotted and flowing, from the backend that acts — a detection to judge or a rule proposed in blue from its workspace, an isolation to approve (or a check that failed) in red and a block in orange from the NSG, a restore to approve in green from the vault, another card in purple from PROTECT. Before, every card waiting was purple. An action Glorfindel took alone waits for no one: no dotted line. Out of reach, a card is a detection's only. A planet on which only verdicts wait is blue, as the legend's « a verdict waits ». On a phone, the 360°'s legend says its lines. A line waiting is never lost under the line done of its colour (an isolation and its check that failed): it runs a step beside it.
