### Fixed
- **The watch's own return channel no longer fires `data-exfiltration-blob`, and a card attributed to no VM no longer names one** (SLF1, #929; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La voie de retour de la watch »). On Imladris, the watch's writes to its return channel (`heartbeat.json`, `detections/<run>/<signal>.json`, PutBlob from its private address) made the rule fire about every 20 minutes, a hundred recurrences in three days, each card naming a VM of the bench as its resource. The shipped query now keeps, per caller address and account, who wrote in which container and how many of the writes are objects a return channel holds (`BlobWriters`); the RulePoller sets aside only the writes made by the identity the watch writes with (the `oid` of its own storage token), in the container of its configured `EREGION_WATCH_CHANNEL`, on those objects. Another write from the same address, another container of the same account, another identity in the container, no channel configured, the watch's identity not read: a card, as before. What is set aside is counted on the rule (`rule_status.json`, `own_channel`; the console's rule line, « N own »), written to the watch's log, never silent; the column is taken off before any signal, so the row the model reads is the one of before, to the byte. The same reading on an `attack_started`'s poll: the watch's heartbeat is no longer taken for a run's detection.
  - A card on a detection attributed to no VM keeps where it is filed (its anchor VM, its type, what is approvable, the attribution guard: unchanged) and says « not attributed » with its candidates in TO JUDGE, on its card, in the console's title, on Discord (« non attribuée — candidates : … ») and in `glorfindel pending`; it never folds into a card of its anchor VM.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_own_channel.py`; `tests/unit/kql_subset.py` reads `split`, an index on an array, `bag_pack`, `sum`, `make_list`.
