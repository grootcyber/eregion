- **The contract arms FOR Stratus — passive unless Stratus holds nothing but its closed role, on a cold dedicated subscription** (R2c, pièce 2, docs/design/stratus-cible.md « Le code hermétique de R2c »). ### Security
- **`orodruin-kill` cuts the Stratus identity's role too, not only Orodruin's** (R2c, docs/design/stratus-cible.md §1). Once Stratus runs under a dedicated identity on a dedicated subscription, `orodruin-kill` — which revokes Orodruin's own identity on the contract's and the VM's subscriptions — would leave the Stratus identity's role untouched. Orodruin cannot revoke it itself (on the dedicated subscription it holds only `AUTHZ_READ_ONLY`, and Stratus's closed role has no `Authorization/*/write`), so the revocation is the operator's own gesture, which `kill.py` now tools: given the Stratus object id and the dedicated subscription (`--stratus-oid` / `--stratus-subscription`, from arguments or a Terraform output, never hardcoded), right after Orodruin's own revocation it lists the Stratus identity's assignments with the M191 pattern (`--all` so an assignment posed on a resource group does not escape, `--assignee-object-id` for the identity, `--subscription` for the dedicated one), deletes them, and reads again — « nothing left » is proven from a listing, never assumed. An empty listing is the proof « nothing to revoke », not a failure. Without the two arguments the step is simply absent. The dedicated subscription is never printed (M124). The step is scoped and testable; the dedicated identity, its closed role and the contract's refusal of a colliding `EREGION_STRATUS_SUBSCRIPTION` are the lot's next pieces.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_orodruin.py` (the step runs right after the revocation when given, is skipped when absent, and a failure is reported without stopping the rest) and `tests/unit/test_orodruin_kill.py` (the role is revoked and proven gone with the M191 listing; an assignment still listed after the deletion is an error; nothing to revoke is the proof, not a failure; the step is absent without an identity; the dry run lists but deletes nothing; the dedicated subscription is never printed; `main` wires the two arguments and hides the subscription). Mutants M4440 (the revocation never run), M4441 (run in the wrong place), M4442 (« nothing left » assumed), M4443 (the deletion skipped), M4444 (the identity filter dropped), M4445 (`--all` dropped — the M191 bug), M4446 (the absent-identity guard inverted), M4447 (the subscription no longer redacted), `scripts/mutants/R2c.py`.
