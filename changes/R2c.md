### Added
- **The contract arms FOR Stratus — passive unless Stratus holds nothing but its closed role, on a cold dedicated subscription** (R2c, pièce 2, docs/design/stratus-cible.md « Le code hermétique de R2c »). A `stratus` guarantee joins `orodruin-check`'s readers (`eregion_core/orodruin.py`), active only when `EREGION_STRATUS_SUBSCRIPTION` names Stratus's dedicated subscription — unset, the contract stays Orodruin's own, unchanged (the non-regression measured at réel 19/11). Then Orodruin arms for Stratus only if, on that subscription: Stratus holds **nothing but its closed role** (the allow-list of the 31 measured actions, SEC11 — any action beyond it, a `Microsoft.Authorization` action or a wildcard is more than the role; the role is identified by its **actions**, never a role-definition id hardcoded (Jonathan, 19/11), and a test holds the allow-list EQUAL to `infra/orodruin/stratus_guardrails.tf`'s role, against drift); the subscription is **cold** (no resource group but Azure's own — `NetworkWatcherRG`, `DefaultResourceGroup-*`, M6); its activity-log **diagnostic** routes to a live workspace. It **refuses** a subscription that is the bench's, the VM's, Imladris's or Orodruin's own (`AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` + the operator's `EREGION_PROTECTED_SUBSCRIPTIONS`) — the v1 blocker — and never takes an empty or partial listing for an absence (M5, `stratus_identity_unprovable`). It reads (a) Stratus's assignments with Orodruin's `AUTHZ_READ_ONLY` token, (b) the groups and the diagnostic with Stratus's token, by Orodruin's process outside the sandbox. **Fail closed**: a read that cannot happen — or the federated credential not yet wired (pièce 4) — leaves it passive, never armed.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_stratus_contract.py` (the arming logic on a fake dedicated subscription — armed, and each passive reason; the reader with an injected `get` and fake tokens reads (a) with Orodruin's token filtered by Stratus's oid and (b) with Stratus's token; a forbidden subscription refused before any token; the allow-list held EQUAL to the Terraform; a partial reader set has no `stratus` and is skipped, not a crash). Mutants M4461–M4466 (`scripts/mutants/R2c.py`).
- **The hardened sandbox — the IMDS is unreachable from Stratus's network, and `orodruin-check` verifies it** (R2c, pièce 3, docs/design/stratus-cible.md « Le code hermétique de R2c »). At Orodruin's startup (`infra/orodruin/main.tf`, cloud-init) a dedicated docker network `172.30.250.0/24` carries Stratus's egress to ARM, and a systemd service — after `docker.service`, so the `DOCKER-USER` chain already exists — drops its traffic to the IMDS (`169.254.169.254`): a Stratus container on that network cannot obtain Orodruin's managed-identity token (M4: IMDS 000 measured, bridge witness 200). `ORO_HARDEN` is unchanged; never `--network host`, the docker socket, nor `NET_ADMIN` (pièce 4 runs Stratus on that network). The contract's sandbox guarantee (`eregion_core/orodruin.py`, `read_sandbox` / `sandbox_drops_imds`) reads the `DOCKER-USER` rule before arming for Stratus — read-only, nothing changed; the rule absent, or unreadable (no privilege to read iptables), leaves it **passive** (fail closed). The rule binds the same network and IMDS address the contract reads, held equal by the tests.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_stratus_contract.py` (the rule parsed from `iptables -S DOCKER-USER`: a drop to the IMDS from the sandbox network counts, one to another destination or from another network does not, an accept does not; `read_sandbox` inert without the subscription, arms with the rule, flags a missing one, fails closed when iptables cannot be read) and `tests/unit/test_orodruin.py` (the cloud-init poses the network, the systemd service after docker, and the DROP rule). ### Security
- **`orodruin-kill` cuts the Stratus identity's role too, not only Orodruin's** (R2c, docs/design/stratus-cible.md §1). Once Stratus runs under a dedicated identity on a dedicated subscription, `orodruin-kill` — which revokes Orodruin's own identity on the contract's and the VM's subscriptions — would leave the Stratus identity's role untouched. Orodruin cannot revoke it itself (on the dedicated subscription it holds only `AUTHZ_READ_ONLY`, and Stratus's closed role has no `Authorization/*/write`), so the revocation is the operator's own gesture, which `kill.py` now tools: given the Stratus object id and the dedicated subscription (`--stratus-oid` / `--stratus-subscription`, from arguments or a Terraform output, never hardcoded), right after Orodruin's own revocation it lists the Stratus identity's assignments with the M191 pattern (`--all` so an assignment posed on a resource group does not escape, `--assignee-object-id` for the identity, `--subscription` for the dedicated one), deletes them, and reads again — « nothing left » is proven from a listing, never assumed. An empty listing is the proof « nothing to revoke », not a failure. Without the two arguments the step is simply absent. The dedicated subscription is never printed (M124). The step is scoped and testable; the dedicated identity, its closed role and the contract's refusal of a colliding `EREGION_STRATUS_SUBSCRIPTION` are the lot's next pieces.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_orodruin.py` (the step runs right after the revocation when given, is skipped when absent, and a failure is reported without stopping the rest) and `tests/unit/test_orodruin_kill.py` (the role is revoked and proven gone with the M191 listing; an assignment still listed after the deletion is an error; nothing to revoke is the proof, not a failure; the step is absent without an identity; the dry run lists but deletes nothing; the dedicated subscription is never printed; `main` wires the two arguments and hides the subscription). Mutants M4440 (the revocation never run), M4441 (run in the wrong place), M4442 (« nothing left » assumed), M4443 (the deletion skipped), M4444 (the identity filter dropped), M4445 (`--all` dropped — the M191 bug), M4446 (the absent-identity guard inverted), M4447 (the subscription no longer redacted), `scripts/mutants/R2c.py`.
