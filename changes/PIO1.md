### Added
- **Who writes under Linux, declared to be measured at the bench** (PIO1, #1258; `docs/operations.md`, “Qui écrit sous Linux, au banc (PIO1)”). Under Linux the Azure Monitor agent sends no `Process` counter to Log Analytics, so the enquiry could not say who writes; its one way is the OpenTelemetry metric `process.disk.io`, sent to an Azure Monitor workspace and read in PromQL.
  - The collection, off by default (`process_io_enabled`, `infra/terraform/process_io.tf`): an Azure Monitor workspace, a data collection rule of its own that collects `process.disk.io` alone (the `Microsoft-OtelPerfMetrics` stream under `performanceCountersOTel`, written with azapi at API `2024-03-11`, which azurerm 4.81.0 cannot express), its association with each bench VM, and `Monitoring Reader` on the workspace for the operator who applies. The rule's own collection rule is untouched. Targeted Make targets: `make celebrimbor-process-io-plan`, `make celebrimbor-process-io-up`; outputs `process_io_workspace_id`, `process_io_workspace_name`, `process_io_query_endpoint`.
  - The watch's right, off by default (`watch_process_io_read_enabled`, `infra/glorfindel-host/watch_process_io.tf`): `Monitoring Reader` (`*/read`: the workspace's settings and data, no write) on the bench's Azure Monitor workspace alone, for Imladris's managed identity; the role resolved at the plan (`azurerm_role_definition`), which stops if it does not resolve. That it is enough for PromQL, or that Imladris's `Reader` on the bench's group is already enough, is to be measured; if not, the reader says so, and a custom role reduced to `microsoft.monitor/accounts/data/metrics/read` follows.
  - The reader (`glorfindel/providers/azure/prometheus.py`): one PromQL instant query on the workspace's own host, read only, no redirection; the top writers by executable over the five minutes before the signal; its own budget (`GLORFINDEL_PROM_BUDGET_PER_MIN`, 30 by default); a 403, an absent workspace or an unreadable answer is a failure, said; no workspace or no series for the VM is “without object”, never “nothing written”. The process's name is the guest's word (`/proc/[pid]/status`): no hypothesis that relaxes leans on it.
  - The reading `process_write_io` in the enquiry's catalogue, kept apart (`catalog.A_MESURER`, never `READS`): neither `investigate`, nor `decide`, nor the loop runs it, and nothing the model sees changes; `scripts/capture_enquete.py --prom-endpoint` (`make enquete-capture … ENQ_PROM=…`) reads it apart (`a_mesurer`), never among the readings the harness replays. `top_write_processes` and `backup_agent_check` stay without object under Linux.
  - Not measured, said in the docs: the series' and labels' names in PromQL, that the bench's Linux agent collects the metric (no minimum version documented), that the role is enough for PromQL, the ingestion latency. Expected cost: about 0.14 USD a VM a month at the public retail price (0.11 USD per 10 million samples ingested), about 0.29 USD for the bench's two VMs and 1.43 USD for ten.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_process_io.py`, `infra/terraform/tests/process_io.tftest.hcl`, `infra/glorfindel-host/tests/watch_process_io.tftest.hcl`.
