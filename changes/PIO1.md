### Added
- **Who writes under Linux, declared to be measured at the bench** (PIO1, #1258; `docs/operations.md`, “Qui écrit sous Linux, au banc (PIO1)”). Under Linux the Azure Monitor agent sends no `Process` counter to Log Analytics, so the enquiry could not say who writes; its one way is the OpenTelemetry metric `process.disk.io`, sent to an Azure Monitor workspace and read in PromQL.
  - The collection, off by default (`process_io_enabled`, `infra/terraform/process_io.tf`): an Azure Monitor workspace, a data collection rule of its own that collects `process.disk.io` alone (the `Microsoft-OtelPerfMetrics` stream under `performanceCountersOTel`, written with azapi at API `2024-03-11`, which azurerm 4.81.0 cannot express), and its association with each bench VM. The rule's own collection rule is untouched. Targeted Make targets: `make celebrimbor-process-io-plan`, `make celebrimbor-process-io-up`; outputs `process_io_workspace_id`, `process_io_workspace_name`, `process_io_query_endpoint`.
  - No new right (the maintainer's decision): the watch would read the workspace with the `Reader` it already has on the bench's group, the operator with the Owner role. Built-in reading roles carry no data action; that `*/read` is enough for PromQL is the one hypothesis: the bench says, under the operator's Owner, whether a data action is asked, and ENQ5b's end-to-end run, under the watch's identity, whether `*/read` alone is enough. If not, the reader says so, and a later lot declares a custom role reduced to `microsoft.monitor/accounts/data/metrics/read`.
  - The reader (`glorfindel/providers/azure/prometheus.py`): one PromQL instant query on the workspace's own host, read only, no redirection; the top writers by executable over the five minutes before the signal; its own budget (`GLORFINDEL_PROM_BUDGET_PER_MIN`, 30 by default); a 403, an absent workspace or an unreadable answer is a failure, said; no workspace or no series for the VM is “without object”, never “nothing written”. The process's name is the guest's word (`/proc/[pid]/status`): no hypothesis that relaxes leans on it.
  - The reading `process_write_io` in the enquiry's catalogue, kept apart (`catalog.A_MESURER`, never `READS`): neither `investigate`, nor `decide`, nor the loop runs it, and nothing the model sees changes; `scripts/capture_enquete.py --prom-endpoint` (`make enquete-capture … ENQ_PROM=…`) reads it apart (`a_mesurer`), never among the readings the harness replays. `top_write_processes` and `backup_agent_check` stay without object under Linux.
  - Not measured, said in the docs: the series' and labels' names in PromQL, that the bench's Linux agent collects the metric (no minimum version documented), that `*/read` is enough for PromQL, the ingestion latency. Expected cost: about 0.14 USD a VM a month at the public retail price (0.11 USD per 10 million samples ingested), about 0.29 USD for the bench's two VMs and 1.43 USD for ten.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_process_io.py`, `infra/terraform/tests/process_io.tftest.hcl`.
