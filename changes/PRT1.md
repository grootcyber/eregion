- **The watch's ceiling of calls to the model holds its bound: every call a decision may make is reserved at its admission, and what it did not make is given back when it ends** (PRT1, #1060; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La file de décision » ; `docs/raisonnement.md` §2.5). `DecisionGate.admit` reserved one call and the confirming draws (D-1) were counted after the decision: the VMs' threads admitted together passed the bound — five low detections made fifteen calls for a bound of six, a burst of eight grave and two low ones thirty calls in the minute for ten. A decision now reserves `decision_draws` (three by default, never more than its class's room: six for the low class, four for the grave ones), counted while it is in flight however long it takes; when it ends, its reserve is given back but the calls it made, counted from its end (one in human_only or non_disruptive, or when a gate held the action before the draws; none when the model was never reached). A call that failed stays counted: a failed cycle, or a graph that raised, keeps its whole reserve. At any moment the calls counted in the last minute stay at most ten, the low class's at most six, and the grave ones keep their four places; the bounds themselves are unchanged. The price, said: the low class admits at most two decisions of three draws a minute, or four of one call.
