### Added
- **The memory of decisions measured on a copy of a real store, offline, and the choice put to the maintainer** (MEM1, #1016; `docs/design/memoire.md`). `scripts/memory_replay.py` replays a copy of the store, as `make imladris-pull` brings it back, signal by signal: each real cycle is ranked against the real cycles stored before it, within the age bound, by the code's ranking (same rule, then same VM, then the distance; one precedent per VM; three places) and by others (the date; the technique, then the date or the distance).
  - It runs offline: the embedded Chroma client with its telemetry off, the hub offline, and Chroma's ONNX model read from the disk, never fetched. It refuses the operator's store, under HOME and under the home the system gives him, as well as any folder that is not a store.
  - It prints aggregated numbers only. A rule's name is printed only when it is a shipped rule's, and a technique only when it reads as an ATT&CK id.
  - On the bench's copy of 21/11, 479 signals give only 6 distinct query texts: the query is a template, and the detected row never enters it. The distance and the date give the same precedents 21 times out of 479. The share of precedents bearing the signal's technique is 77.3% by the distance, 80.2% by the date, and 92.9% with the technique among the keys, whether the date or the distance comes last.
  - The note lays out the options (a table by keys and date with chromadb out of the dependencies; Chroma kept with the technique among the keys; a corpus where meaning matters, for the enquiry; the harness first), what each changes of what the model sees, and the questions to the maintainer. The memory itself does not change.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_memory_replay.py`, on stores written by the real memory with the embedded Chroma client (mutants M6957–M6961, `scripts/mutants/MEM1.py`).
