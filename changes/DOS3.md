### Added
- **The dossier of an isolation, step 0: at the end of each cycle in scope, the facts the cycle already holds, written beside the card with no call to Azure nor to a model** (DOS3, #1043; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le dossier d'une isolation » ; design: `docs/design/dossier-isolation.md` §3.1, §3.5 to §3.7, §4.1, §4.5, §4.6). On bree's night (10/11), the operator read by hand what the cycle already knew. `store_cycle` now writes `~/.glorfindel/dossier/<vm>-<h8>-<s8>.json` (`glorfindel/dossier.py`, the facts in `glorfindel/dossier_local.py`): the VM's past cards for the same rule over 10 days and their verdicts (F3c), the login lines the model saw on a brute force (F4c), the session drain (F5b, pending while DRN1 follows it), the verification (F5c), and the decision as it was taken (F6a to F6h: the rule's fingerprint and row, the mode, the threshold and what held, the draws, the model and its sampling, the version, what each query of the enquiry could and could not say, real or exercise). Each fact says its source, its author, what it read and its state: read, empty, without object (the Process counter under Linux), unreadable with its reason, pending. An unreadable source is never written empty; a fact that breaks is unreadable, never the dossier lost. The debug line names the file (`dossier`), never its content.
  - The scope (the operator's decision of 12/11): every isolation executed, and every card that proposes `isolate_vm` or `block_suspicious_ip` without executing it (held by the mode, the confidence, the draws, a gate; the floor's card, the ceiling's). Never a dry run, a cycle of the control plane or of an identity, a snapshot alone. An exercise has its dossier, marked exercise.
  - At most `GLORFINDEL_DOSSIER_MAX_PER_H` (6) dossiers composed per hour for the whole watch (counted by each process, the facts read outside its lock); past it, or at 0, a dossier « not composed » that says why — never silence. Kept 30 days after the lift (a VM isolated again while isolated is still held), then removed by the watch's housekeeping (once an hour, local files only; nothing done while an isolation state cannot be read).
  - Written by Glorfindel (the watch, and `respond` out of dry run), never by the console nor the bot; whole, 0600 (its directory put back to 0700), never through a link (a link in its place is replaced, a directory that is a link refused). Nothing the model sees changes: the dossier enters neither the signal, nor the memory, nor the message, frozen to the byte by a test; no gate imports it (a test). `glorfindel/agent.py` slimmed by six lines (its ceiling holds, WCH1).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_dossier.py`; `tests/unit/test_watch_loop.py` (the housekeeping's order). Mutants M6980–M6983 and M7075–M7077 (`scripts/mutants/DOS3.py`).
