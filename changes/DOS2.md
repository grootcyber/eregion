### Added
- **A cycle's debug line says what the decision was taken with: the model, its sampling, the settings in force, the version of Glorfindel and the rule that fired** (DOS2, #1042; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La décision notée »; design: `docs/design/dossier-isolation.md` §3.7). On bree's night (10/11), the line named neither the model nor the temperature: `decision_temperature` read `null` with the default model, which refuses a fixed temperature, and nothing said which version or which query had fired. The line (`runs/glorfindel/<run_id>_debug.jsonl`, written by `store_cycle` through `glorfindel/cycle_line.py`) now carries `decision_model` (the model called, a failed call included), `decision_sampling` (the temperature asked, whether the model refused it, and `fixed` or `provider_default`), `confirming_sampling` (the confirming draws, always the provider's default), `confidence_threshold` and `decision_draws_required` (the settings in force; a malformed threshold is `null`, never `Infinity`), `glorfindel_version` (`{ref, revision, package}`) and, on a detection, `rule_fingerprint` (the rule's name and the first eight hex characters of the sha256 of the query that fired: the one the watch's rule poller loaded at its start, kept in the process and never in the signal — the watch does not reload its rules, so after an approval or an edited client layer the disk no longer says what runs —, else the polled `detection_query`, else the rules on disk). A field that cannot be read never costs the line.
  - Nothing the model sees changes: the memory's `cycle` (which feeds `past_cycles`) and the message to the model are frozen to the byte by a test, at their value before the lot; the new fields reach neither the memory nor the signal.
  - `version()` moved from `eregion_console/sections.py` to `eregion_core/version.py`: the watch reads it without importing the console (D1), and the console (`/api/sections`) imports it from there. `glorfindel/agent.py` slimmed by six lines (its ceiling holds, WCH1).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_cycle_line.py`; `tests/unit/test_console.py` patches the version at its new place. Mutants M6977–M6979 and M7068–M7070 (`scripts/mutants/DOS2.py`); M567's anchor moved with `version()`.
