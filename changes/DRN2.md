### Fixed
- **A session cut announced pending is closed, once: its outcome and when it was read so, in the VM's Discord thread when the bot is configured, else by the webhook, and on the console's card** (DRN2, #754; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La coupure suivie sur Azure »). On the bench on 12/11 the cut of gondolin's isolation ran 304 s on Azure: the autonomous isolation's notification said « coupure en attente … suivie », the reassertion read it drained at its next pass, 23 s after its end — and no message said so; the Discord card stayed on « en attente ». Every cut the watch announced pending now gets a closure (`glorfindel/vm_notices.py`, called by `glorfindel/drain_watch.py`): cut (« Coupure des sessions aboutie sur …, relue N s après la demande »), refused, sessions left open, never ended, or no longer followed (its VM released, or isolated again, before its end). The bot posts it in the thread where the VM's cards are, reopened or created without pinging the role; without the bot, the webhook carries it; a failed outcome keeps its `verification_failed` card, which the closure names. The console's ISOLATED badge says the cut: « cut pending », then « sessions cut · N s », « ⚠ cut refused », « ⚠ sessions open » or « ⚠ cut never ended », the closure's words on hover (`/api/state`, `drain_view`; a cut that ended within its first wait adds nothing).
  - **Once per cut, by its nonce** (the VM and `asked_at`), even after a restart: the notice is recorded in `~/.glorfindel/vm_notices.jsonl` before it goes anywhere, and the same key says nothing again; the bot keeps the keys it posted (`bot_notices_posted.json`) and posts no notice older than 24 h. Its card, then the state — the outcome read on Azure, and a mark `drain_closure_due` —, then the closure, then the mark taken off (L1A): a card refused leaves the cut pending; a closure that cannot be said (a full disk, the watch stopped) is said from the state at the next pass that finds the mark. At most once: a webhook that fails is not retried. A cut asked again by the same watch before its end is closed « reprise avant sa fin ».
  - **A notice decides nothing**: the console and the bot write `~/.glorfindel` too, so the watch only reads in the store whether a closure was said, never an outcome — a forged « aboutie » never changes a cut's state, and the console takes a notice's words only when they say the state's outcome. A forger can still silence a closure or have one said twice; open until FS1f (`docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md` §3.2). A store with bytes that are not UTF-8 stops neither the follow of the cuts nor the bot's turn.
