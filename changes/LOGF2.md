### Fixed
- **The silence of AuditLogs and SigninLogs is judged by Entra, read on Microsoft Graph: a calm tenant is no longer a `logs_silent` card, a cut collection still is; without the right, their threshold is 7 days, said** (LOGF2, #1233; `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/operations.md`, « La fraîcheur des journaux »). In 0.6.9 the card opened on AuditLogs after 26 h without a log, while the bench's tenant had simply not changed. The watch now reads Entra's last event older than the ingestion's delay (15 min, a hypothesis to measure on the bench) — `GET /auditLogs/directoryAudits?$top=1&$orderby=activityDateTime desc&$filter=activityDateTime le <now − 15 min>` for AuditLogs, `GET /auditLogs/signIns?$top=1&$filter=createdDateTime le <now − 15 min>` for SigninLogs, a GET of the existing read-only reader, every 15 min on a Graph budget of its own (`GLORFINDEL_GRAPH_BUDGET_PER_MIN`, 30). The card opens only when that event is more recent than the last log the workspace received, and closes only when the last log catches up with it: a tenant whose events come every few minutes no longer hides a cut. Entra with no such event is never a card; the card says both dates. A 403 (`AuditLog.Read.All` missing), Graph unreadable, a date past the cutoff, or a read the budget kept putting off for 30 min is said — never « nothing » — and the threshold becomes 7 days; a card the comparison opened is not closed by a 403. The tables of Entra with no equivalent on Graph v1.0 (the service principals', non-interactive and managed identities' sign-ins, listed by Graph's beta only) keep LOGF1's 24 h. AuditLogs and SigninLogs are read over 14 days; AzureActivity keeps its 24 h. Graph is read in the watch's own tenant: a workspace fed by another tenant is a known limit. The Entra ID tile follows the watch's verdict and shows, per table, Entra's event next to the last log received, or « Graph unread: … — threshold 7 days ».
