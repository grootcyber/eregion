### Fixed
- **The silence of a table of Entra ID is judged by Entra, read on Microsoft Graph: a calm tenant is no longer a `logs_silent` card; without the right, the threshold of Entra's tables is 7 days, said** (LOGF2, #1233; `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/operations.md`, « La fraîcheur des journaux »). In 0.6.9 the card opened on AuditLogs after 26 h without a log, while the bench's tenant had simply not changed. The watch now reads Entra's last event on Graph — `GET /auditLogs/directoryAudits?$top=1&$orderby=activityDateTime desc` for AuditLogs, `GET /auditLogs/signIns?$top=1&$orderby=createdDateTime desc` for SigninLogs, a GET of the existing read-only reader, every 15 min on a Graph budget of its own (`GLORFINDEL_GRAPH_BUDGET_PER_MIN`, 30) — and opens the card of a table of Entra only when Entra has an event more recent than the last log the workspace received, for more than 15 min (`INGEST_DELAY_S`); the card says both dates. Entra with no event is never a card. A 403 (`AuditLog.Read.All` missing), Graph unreadable, or a table with no equivalent on Graph v1.0 (the service principals' sign-ins, listed by Graph's beta only) is said — never « nothing » — and the threshold of that table becomes 7 days; a card the comparison opened is not closed by a 403. The tables of Entra are read over 14 days; AzureActivity keeps its 24 h. The Entra ID tile follows the watch's verdict and shows, per table, Entra's last event next to the last log received, or « Graph unread: … — threshold 7 days ».
