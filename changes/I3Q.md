### Fixed
- **A perimeter block says a NIC its verification couldn't read unread, never « the IP still reaches them », and says it beside the NICs proven open** (I3Q, #771; `glorfindel/providers/azure/blocks.py`, `docs/azure.md`, `docs/design/preuves.md`). What I3N (#746) left open: the branch `False` of I3P. When the verification that a perimeter block makes right after its write concludes `False` (a NIC proven open, or a block of ours bypassed), `_block_ip_subnet` took off its list the NICs proven held (`blocked_nics`, `held_by_perimeter`) and kept every other NIC off the perimeter « not behind it, {ip} still reaches them » (`uncovered_nics`). Among them were the NICs the verification couldn't read: their rules, their subnet or their perimeter answered 429 (`nics_unknown`, `perimeter_unread`), or the VM's NICs couldn't be listed. The claim was positive and made without a read. The answer also held one key only, `uncovered_nics` or `nics_unread`, never both. Jonathan decided it on 12/11: say them unread. The block now sorts the NICs off its perimeter into three kinds. The NICs proven held stay off the lists, as before. The NICs proven open (`open_nics`: nothing of ours blocks them) go in `uncovered_nics`, with « still reaches them ». The others go in `nics_unread`, each with its name and why (« db-nic-0 (périmètre illisible) »; for a NIC the verification couldn't judge, its own reason, now given by `verify_block_ip` in `nics_unknown_why`: quarantine, subnet NSG, rules or precedence unreadable; « cartes illisibles » when it couldn't list the NICs), with « unread … whether {ip} reaches them is unverified ». A perimeter alone whose own pair is bypassed or gone stops its verification before the NICs off it: the block now asks each of them itself (`verify_block._sort_off_perimeter`, `_perimeter_holds`), held, unknown or reached. The review of the first fix found it saying « non jugée … unverified » of a NIC the IP reaches there, where `main` said it reached. A NIC with a block of its own is never asked on its perimeter alone (« non lue »), and a pair gone is said (`missing_rules`, `blocked_partial`). Both keys can be said together, the note says both, and the status stays `blocked_partial` whenever a NIC is open or unread, never `blocked`. `nics_unread` stays a string, as in the branch `None`, so the console and the drift card read it unchanged. The console's promotion window already says both keys, each on its own line; a test now holds it. The machine's oracle reads the two keys together (`said`). It is tightened, never excused (A0): a `nics_unread` that names NICs excuses those alone, no longer every NIC off the perimeter. I3P holds: an unread NIC is never said covered, and its test now expects `nics_unread`, no longer `uncovered_nics`.
