### Added
- **The drill of the week, on Orodruin's side: its timer, its arming, its guards** (DRL1a, #613; `docs/design/drill-banc.md`). Nothing is armed by it: the arming stays the operator's.
  - `drills/banc-hebdo.yaml`, a shipped drill with an optional `schedule` (a day, an hour of UTC, its own cap), read by the drill's exact reader: shipped drills only, every wire on `{zone: bench}`, a public, implemented, non-destructive technique. What the watch should see lives apart, in `expectations/banc-hebdo.yaml`, with its own exact reader (`eregion_core/expectations.py`); the injectors' image copies `drills/`, never `expectations/`.
  - `python -m eregion_core.scheduled_drill run`, the service's one command: the arming, the drill read from the image (its whole file's fingerprint), the image's catalogue, the 30-minute window, the operator's permit (rewritten under If-Match), Orodruin's lock handed down to the runner, the registry of the occurrences played, the request, the contract, the watch, the zone `bench` (judged and judged again before each wire), the isolation read before any start, the target started (journaled first, counted in the cap) and its Heartbeat awaited, then the wire; the pulse `orodruin/drill.json` at each occurrence. SIGTERM is relayed to the wire and awaited 75 s; `annatar wire` takes it once, closes its window and cleans up.
  - A wire's exercise window now lasts the time its runner left it plus 5 min, never past the 2 h bound.
  - `make orodruin-drill-check`, `make orodruin-drill-arm DRILL= TARGETS=` (never `all`, refused without the scheduled start of DRL1c at the drill's hour less 20 min), `make orodruin-drill-disarm`; the kill disables the scheduled start right after the revocation, removes the permit, and writes the pulse `disarmed (killed)`; `orodruin-down` says the arming is lost.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_scheduled_drill.py`, `tests/unit/test_drill_expectations.py`, `tests/unit/test_drill_sigterm.py`.
