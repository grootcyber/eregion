### Added
- **The watch's breaker in dollars: two envelopes, three buckets, the criticality in the config** (PRT2, #1160; `docs/raisonnement.md` §2.5, `docs/operations.md`, « Le budget du modèle dans la watch »). The real has 5 $ a day, the exercises 5 $ a day together and 2 $ a campaign, each with its general breaker, reset at midnight UTC (`GLORFINDEL_MODEL_*`). The envelope is chosen by the event's source: an exercise only when an injector's window covers the VM and its zone is the bench's or the twin's — in zone production, or a zone unread, it counts in the real. The graves keep a reserve of 0.50 $ per critical object (at most half the real), and a grave on a critical object draws on the whole envelope; the ordinaries have 1 $ per VM. Each call is counted at LiteLLM's price, checked before it is made (`eregion_core/llm_call.py`'s guard); an unknown cost is 0.05 $, never 0; a 429 is 0. A decision is admitted only when its calls fit, priced unknown; the overrun is bounded by the share of a decision admitted. The count survives the restart (`~/.glorfindel/model_budget.json`). Exhausted, a grave goes the code's path (the snapshot under the VM's mode and the attribution's guard, its card; B3a's floor proposes the isolation; a release after a restore left to a human), an ordinary becomes a card, an exercise pauses, never the real; a card `model_budget_tripped` says it once a day for each breaker. The provider's limits (429, quota): the watch slows, then takes the code's path, never a failed cycle. What the model sees is unchanged. The hard ceiling stays at the provider's: the operator sets a monthly spending limit on the key's workspace.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_model_budget.py`, `tests/unit/test_decision_queue.py`; mutants M7413–M7432 (`scripts/mutants/PRT2.py`).
- **`critical: true` on an entry of `autonomy.assets`, read strictly and classed** (PRT2, #1160; `docs/design/configuration-console.md`, §5.2). True or false without quotes, nothing else; no Azure tag, no other source. Setting it tightens, taking it away relaxes. A push to Imladris never loses a critical object.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_config.py`, `tests/unit/test_config_edit.py`, `tests/unit/test_glorfindel_host.py`.
