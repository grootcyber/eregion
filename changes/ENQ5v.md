### Fixed
- **The backup vault's jobs are asked on the read's own window, never on the service's default range** (ENQ5v, #1221; `docs/design/enquete-raisonnee.md` §9.7, `docs/azure.md`). The bench's capture of 26/11 read no job for bree on 10/11, though the vault showed the backup of 02:33:19 inside the read's window: the listing was sent without a time range, and the code filtered the window on its side, so an episode read days later saw only the range the service gives by default, which the documentation does not state. Replayed after the vault's latency, that empty answer reads as « read whole, no job », and the ransomware sheet's backup hypothesis is infirmed where a backup ran. The enquiry's `vault_jobs` and the dossier's ten days of `backup_jobs` now pass their window to the service (`startTime` and `endTime`, in UTC, with the clauses and time format of `az backup job list --start-date`; the lower bound an hour earlier, the upper one a day later but never past the moment of the read, since what the service compares its bounds to, a job's start or its end, is not documented and stays to measure), then keep their own window exactly; a filter the service refuses is a failed read, never « no job ». The note also diagnoses the other three reads that came back empty on the four captured episodes: the per-process write counters do not exist under Linux (already said `sans_objet`; a collection is proposed, not applied), no `sudo` ran in those windows (the bench's injectors and faults act as root without it), and the heartbeat read has a blind spot on a gap that straddles its window's start, whose fix goes with ENQ5b's end-to-end run. Read-only queries for the maintainer confirm each cause. What the model sees does not change.
