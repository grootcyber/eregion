### Changed
- **The bot mute, a session cut's closure falls back on the webhook too — every one, a drained cut too, never pinging** (JUG2b, #768, decided by Jonathan on 12/11; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Sa clôture, une fois » and « alerting webhook + bot Discord »). Since JUG2 only the announcement of an action taken alone fell back; a closure (DRN2: drained, refused, sessions left open, never ended, no longer followed) still waited for the bot, and was lost after 24 h. It now takes the same path (`vm_notices.RELAY_KINDS`): the same heartbeat, the same passes, a webhook that fails gives it back to the bot, once per closure, nothing posted twice at the bot's return. No ping: only an action taken alone pings (JUG1) — `relayed.ping` false, `allowed_mentions` empty. Its line says the bot is silent and that the VM's isolation and its cards are in the console. It is never posted from the reassertion: said under its VM's lock, it is recorded for the bot, and the next pass of the fallback says it, in its own thread, within a minute — a webhook slow or unreachable holds neither that lock (an isolation of the same VM) nor the reassertion of the next VMs. It decides nothing: the cut's outcome is the one read on Azure, written in the isolation's state (DRN2). The known limit « a session cut's closure still waits for the bot » (JUG2) is gone.

### Fixed
- **The webhook fallback never holds the watch's loop** (JUG2b, #768; `docs/glorfindel.md`). `relay_pass` ran in the watch's main loop: a webhook that did not answer cost 10 to 25 s per announcement at each pass, delaying the reading of `runs/`, the watch's heartbeat, the reassertion, the TTL and the answer to « Verify ». It now runs in a thread of its own (`cli._relay_notices`, as the reassertion or the door's facts), one pass at a time (the thread not started again while one runs, and `vm_notices._PASS`). Each POST is bounded: 5 s to connect, 5 s to read, and 10 s for the whole of it, the DNS resolution included (`POST_DEADLINE_S`: the POST in a thread its caller waits for, then abandons). A pass stops at the first webhook rate limited, refusing (a 4xx: a webhook deleted refuses the next ones too), unreachable (no answer, or none within 10 s) or that nothing reached — the rest goes back to the bot, or to the next pass — and starts no post after 60 s (`RELAY_PASS_BUDGET_S`). The real bound: the watch's loop, nothing; a pass, its budget and one announcement (0.5 s of spacing, two POSTs of 10 s and a short 429's wait of 5 s), some 86 s, under the 2 min after which a mark is taken back.
  - **The tries of a notice are capped**: a post that may have been delivered — a read timeout, the 10 s deadline passed (ambiguous: a resolution that hangs as well as an answer that never comes), the connection cut once the request was sent, a 5xx — counts a try (`tries`, beside `relayed`; a mark taken back counts one too). After 3 (`RELAY_MAX_TRIES`) the webhook says it no more, said once in the watch's log — a webhook that delivered but whose answer was lost no longer pings at every pass for 24 h; the notice waits for the bot, whose card pings at its return. What delivered nothing counts none: a post from which nothing left — the name not resolved, the connection refused or not made in time (`vm_notices._nothing_sent`) — stops the pass without a try, so a network or DNS cut, however long, never silences the fallback; nor does a refusal (a 4xx, a 429).
  - **The bot's SIGTERM handler is put back** once `run()` returns (`bot._restore_sigterm`): a caller that goes on — a test's worker — no longer keeps it.
  - `post_webhook`'s docstring said a webhook that fails is never tried again: a short 429 is, once.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bot_fallback.py` (the real loop of `glorfindel watch` with a webhook that never answers: its heartbeat written and its reassertion started while the POST waits; one pass at a time; a pass stopped at a webhook unreachable, refusing, at its budget; what left nothing told from what may have been delivered; a network cut counting no try; a POST past its deadline; its connect and read bounds; the bound said in the doc; the cap on a read timeout and a 502, none on a 404 or a long 429, a mark taken back; the SIGTERM handler put back; every closure by the next pass without a ping, a closure that never waits for the webhook, the bot alive, a closure left to the bot then relayed, the bot back posting none), `tests/unit/test_bot.py` (the handler put back), `tests/unit/test_drain_closure.py` (the bot alive, the closure waits for it). Mutants M5005–M5014 in `scripts/mutants/JUG2b.py`; M4998 re-anchored in `scripts/mutants/JUG2.py`.
