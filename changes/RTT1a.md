### Changed
- **A cloud detection's card is titled by its rule, in English** (RTT1a, #1174; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le titre d'une carte de détection cloud »). A card of the control plane or of an identity was titled by its type alone — « Entra ID identity change » for a mere sign-in. Each of the eleven shipped rules of the two families now declares a short `title:` (`Entra ID unusual sign-in`, `NSG changed outside Glorfindel`, `Run Command by another caller`…), checked at load as any field of a rule (a string, at most 80 characters, one line, no control character): in the client's layer, an unreadable one skips its entry, said, and the shipped rule of its name loads instead. The card keeps it in `rule_title`, written once with the card from the rule alone — never from the signal nor the model; a rule without a title, a card written before, or rules unreadable when the card is written take the neutral title of the family (`Entra ID detection`, `Control-plane detection`). Discord (the embed and `/pending`), the webhook, `glorfindel pending`, `glorfindel tui` and `escalations.to_judge()` show it, escaped as any text; the types `identity_change` and `control_plane_change`, and every guard that reads them, are unchanged. The bench's copies of `nsg-change-outside-glorfindel` and `entra-sp-unusual-signin` declare the title again, with their new `base_sha256`: `make imladris-client-rules` after the release, or the watch sets them aside and the shipped rules run.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_rule_title.py` — the eleven titles word for word, from the rule to the card through `escalate_to_human`; an unreadable title refused; a client rule without a title, and one in a shipped rule's place; a card of before; the title never from the signal; a rule renamed never rewrites a card; the types and their guards; Discord, the webhook, the CLI, the TUI and `to_judge`, each escaped. Mutants M7536–M7545.
