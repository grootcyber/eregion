### Added
- **The watch reads the readable name of each subscription it sees, for the console** (SUBN1, #1190; `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/operations.md`, “Les noms des abonnements”). A watch acts in its own subscription only, but its workspaces discover VMs of others: the console's map, grouped by subscription (GRP1, #1189), can now say each one's name instead of its masked id.
  - The subscriptions seen are the watch's own (`AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`) and those of the discovered VMs' resource ids, once each; only an id shaped as a GUID is taken. Each name is read from Azure Resource Manager (`GET /subscriptions/{id}`, its `displayName`) with the watch's identity, over REST: no new dependency. Reader on the subscription is enough; without it, the read fails and is served as an error (ARM's status and code), the console keeping the masked id. Which code ARM gives without the right is a hypothesis to measure on the bench: a 403 `AuthorizationFailed`, a 404 `SubscriptionNotFound` for a subscription the identity does not see, or a 401 `InvalidAuthenticationTokenTenant` for one of another tenant.
  - Read at the start, every 6 h, and as soon as a new subscription appears (the next housekeeping pass), ten at most per pass; in a thread of its own, one at a time, never in dry-run.
  - The name is a text from outside: its control and format characters removed, a GUID it carries masked, cut at 64 characters. It goes to the console only: never to the model, the signal, Discord nor the watch's log, which says at most “subscription names: 3 of 4 read”. An error keeps ARM's status and code, never its message, its control and format characters removed as the name's, its GUIDs and URLs masked before it is cut. A subscription no longer seen, then seen again, is read again at the next pass.
  - `~/.glorfindel/subscriptions.json`, written at once; `/api/watch/status` carries it as `subscriptions` (`read_at`, and per masked key, the first 8 and last 4 characters in lower case as `/api/config` shows them, `name` and `error`; both null: not read yet), absent when the file cannot be read. The route stands when the module raises. The watch's fields of this route move out of `eregion_console/api.py` into `eregion_console/watch_fields.py`.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_subscription_names.py`, `tests/unit/test_watch_loop.py`; mutants M7661–M7670 and M7648 (`scripts/mutants/SUBN1.py`).
