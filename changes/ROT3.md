### Added
- **Imladris's CD now confirms in `#deploy` a pair's secret, or the console's token, renewed after a reminder left** (ROT3, #1170; `docs/operations.md`, « Faire tourner un secret » and « Faire tourner le jeton de la console d'Imladris »; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Les rappels d'échéance des paires »). Before, once Jonathan turned a pair (`make pair-rotate`), the CD saw the new end and started its tiers over without a word: the J-14 reminder stayed alone in `#deploy`, no longer true. Now, when the CD reads for a pair an end later than the one a reminder already left for, it posts once, in green and without the mention: « ✅ Le secret de la paire « console » a été renouvelé : fin le 2027-02-23 (dans 90 jours) », then « Les rappels repartent pour cette date : J-14, J-7, J-1 et le jour même. » — nothing else, neither a secret nor an id; the console's token in its own words (« ✅ Le jeton de la console d'Imladris a tourné : à refaire le … »). The CD confirms, not the rotation command: it sees `make pair-expiry-note` and the token's note too. The confirmation is noted in `reminders.json` with the end it confirms and saved at once: once, even after a reboot. Refused by Discord, it is tried again at the next pass; without `#deploy`'s webhook, the journal says so and it waits. An end nearer or the same, or a first end never reminded, confirms nothing. A confirmation still waiting follows a second rotation, against the end the reminder was for. `make imladris-cd-status` says « renouvelé le … » for the end in place. The agent of the CD changes (`eregion_cd.py`): `make imladris-cd-install` after the merge, then `make imladris-cd-enable`.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_pair_rotate.py` and `tests/unit/test_console_rotate.py`, the real CD agent on a fake Discord (mutants M7516–M7525, `scripts/mutants/ROT3.py`).
