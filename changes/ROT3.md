### Added
- **Imladris's CD now confirms in `#deploy` a pair's secret, or the console's token, renewed after a reminder left** (ROT3, #1170; `docs/operations.md`, « Faire tourner un secret » and « Faire tourner le jeton de la console d'Imladris »; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Les rappels d'échéance des paires »). Before, once Jonathan turned a pair (`make pair-rotate`), the CD saw the new end and started its tiers over without a word: the J-14 reminder stayed alone in `#deploy`, no longer true. Now, when the CD reads for a pair an end later than the one a reminder already left for, it posts once, in green and without the mention: « ✅ Le secret de la paire « console » a été renouvelé : fin le 2027-02-23 (dans 90 jours) », then « Les rappels repartent pour cette date : J-14, J-7, J-1 et le jour même. » — nothing else, neither a secret nor an id; the console's token in words that claim only what the CD sees, a new day (« ✅ Le jeton de la console d'Imladris : nouvelle date, à refaire le … »: `make imladris-console-token-note` notes one without turning the token). The CD confirms, not the rotation command: it sees `make pair-expiry-note` and the token's note too. The confirmation is noted in `reminders.json` with the end it confirms and saved at once: once, even after a reboot — unless the disk refuses the state (full): said in the journal (« confirmation partie, état non écrit (disque ?) »), the pair's due tier still sent in that pass, and the confirmation may leave again at the next pass. Refused by Discord, it is tried again at the next pass; without `#deploy`'s webhook, the journal says so and it waits. Still waiting once the new end has passed, it is dropped and noted so: never « renewed » beside « expired ». An end nearer or the same, or a first end never reminded, confirms nothing. A confirmation still waiting follows a second rotation, against the end the reminder was for. `make imladris-cd-status` says « renouvelé le … » for the end in place. The agent of the CD changes (`eregion_cd.py`): `make imladris-cd-install` after the merge, then `make imladris-cd-enable`. A rotation made before the installation is confirmed at the first pass if the agent before posted nothing since (it writes `reminders.json` only after a reminder); otherwise its tiers started over were saved, and nothing is confirmed after the fact.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_pair_rotate.py` and `tests/unit/test_console_rotate.py`, the real CD agent on a fake Discord (mutants M7516–M7525, `scripts/mutants/ROT3.py`).
