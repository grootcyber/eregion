### Changed
- **In the sky, the live feed is as wide as its lines, each stopped at the ring** (SKY7, #1282; `docs/design/console.md`, « Le fil jusqu'à l'anneau »). Jonathan's choice (27/11), from the 0.6.13 on Imladris: in a 1430 × 1129 window the feed cut a VM's name to « vm… », and what tells two VMs apart was gone. The feed was held off a circle beyond the ring the sky draws, and by a fixed width on larger screens. Now it is as wide as its widest line, short of To judge. Each line stops 8 px before the arc at its own height, and before the labels the sky draws beside it; the feed's box is clipped where it would cross the arc. It goes down only as deep as a line keeps its time, its action and its share, and scrolls past that. On a small screen — where a centred circle left less than 300 px on each side (1280 × 900, 1430 × 1129, 1600 × 1000, half a 34″) — the circle now sits at the right and the three lists at the left: To judge at the top, the feed under it, the legend at the bottom, the bands right of them (Jonathan's choice, on a prototype in the console). The feed goes down to the legend, the most lines seen, and the circle is drawn up to 10 % smaller so that 420 px stay free beside it, the list's margin included: a feed up to about 434 px stays whole; a longer line loses the end of its name. A tile's card also looks for room under and above each list (at 1280 × 900 it covered the legend). A VM's name is its Azure name everywhere in the sky, as the bubble, To judge and the card already said it: since SKY1 the sky's labels dropped its « vm- », never decided nor written (Jonathan's choice, « Le nom entier partout »). On large screens and in a tall window the lists keep their places (the feed widens there too); the table and a phone are unchanged, the drawing back to its size out of the sky.
