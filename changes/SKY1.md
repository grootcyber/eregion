### Added
- **The infrastructure map at 360°, the large screen's default view** (SKY1, #1236; `docs/design/console.md`, « La vue à 360° »). Jonathan's choice on an animated mockup (26/11). The toggle becomes « VIEW · 360° · tiles · table »; with no choice, 360° on a large screen and the table on a phone. Glorfindel is one node at the centre, its ring in three named arcs (DETECT, PROTECT, RECOVER); its backends are satellites drawn from their arc — the log sources, the NSGs themselves (`/api/nsgs`), the vaults — sized by what they serve, the smallest beyond six into one « +N ». The estate is drawn in systems that follow « group by »: a sun per resource group or per subscription, its resources as slowly turning planets (still under the pointer and under `prefers-reduced-motion`), or one star per resource without a grouping; Entra ID and the control plane are lone bodies the size of a sun; a sector per subscription, out of reach dashed. A line lights only when something happens, from the backend that acts: flowing while a human must act, solid once done, pulsing while a restore runs; out of reach, DETECT's only; a line never crosses Glorfindel. Hovering a resource, a sun, a satellite or an arc lights what it serves; a click pins, and so do Enter and Space from the keyboard. Out of reach, a resource has neither an NSG nor a vault of the watch's. The panel beside the sky shows the tile itself, unfolded, with its buttons; at rest, the three modules and their alerts. It is written again only when what it says or its guards change (read-only, out of reach); while the operator is in one of its fields, only when a guard changes. The watch's cards are red waves inside Glorfindel's ring. The SVG is kept from one refresh to the next, so its motion never restarts.
