### Added
- **The dossier of a block of an internal address: a block executed alone still has none, but when the address it cut is internal, its cycle gets the dossier of step 0** (DOS3b, #1063; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le dossier d'une isolation » ; design: `docs/design/dossier-isolation.md` §4.6, §11, §12, the operator's decisions of 23/11). A block is reversible, and its card, its reasoning and its decision line already say why; for an internal address, « what did it cut? » counts. « Internal » is said in code (`glorfindel/dossier_block.py`): the private ranges (RFC 1918, IPv6's unique local), the shared space of carrier NAT (100.64.0.0/10), the link local and the loopback; an IPv4 address written as IPv6 is that IPv4 address. The client's networks are not among them: the registry of assets keeps no VNet nor subnet. The dossier says the address, its class and its range; its verification fact says « block verified ». An address that cannot be read (absent, a range, a text) is never said internal nor public: its dossier is written « not composed », with why — never silence — and is not counted under the hourly ceiling.
  - Kept 30 days after the unblock seen: held while a block of its VM and its address is recorded (the same address whatever its writing; an unreadable one, by any block of its VM), its clock stopped if blocked again; no block's dossier moves while a block state cannot be read, the isolations' go on.
  - A limit, said: the never-block list already refuses every block of these ranges, so the case does not arise today; the scope holds it all the same.
  - The design records the decisions of 23/11: a detection with no attributed VM keeps its local dossier, and the reads of Azure (DOS4) will never be made for it; the hourly ceiling is to be asked again when DOS4 is framed. Nothing the model sees changes, frozen to the byte by a test.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_dossier_block.py`. Mutants M7091–M7094 (`scripts/mutants/DOS3b.py`).
