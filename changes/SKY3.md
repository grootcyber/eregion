### Fixed
- **The 360°'s hover and selection** (SKY3, #1246; `docs/design/console.md`, « Le survol et la sélection, justes »). Found by Jonathan on Imladris (26/11): entering the big circle greyed everything, and a selection stayed when the click was elsewhere. The sky read what the pointer was on up to the page, whose `<body>` carries a `data-m` of its own (the phone's tab): an empty spot of the sky took a focus on no module and dimmed everything; it reads within the sky only now. A system answered the pointer on 1.3 times its orbit plus 14 px; its reach is its orbit now. A hover waits 140 ms before it dims the rest, leaving the sky is at once. A click outside the sky and its panel lets go of a selection, Escape too, unless a window is open.
