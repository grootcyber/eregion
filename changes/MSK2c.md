### Security
- **The TUI masks what it shows of a card or a signal as the CLI does: no subscription's id, no raw ARM path** (MSK2c, #798; found by MSK3b's review; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La TUI masque comme la CLI »). `glorfindel dashboard` showed a card's suggested steps as written, so a NSG's id, and its subscription with it, could be read there. Each step now goes through the CLI's masking (`masking.shown`, with the card's id): an ARM id is said by its name and group, a command naming its VM by its id is said by `<group>/<name>`, and a bare id of the card's subscription becomes « <abonnement> », even when the shell names another one. The step is **cut to 72 characters after the masking, never before**, because an id cut in the middle is no longer read by the mask and its first characters would show. Every other text of a card or a signal the TUI writes is masked too: the card's line (its action, its resource; a card filed on a subscription says « <abonnement> »), the label of a confirmation (ack, release, unblock, reset, restore), the restore's screen (the moment's why, its warning, the typed moment's error, and an unreadable vault's error, whose Azure 403 cites its scope) and what the restore answered, the RESOURCES panel, and the FEED's lines (a signal's event, TTP and resource; a cycle's action and TTP). The card's own id (its first 8 characters), a block's IP, the moments, ages and confidence, a command's `<group>/<name>`, what the operator types and the names of `runs/`' files (a run's name) are written as they are, each listed with its reason in `glorfindel/tui.py`. The cards, the signals and the journals keep the full id; nothing the model sees changes.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_tui_masking.py`, `tests/unit/test_cli_masking.py` (its guard counts the TUI among those that mask). Mutants M5220–M5224.
