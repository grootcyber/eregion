### Security
- **The TUI masks what it shows of a card or a signal as the CLI does: no subscription's id, no raw ARM path** (MSK2c, #798; found by MSK3b's review; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La TUI masque comme la CLI »). `glorfindel dashboard` showed a card's suggested steps as written, so a NSG's id, and its subscription with it, could be read there. Each step now goes through the CLI's masking (`masking.shown`, with the card's id): an ARM id is said by its name and group, a command naming its VM by its id is said by `<group>/<name>`, and a bare id of the card's subscription becomes « <abonnement> », even when the shell names another one. The step is **cut to 72 characters after the masking, never before**, because an id cut in the middle is no longer read by the mask and its first characters would show. Every other text of a card or a signal the TUI writes is masked too: the card's line (its action, its resource; a card filed on a subscription says « <abonnement> »), the label of a confirmation (ack, release, unblock, reset, restore), the restore's screen (the moment's why, its warning, the typed moment's error, and an unreadable vault's error, whose Azure 403 cites its scope: kept whole, cut to 200 characters once masked) and what the restore answered (the CLI's output masked before `restore_plan.failure_text` keeps its last 600 characters: cut first, an id would lose « /subscriptions/ » and its GUID's start, and the rest would pass; `failure_text` is unchanged for the bot and `recovery_watch`), the RESOURCES panel, and the FEED's lines (a signal's event, TTP and resource, masked by the signal's id; a cycle's action and TTP). No cut of the TUI comes before the masking. The card's own id (its first 8 characters), a block's IP, the moments, ages and confidence, a command's `<group>/<name>`, what the operator types and the names of `runs/`' files (a run's name) are written as they are, each listed with its reason in `glorfindel/tui.py`. The cards, the signals and the journals keep the full id; nothing the model sees changes.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_tui_masking.py`, `tests/unit/test_cli_masking.py` (its guard counts the TUI among those that mask). Mutants M5220–M5224, M5255–M5258.
