### Fixed
- **Three intermittent tests steadied, none weakened: each reads its clock and its marker where the code under test does** (FLK3, #900). No product code changes: the three causes were in the tests.
  - `test_recovery_watch.py` (five tests red on Python 3.12 in the CI of #882, 18/11): the test's « now » was taken once, at the module's import, while the code under test dates the isolation, the cards and the activity on the real clock. Under xdist a worker imports the file at collection and runs it more than ten minutes later on the CI (12 400 tests): a point « 10 min after the isolation » fell before it, a job « 40 min after » before the isolation. `_NOW` is now taken again at the start of each test; a test says the two clocks stay seconds apart. Reproduced at once by aging `_NOW` by 10 min 15 s after the collection (the five, and two more); green 20 times in a row under `-n 4` with `_NOW` aged by 15 min.
  - `test_console.py::test_each_console_has_its_own_cookie`, and `test_the_cookie_is_a_session_derived_from_the_token`, which had the same race: the session cookie was recomputed on the clock after the request, a second that may have turned since. The cookie is now read as the console issued it: its name, its date within the request on the console's clock, its value the one the token issues for that date, to the byte. Reproduced at once by a console clock that turns the second at each read; green 20 times in a row under it. M1179, M1180 and M1302 still caught.
  - `test_drill_sigterm.py`: the wire said « ready » before entering the region that catches SIGTERM; the test signals the moment it sees the marker, and a signal taken in between was raised where nothing caught it (exit 1, not 143; the CI of #882, 18/11). The wire now writes its marker from inside that region, renamed into place. Reproduced at once by half a second between the marker and the wire's next step; green 20 times in a row under it.
  - The whole suite under `-n 4` with every module-level « now » of a test module aged by 15 min: green, no other test of that kind.
