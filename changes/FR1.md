### Fixed
- **The fault rate no longer measures a fault chained with a technique on its target, nor a wire of a Dagorlad request** (FR1, #1006; `docs/analysis.md`, « Le taux des pannes prises pour une attaque »). Such a fault is now « not measured », with its reason, and stays out of the rate. Before, the rate set a decision aside for a technique only when it bore that technique's `ttp`, on its VM, within 15 minutes of its T0. The rate could then hide a card of the fault bearing that `ttp`, or count against the fault a card of the technique from another rule. A Dagorlad request chains both on purpose (DG-b), and `make imladris-fault-rate` reads 30 days of runs.
  - A fault is not measured when its report carries a request (`metrics.request`, written by Gothmog), or when it started in a technique run's exercise window, its grace included, on one of its targets. Targets are compared without case, by full id or by VM name, and a resource group's scope holds the VMs under it.
  - The technique's window is the one this host holds and believes, never narrower than `[T0, T0 + 15 min]`. When the window cannot be found, the absence of the technique cannot be proven: the window then runs from the T0 to the longest a window may last (2 h, plus the 15 minutes of grace), and the reason says so. A technique started before the period counts too.
  - A wire of a request is not measured even when its request holds no technique.
  - A session of faults alone gives the same rate as before. Still open: a technique played during a fault, outside a request, is left to X1; a technique whose window is on this host without its report is not seen.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_fault_rate.py` (mutants M6900–M6909 and M6920–M6922, `scripts/mutants/FR1.py`).
