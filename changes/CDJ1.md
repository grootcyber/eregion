### Fixed
- **`make imladris-cd-status` shows the last 15 lines of the CD's journal of the day, not the first** (CDJ1, #783; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Le CD d'Imladris »). The recipe ran `journalctl -u eregion-cd --since -1d -n 15`: on Imladris's systemd 249 (Ubuntu 22.04), `--since` with `-n` reads forward from the window's start and stops at 15 lines. On 13/11, right after 0.5.12 was deployed and the CD's agent reinstalled, the status showed passes of the day before (« v0.5.10 tourne déjà »), never the deployment just made. The day's journal is now read whole and its last 15 lines kept (`| tail -n 15`), whatever the version; a recent systemd (255) already gave the last lines, which is why it never showed on a workstation.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_host_cd.py` (the real recipe, its ssh faked, its command then run against a fake journalctl of systemd 249 and of 255: the newest 15 lines in their order; the window of a day and the CD's unit). Mutants M5120–M5123.
