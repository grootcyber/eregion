### Fixed
- **A perimeter block whose NSG the customer took off its subnet is said gone, never bypassed, and never put back** (RSM3, #1057, #1036; `docs/design/reaffirmation.md`, E7; `docs/azure.md`). A perimeter pair on an NSG that no longer governs any network card of the VM (the subnet's NSG taken off, or another put in its place) is gone for where it lies, whatever its content: a bypass supposes the hold intact. Before, a perimeter block alone was said bypassed by an allow added on that detached NSG, or « not covered … (another subnet) » without one; and a VM blocked on its subnet and for itself (one merged entry) was read held by its own rule, the check true, the pass silent while the subnet's other VMs were reached again (found by RSM2a's invariant night n° 50).
  - The check asks first whether the pair's NSG still governs the VM's subnet, from the cards it reads anyway: no extra read for a merged entry, the cards read once for a perimeter alone. It answers `perimeter_off`: false for a perimeter alone, true for a merged entry whose VM holds by its own rules.
  - The reassertion says it on a card of the block of its own (`perimeter_off`): the NSG taken off, the rule no longer blocking the IP for the subnet's VMs, and the commands (put the NSG back, block again on the subnet's current NSG or on the VM, unblock). Once per NSG, closed when the NSG is read on the subnet again; a bypass card said before closes.
  - Nothing is written: neither on the NSG the customer took off, nor on another without the operator's decision. The VM's own rules of a merged entry, gone too, are put back alone.
  - The subnet of a card unreadable, no card governed, or the VM's cards themselves unreadable: where the pair lies is unknown, never gone nor held while it is whole there, and neither time puts it back — deferred, not lost: the VM's own rules are put back once, as ever (their next drift said, never rewritten: no fight with the customer's Terraform), the pair alone is marked deferred, its card says it comes once at the next pass the read allows, and that pass puts the pair alone back on its own NSG, once, whatever it answers. A pair read whole on its NSG is never deferred; a refusal for another NSG stays spent (a decision to make). A put-back of the reassertion also asks the NSG the subnet carries at the write and refuses any other than the one the entry records, saying so: before, with the cards unreadable at the check, a pair gone was put back on a replacement NSG.
  - The card names the three possible causes (the NSG taken off, replaced, or the VM moved to another subnet), and says the subnet's other VMs uncovered only under the first two. A fresh block whose check reads its pair off answers `blocked_partial`.
  - Known limit: an NSG shared by two subnets of the VM, taken off one of them, keeps the pair read held while a card of the VM stays behind it on the other; recording the pair's subnet is left to a later lot.
  - The invariant machine excuses nothing of this family any more: its exemption for #1057 is removed, and the two sequences frozen as strict expected failures now pass as plain tests.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_reassert_gone.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_hold.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariants.py`. Mutants M7057–M7060 and M7492–M7495 (`scripts/mutants/RSM3.py`); M7048 and M7049, which guarded the exemption, are retired with it.
- **A deleted VM has its own card, and nothing is written for it** (RSM3, #1036; Q6, the maintainer's decision of 21/11). The reassertion said a recorded isolation or block of a deleted VM « not verified » with a `ResourceNotFound`, or tried to put its rules back and said « putting it back failed ». It now says the VM no longer exists, with the command that lifts the isolation or the block, and closes the card at the lift; no write is tried. The VM is read gone only on its own « not found » (`ResourceNotFound`, or its group's; any other 404 is not, and a provider that can't tell answers none), and only where the check raised, could not conclude, or read rules gone without reading the cards: a throttled read stays « not verified ». The « not verified » card now reads « du blocage ». Known limit: a block entry of an older version (one rule, no placement) of a deleted VM is not read gone.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_reassert_gone.py`.
  - The console names its cards: `vm_gone` and `perimeter_off` have their label, and the action `review_gone_vm` its own (`eregion_console/static/glorfindel.js`).
- **The night of 25/11 frozen** (RSM3, #1036; run 45620147549). Its four sequences — an isolation refused on a NIC left bare by its subnet's NSG taken off, then the NSG of the subnet that held the pair taken off or replaced, directly, through a release cut off midway, or through a race — said the VM released with the pair left. The defect was `isolate_vm`'s, fixed by ISO1 (#1194: the partial isolation recorded); RSM2a's step (`customer_changes_subnet_nsg`) is what reached it. They pass now and fail under M7681, by I8 at the isolation and, without that check, by I3 at the release.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py` (`test_a_bare_nic_refused_then_its_subnet_nsg_taken_off_is_released`, `test_a_bare_nic_refused_then_a_cut_release_or_a_race_then_its_subnet_nsg_changed`).
