### Fixed
- **A block written whole again says anew its perimeter pair still on an NSG the customer took off the subnet, once the operator had acknowledged the card that said it** (REL068b, the 0.6.8 release gate, #1209; `docs/design/reaffirmation.md`). A VM was blocked on its subnet and for itself, and the customer took the subnet's NSG off. The reassertion said so on a card of the block (`perimeter_off`) and marked the pair said for that NSG. The operator acknowledged the card, and the VM was then blocked again. A block written whole is a new block and a new episode, so the write drops the marks of the cards said before it (L1Q). It kept this one, and every pass after read the pair as already said: still named, still gone, and nothing said again. The write now drops that mark with the others. The pair is still never put back, since the NSG taken off is the customer's (E7). Two follow-ups from the review keep the card true:
  - the card closes once the pair is read on the subnet's NSG again, whether the mark is set or not. Blocked again at home (on the NSG the subnet carries now, or the NSG put back), the entry had no mark left, and its card stayed pending for ever, saying gone a pair that holds;
  - the reassertion's own put-back of a merged entry's VM rules (`--scope vm`) puts the mark back after its writes. That write leaves the pair where it lies and ends no episode of it; without the mark, the next pass said the pair off again, two cards for one episode.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py` freezes the gate's sequence (23 steps reduced to 3) and its primitive form in both modes, red before the fix with the gate's message (« L1: the block on …/Db--01 vanished from Azure; the reassertion neither put it back nor said so »). It also freezes the review's two sequences, red before their fix: the card closed once blocked again at home (the NSG replaced, or put back), and one card only across four passes that put back the VM's rules. Mutants M7741–M7743 (`scripts/mutants/REL068b.py`).
