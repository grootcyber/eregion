### Fixed
- **The console's live feed says the day of a line that is not today's, and puts the latest on top, each line in its place by its full moment** (CON-D, #735; `docs/design/console.md`). On the bench on 11/11, a « snapshot » line of the day before was taken for one of that afternoon and sent an incident's investigation down a false trail: the feed said « 16:15:45 UTC », never the day. A line that is not of today, in UTC, now says « yesterday 16:15:45 » or its date (« 10/11 16:15:45 », its year when it is not this one); a line of today keeps its time alone; the full date and time on hover. Past midnight UTC, the page's lines say their day again. The server reads each line's moment in UTC (`_feed_when` in `eregion_console/api.py`: `at`, `day`, `time`; a time with no offset read as UTC, a timestamp that does not read goes to the bottom) and orders by it: the history, sorted until now on the text of the timestamp, and each turn of the live feed, until now file by file. The page puts each line above the first one not newer than it: a late line (a signal pulled from Orodruin) goes below the newer ones. The feed of 11/11 (16:15, 21:05, 15:54, 16:22, 18:36, oldest on top) reads as a right order over two days: the operator's own actions are shown whatever their age, and a run's file recent by its mtime can hold older lines — their date now says it.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_feed_day.py` (around midnight UTC, offsets, the order of the history and of a turn; the page read in its text). Mutants M4820–M4823 in `scripts/mutants/CON-D.py`.
