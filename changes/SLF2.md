- **Orodruin's exercise channel and a watch that restarts no longer make `data-exfiltration-blob` cards** (SLF2, #1010; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le canal d'exercices d'Orodruin et la watch qui redémarre »). Two cards of the 21/11, judged Wrong: a single PutBlob of Orodruin's run report into its exercise channel (unattributed, a snapshot at 40 %), and the watch's own heartbeat for a window after the restart of the v0.6.3's deployment.
  - **The exercise channel**: an entry of `BlobWriters` is set aside only when its account is the exercise channel's, its container is its container (`EREGION_EXERCISE_CHANNEL`), and its writer is Orodruin's identity — the object id of its managed identity's principal, `EREGION_ORODRUIN_PRINCIPAL_ID`, a new variable in the watch's environment (Imladris's `host.env`, never the repository; `host_env.py --check` refuses a value that is no GUID). Never the address. Another identity, a write without one (a key, a SAS), another container or another account stays a card. The shipped rule only (ATT1); a column that cannot be read stays a card (SLF1b).
  - **Without the variable** (the channel configured), nothing is set aside, and it is said: `exercise_channel.config` in the rule's status (`unset`, `malformed`, `no channel`), once in the watch's log, « ⚠ Orodruin id » on the rule's line in the console. The gesture: `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « L'identité d'Orodruin (SLF2) ».
  - **The watch that restarts**: the watch keeps a record of its own writes over the rule's window (`~/.glorfindel/own_channel.json`: its identity, its container, when it wrote); a watch that starts and cannot read its own identity yet lets the one before stand in — that identity's writes, in that container of that account, the channel's objects, at most as many as the record holds in the window. Once its identity is read the record is not used; another identity's writes are never set aside; a record absent, unreadable, of another channel or empty in the window stands in for nothing (the identity « unread », a card). The cause of the 21/11's card is still a hypothesis, to measure at the next deployment.
  - Counted and said: `exercise_channel` (writes, rows) and `own_channel.restart` in `rule_status.json` and `status_snapshot`, the watch's log, the rule's line and details in the console (« N Orodruin »), through `_attr`.
