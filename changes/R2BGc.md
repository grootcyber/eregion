### Added

- **Stratus's closed role, Orodruin's read-only authz and the federated credential, as Terraform on the dedicated subscription (G2/G3/G4)** (R2BGc, Partie de #608, docs/design/stratus-cible.md § 6). Now that the minimal per-technique actions are measured (M1, Étape 1 au réel), `infra/orodruin/stratus_guardrails.tf` poses, on the dedicated Stratus subscription only:
  - **G3** — a closed custom role carrying exactly the 31 measured actions (19 writes/deletes/actions of the two techniques + 9 reads the activity log does not trace + 3 joins), with **no wildcard and no `Microsoft.Authorization` action**. The actions are listed **inline** (so `scripts/identity_map.py` reads them for § 3.0), never through a local or a `concat`; the wildcard is forbidden at the source (a test and a mutant) **and** a `not_actions` belt would strip it should one ever enter (the protocol's § 6 point 2: diagnostic-settings write/delete, `Consumption/*`, `CostManagement/*`, federated-credentials write/delete), each belt action named in the `stratus` line of the matrix § 3. Adding a candidate read during the measurement cycle is one line here plus its § 3.0 line plus a plan and an apply — never an override variable. The role is defined whenever the guardrails are enabled, but assigned to Stratus (as a ServicePrincipal) only with `stratus_principal_id`.
  - **G4** — an `AUTHZ_READ_ONLY` custom role for Orodruin, exactly `roleAssignments/read` + `roleDefinitions/read` (never the built-in Reader, which would break the contract `eregion_core/orodruin.py`), so Orodruin can prove from the dedicated subscription that it holds no right there (M5: an empty listing ≠ absence of a right).
  - **G2** — an `azuread_application_federated_identity_credential` whose subject is Orodruin's managed identity: one becomes Stratus only from Orodruin's sandbox (`ARM_USE_OIDC` / `WorkloadIdentityCredential`), never Orodruin's managed identity directly, which holds no role on the dedicated subscription (M4). Posed only with `stratus_client_id`; the app's resource id is read from a data source, the issuer from the tenant (never hardcoded). The apply writes on the app (Graph) under the operator's login, never Orodruin's identity (GRAPH_READ_ONLY intact).
  - Everything is off by default (`stratus_guardrails_enabled = false`, count 0); the identity variables are empty by default.
  - The identity map (`docs/design/identites.md`) now holds these declarations: the two roles' actions in § 3.0, the five keys (role definitions, assignments, federated credential) in the § 4 register, and the `stratus` line moves from « prévue » to « dépôt, à la main » (its role and federation are now declared in the repo; `stratus` leaves the planned set in `scripts/identity_map.py`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_stratus_guardrails.py` (the closed role has no wildcard and no Authorization action, carries the 31 measured actions inline, keeps its not_actions belt (named in the matrix § 3), is scoped to the dedicated subscription and assigned as a ServicePrincipal only with a principal; Orodruin's authz is exactly the two reads, assigned to its own managed identity; the federated credential trusts only Orodruin's identity, issuer from the tenant, app id from a data source) and the Terraform-native `infra/orodruin/tests/stratus_guardrails.tftest.hcl` (a real plan: the role, authz and federation pose and stay bounded). Mutants M4448–M4460 (`scripts/mutants/R2BGc.py`); M5656 (R2BG) removed with its now-lifted "no closed role yet" invariant.
