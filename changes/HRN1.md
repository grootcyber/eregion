### Added
- **The harness measures the latency, the tokens and the cost of each call to the model** (HRN1, #1065; `docs/operations.md`, « La latence, les jetons, le coût » ; `docs/raisonnement.md` §8.1). The budget of the enquiry (ENQ1) and the breaker in dollars (PRT2) need real figures; the harness had none. `scripts/harness_meter.py` wraps `completion_with_tool` for the length of a run of `scripts/llm_smoke.py`, every argument passed through and the response returned as it came (nothing the model sees changes): each call — the decision, its confirming draws in their threads, a rule proposal — notes its latency (a monotonic clock around it), its input and output tokens (the response's `usage`) and its cost (what LiteLLM can price for the model). A cost LiteLLM cannot compute is said unknown, never 0, and makes the run's cost unknown, said with what is known; a failed call is counted apart. The report says, per model and per genre of case: the latency per call (median, p95, max), the mean tokens, the cost per decision with and without its draws, the run's cost; a « COST & LATENCY » table joins the leaderboard. `JSON_OUT` keeps the summary and every call, and now reaches the decision's harness too (`make llm-compare … JSON_OUT=<file>`).
  - The attestation does not change: `requirements/harness.json` and the reference pass keep their fields, to the byte, whether a run was measured or not (a test); Imladris's CD verifies those signed bytes and reads the draws only.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_harness_meter.py` (fabricated responses with a fabricated `usage`, no call to a model). Mutants M7100–M7102 (`scripts/mutants/HRN1.py`).
