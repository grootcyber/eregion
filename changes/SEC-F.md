### Security
- **Glorfindel says what the outside sends as it is, and no print of it stops a decision** (SEC-F, #845; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le texte du dehors n'est jamais du balisage » ; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, sections 2, 3 and 4). The action and the reasoning the model renders, the fields of a line an injector writes, the fields of the cards' store, a Discord member's name, the text of an exception: Rich read them as markup in the watch, the CLI, the bot and the TUI — a closing tag with no opening one raised, and the `except` that printed it raised again. Every such value now goes through `outside()` where it is printed (`glorfindel/terminal.py`: its markup escaped, its control characters shown, never obeyed); the console of the CLI, the watch, the graph, the bot and the TUI never raises on markup; an error is said escaped by `say_failure`, which never raises in turn.
  - The watch's decision thread of a VM no longer dies: a display that fails leaves its detection decided all the same, and whatever one detection raises is said, the next one taken. The main loop says its errors the same way.
  - A line of `runs/` whose gravity is not one the injectors write (`critical`, `high`, `medium`, `low`), or whose signal id or resource type is not a plain name, is refused at admission and said, like an unknown event or technique.
  - The Discord bot: a card that cannot be posted stops neither the other cards, nor the notices, nor the heartbeat; its loop runs under a guard that says what stopped it and starts it again (one loop per process, a reconnection included); a refused click is answered and logged whatever the member's display name.
  - The commands to copy (the bot's Command button, the TUI) quote every argument for the shell — the IP, the first detection's moment, the card's id —, and the bot's ``` block is never closed by what it holds. `glorfindel pending` shows every card's fields as they are; a card it cannot show is said unreadable, the next one shown.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_outside_markup.py` (the real watch in dry-run, the bot's loop, a refused click, `pending`, the TUI; controls at normal values); mutants M5701–M5720 (`scripts/mutants/SEC-F.py`).
