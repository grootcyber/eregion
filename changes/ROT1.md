### Added
- **A pair's secret turned in one command, `make pair-rotate PAIR=<watch|console|bot>`, and reminded by Imladris in `#deploy` before it ends** (ROT1, #737; `docs/operations.md`, « Faire tourner un secret »; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « Les rappels d'échéance des paires »). Before, a rotation was an `az ad sp credential reset` whose `password` Jonathan pasted into `.envrc` by hand: nothing said which pair serves where nor what to restart, nothing checked the new secret, and nothing reminded him — the console's secret, on its service principal (PAIR1), ends around 08/12. The command, refused in a code session and without a terminal, reads both objects of the pair as PAIR1 does and turns the secret of the one that holds it; says what it will do (the pair, its appId masked, the object, the duration — 90 days by default, Jonathan's decision of 12/11 —, the lines of `.envrc`, the service, the other readers) and asks « [o/N] »; adds a new secret beside the old one (`--append`: nothing is cut); verifies it (a token of the pair, then a read of Azure Resource Manager, retried while Entra propagates it) before anything else; writes it into `.envrc` (a 0600 copy first, the file rewritten beside it and renamed over it, its mode kept, every copy of the old value replaced); recreates the compose service that reads the pair, the new value in its environment; and only then removes the old secret by its key id. It refuses before anything when the loaded environment is not that `.envrc` (a service would be recreated without its id). A failure leaves everything in place, an exception or a Ctrl-C included: before `.envrc`, only the new secret moved, and it is removed; after, `.envrc` is put back from its copy, the service recreated on the old secret, the new one removed; the copy is kept, ignored by git, only when `.envrc` cannot be put back, and that is said. The secret never appears on the screen, in a log or in a process's command line. `make pair-expiry-note PAIR=<pair>` notes the end in place on Imladris without turning anything — once for the console's current secret, which the command did not set.
- **The reminders**: the end date, a date and never a secret, is written on Imladris by the workstation's ssh (`~/.eregion-cd/expiry/<pair>.json`); the CD's timer reads it at each pass and posts in `#deploy`, with Jonathan's mention, at 14, 7 and 1 days before and on the day — the pair, the date, the exact command to type. Once per tier, even after a reboot; a date noted late sends the tier it is in; a new date starts over; a post Discord refuses is tried again at the next pass; without the webhook a tier is never counted sent; the journal gets a line only when something happens (a post, a refusal, a date that does not read, no webhook while a tier is due). `#deploy`'s webhook is already the workshop's only secret on Imladris (Jonathan's decision, 12/11): no new secret, no new timer, no new identity, nothing on Microsoft Graph (`scripts/identity_map.py check`). A rotation made outside the command is not seen: `make azure-pairs-expiry` and the bench's preflight remain the net. The CD's agent changes: `make glorfindel-host-cd-install` from an up-to-date `main`, right after the merge (the agent does not depend on the release).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_pair_rotate.py`, on a fake `az`, a fake `docker`, a fake `ssh` to a fake Imladris running the real agent, a fake `.envrc` and the fake Discord.
  - Known limits: a Ctrl-C at the « [o/N] » question or during the first reads ends in a Python traceback — nothing has been done then, and no secret is in it. On Imladris, if `reminders.json` cannot be written right after a reminder was posted (a full disk), the tier is not recorded and the reminder is posted again, with the mention, at each pass until the disk is freed.
