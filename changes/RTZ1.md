### Changed
- **A restore's moment without a time zone is refused, never taken for UTC** (RTZ1, #923; `docs/azure.md`, « Une heure sans fuseau est refusée sur tout le chemin de restauration »). The moment chooses the recovery point: a zone guessed wrong takes a point from after the attack began, or hours too early. `glorfindel restore --before 2026-11-19T14:05:00`, accepted yesterday as UTC, is now refused before anything is done, and so is such a moment typed in the TUI, the console or the bot's form; the refusal asks for an ISO 8601 time with its zone and gives an example (`2026-11-19T14:05:00Z`). A time with its zone (`Z`, `+00:00`, `+01:00`) reads as before, converted to UTC. The provider refuses such a moment too, before it reads the vault. The help of `glorfindel restore` no longer says « UTC when no offset ».

### Fixed
- **A card or an isolation dated without a time zone no longer gives a guessed restore moment** (RTZ1, #923). Its first detection was read as UTC, and the VM's isolation without an offset as the host's local time. Now no moment is proposed: the console, the bot and the TUI leave the field empty and say what the card or the state carries; `glorfindel restore` without `--before` refuses and says it; `glorfindel pending`, the TUI and the bot copy `--before <ISO8601>`, never the guessed time. Such a card is the one a restore answers (its time may be the earliest), never passed over for a later card's. The recovery watch's « point after » card writes `--before <ISO8601>` rather than `--before ?` when the incident's start is unknown. A normal card proposes its command with the same time as before.
