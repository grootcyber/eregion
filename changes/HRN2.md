### Added
- **The model's probe: once a week, at night, on Imladris, the harness on the model and the key in service, judged against the signed attestation by the CD's own rule** (HRN2, #1079; `infra/glorfindel-host/README.md`, « La sonde du modèle » ; `docs/operations.md` ; `docs/raisonnement.md` §8.5). A provider can move a model under its name: on 23/11, to a message identical to the byte, the model in service drifted from the attestation of 06/11 — on the modest disk write, a snapshot at 0.50 and two isolations at 0.65 and 0.55 held under the gate became 8 isolations out of 8, 5 of them at 0.7 or more, the draws agreeing. The CD compares two versions of the code under the same model, the same day: it could not see it. `infra/glorfindel-host/cd/harness_probe.py`, installed with the CD in `~/.eregion-cd/`, reads what runs (the watch's release and commit, its image, the model's variables of its environment only), the release's `requirements/harness.json` believed only signed by the maintainer's key, and its pass on the lock in service; it runs the release's harness (every case, 3 runs) in a throwaway container of the watch's image — its scripts read-only, an empty out dir its only writable mount, nothing of the watch's state nor of its configuration, the key passed by its name — and judges each case by `eregion_cd.overconfident_drift`, the overconfidence rule `attested` applies to a lock, moved out of it without changing what it refuses: one rule, two readers. A drift (a case gaining 2 overconfident draws or more, or two cases newly overconfident) is said in #deploy through the CD's webhook, with the maintainer's mention and the cases that moved; without one, a short line. Beside it, the judgment, the injection, the memory, the calibration, the latency and the cost. The report goes to `~/.glorfindel/harness-probe/<date>.json`, never one overwritten, the last 26 kept. The probe never changes a mode, a rule or a configuration, never writes Glorfindel's memory, takes no lock: the CD and the watch go on as before.
  - The units `harness-probe.service` and `.timer` (Sunday 02:30 UTC) are installed disabled beside the CD's by `make imladris-cd-install`; `make imladris-probe-check` (free: no call to the model), then `make imladris-probe-enable`; `-probe-disable`, `-probe-run`. About 1.15 $ a run, 5 $ a month.
  - What is not compared is said: a case new since the attestation, or gone (a release that renames a case leaves its old label there, the attestation being made again only with the lock), counted in #deploy and named in the report; no case in common, « no measure » with the mention, never « no drift ». One probe at a time (a non-blocking lock in its work dir: the second says so and erases nothing). The container is bounded beside the watch (`--cpus 1 --memory 1g --pids-limit 256`, no new privileges, every capability dropped), measured offline with the CI's image: its non-root user writes its out dir, a peak of 335 MiB.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_harness_probe.py` (the agent in a throwaway HOME, a fake `docker`, the release's files from a dict: no model, no Docker, no git, no Discord). Mutants M7115–M7119, M7121–M7122 (`scripts/mutants/HRN2.py`).
- **A run of the harness has a ceiling** (HRN2, #1079; `docs/operations.md`, « Le plafond d'un run »). `scripts/llm_smoke.py --max-usd <dollars> [--max-calls N]`: `harness_meter.Budget` checks it before each call, a call refused never made; over the measured cost, or over the bound of calls (by default the most a run makes: cases × runs × draws, 162), the run stops and says why. An unknown cost counts as a possible overrun, never 0: each is charged `max_usd / max_calls`, so a run of unknown cost alone stops at its bound of calls. A run stopped writes `--json` with its budget, never a pass nor an attestation, and exits 3.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_harness_meter.py`.
