### Security
- **A proposed detection rule is judged as soon as the model proposes it, and the routing key is asked of the model** (SEC-A4b, #660; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « L'attribution d'une ligne » ; `docs/operations.md`, « Le harnais des propositions de règles » ; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, section 4). The reader that refuses a data-plane rule at load and at approval — rows that carry neither `_ResourceId` nor `CallerIpAddress` as Azure wrote them — now judges each proposal when it is recorded (`proposed_rules.approval_refusal`): one that does not carry the key is recorded `unapprovable`, with its reason, and never enters the queue of proposals to approve (`pending`: `glorfindel pending`, the console, the bot), nor does a proposal an older version left pending without it. The card of a missed detection says the rule is not approvable and why, with no proposal to approve; `glorfindel propose-rules` says it and offers no `approve-rule`; `approve-rule` on its id says why. The approval keeps its own refusal.
  - The rule-proposal prompt (`glorfindel/detection_authoring.py`, `RULE_PROPOSAL_SYSTEM_PROMPT`) gains one grounding bullet (`ROUTING_KEY_INSTRUCTION`): keep `_ResourceId` in the query's output exactly as the table holds it — in the last `project` and in the `by` of every `summarize`, never written, renamed or recomputed, even when the schema served does not list it — and `CallerIpAddress` too on a storage table. Frozen to the byte by a test; the prompt without it is the previous one, to the byte. The decision's prompt, its few-shot examples and its user message are unchanged.
  - The harness measures it: `make llm-compare MODELS=… RUNS=3 PROPOSALS=1` (`scripts/llm_smoke.py --proposals`) draws the real `author_rule` on every implemented data-plane technique of the catalog and on one missed detection, under the prompt without then with the bullet, and says the share of proposals that carry the key for each — schemas served by the script, nothing of the cloud, never an attestation.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_proposed_rules.py`, `tests/unit/test_detection_authoring.py`, `tests/unit/test_llm_smoke.py` (a simulated model), `tests/unit/test_rule_routing.py`; mutants M5901–M5908 (`scripts/mutants/SEC-A4b.py`).
