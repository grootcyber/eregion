### Added
- **A VM's priority shown in the console, read-only: the mark beside the mode, the section « Priorities » of the page Config** (CRT1, #1215; `docs/design/classification.md` §7, `docs/design/console.md`). On a tile, an `essential` VM wears `ESSENTIAL` next to its mode (`ESSENTIAL · ⚡ NON-DISRUPTIVE`), in capitals with a thin cyan line, never filled and never in an alarm colour. A `quiet` VM wears `quiet` in grey, and a `standard` VM nothing. The tooltip, and a line on the unfolded tile, say the effect of today and nothing more: an essential VM's grave detections keep the model budget's reserve; a quiet one has the same rules and the same budget. The level is the watch's own reading (`level_of`, the exact entry then the longest pattern), sent in `/api/state` (`priority_levels`). The page Config opens with the note of the former format `autonomy:` when the file still uses it (`glorfindel config migrate`). Its section « Priorities » lists the entries by level, with their reason and what each designates today, and the graves' reserve the essential entries keep. It also names each essential VM whose mode lets Glorfindel isolate it on its own (« vm-db-prod may be isolated on its own (non_disruptive): its service stops while it is isolated »), or says « none ». The priority is set in the config file until the signed queue (CFG5): no button, no write. The view « Relaxed » labels an entry that gives a priority only as « priority level », with the watch's own text.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_priorities.py`, mutants M7771–M7780 (`scripts/mutants/CRT1.py`).
- **The watch says the priorities at its start** (CRT1). A line follows the autonomy: « Priorities: 2 essential (vm-db-prod, vm-erp-*) — their grave detections keep a 2.00 $ reserve; 1 quiet (vm-dev-*); every other VM standard ». It is not written when no entry carries a priority, nor for a config that cannot be read.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_priorities.py`.

### Changed
- **The graves' reserve has one reckoning, `model_budget.reserve_of`** (CRT1). The ledger, the watch's start and the console all read it, so a figure shown is never copied: it follows the real's envelope (`GLORFINDEL_MODEL_REAL_USD_PER_DAY`) and its 2.50 $ cap. The figures are those of PRT2, unchanged.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_priorities.py`, `tests/unit/test_model_budget.py`; PRT2's M7413 and M7473 and IPS2b's M6524 re-anchored.
