### Changed
- **The invariant machine reads what Glorfindel holds once, in the test's world, NIC by NIC** (RSM2a, #1033; `docs/design/reaffirmation.md`, §1.1 and §5). No change to the product. An isolation or a block of a VM is read from the in-memory Azure's own rules, never from the code's verification: intact, gone (absent, turned into an allow, narrowed to a port or a protocol, aimed at an address its NIC no longer has, on an NSG that no longer governs the NIC), bypassed, or unknown (an NSG that cannot be read). The reassertion's oracles rest on that one reading, as the maintainer decided on 21/11 (the note's table is the reference).
  - A block has vanished by the content of its rules, no longer by their names: a block whose denies were all turned into allows, or left on a former address, now has to be put back or said.
  - A deny of ours turned into an allow is gone, as the code reads it. This reverses the rule L1Nc (#843) wrote into the acknowledgement oracle (« turned, not vanished: a bypass »): an acknowledged drift card is said again once such a deny, in force at the acknowledgement, is turned. L1Nc was measured again first: the night of 15/11 replayed step by step on the code of that night, on L1Nc's merge and on `main`. The night's deny already aimed at a former address, gone before the card was acknowledged, so the product was rightly silent; its frozen sequence, built by hand, is the one that changes. Neither showed the bypass L1Nc's account described.
  - An isolation gone from a NIC is now required put back or said after every unfaulted pass, not only in two composite rules. Its acknowledged card holds for its episode.
  - The bypass oracle moves first only the hold's rules read intact: a narrowed or turned deny is the disappearance's, never the bypass's.
  - The machine's world now takes a subnet's NSG off it, puts another of the customer's in its place, or puts it back. A perimeter pair left on an NSG that governs nothing is gone. The release without a state cannot read our rules left on such an NSG, so that case is exempted, as for a NIC's own NSG nothing ties to it any more.
  - Found by the machine at 2 000 examples, not fixed here (#1057): a perimeter pair left on an NSG taken off its subnet, with an allow added before it there, is said bypassed by that allow, never gone nor uncovered, while the NIC carries no NSG at all. Frozen as a strict expected failure, its preconditions checked outside it; the machine excuses that case alone until its fix (the perimeter pair only, and a bypass card naming that NSG).
  - The night of 15/11 is frozen as Hypothesis printed it (29 steps): L1 holds and the product says nothing more.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/invariant_hold.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_hold.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariants.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py`. Mutants M7043–M7049 and M7078 (`scripts/mutants/RSM2a.py`); L1Nc's six and L1V's M5492 re-anchored on the new oracle.
