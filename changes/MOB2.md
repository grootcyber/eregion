### Security
- **The workstation's tunnel to the console of Imladris, armed on demand for 12 hours at most, never at boot, never by a code session** (MOB2 ; `docs/demarrer/mobile.md`, `docs/operations.md`, the threat model's frontier 9). Decision of Jonathan (11/11): drive from the iPhone or the iPad without going back to the desk, and never leave the console reachable on the workstation without a gesture. A user unit of systemd, `infra/poste/eregion-console-tunnel.service`, holds what `make glorfindel-host-tunnel` held by hand: `ssh -N -L 127.0.0.1:7007:127.0.0.1:7007` to Imladris, the port bound to 127.0.0.1 alone; the phone still opens its own SSH tunnel to that 127.0.0.1.
  - `make mobile-tunnel-install [DRY_RUN=1]` writes the unit into `~/.config/systemd/user/` and neither enables nor starts it: no `[Install]` section, nothing for `systemctl enable` to do. The address of Imladris is Terraform's at the install; the host key is pinned: `infra/glorfindel-host/known_host.sh` checks it by `HOST_SSH_FINGERPRINT` (required: never accepted at first contact), the one key copied into the tunnel's own known_hosts, the fingerprint named by the unit (`X-Eregion-HostKey`), `StrictHostKeyChecking=yes`, `GlobalKnownHostsFile=/dev/null`, `UpdateHostKeys=no`; `-F none`, `BatchMode=yes`, `ExitOnForwardFailure=yes`, `ServerAliveInterval=30` × `ServerAliveCountMax=3`, `Restart=on-failure` after 15 s. The unit is checked as `mobile-check` reads it before it is written.
  - `make mobile-tunnel-on` arms it, from the workstation or from the phone in `tmux`; `make mobile-tunnel-off` stops it; `make mobile-tunnel-remove [DRY_RUN=1]` stops it and removes the unit, its known_hosts and its deadline. The 12 hours are held by three bounds, each enough: the deadline (now + 12 h) written by the gesture and a transient timer of systemd that stops the unit then (`systemd-run --user --on-active=43200s`, `eregion-console-tunnel-expiry.timer`; without it nothing is armed); the unit's `ExecCondition`, which refuses any start or restart past the deadline or without one; `RuntimeMaxSec=12h` on each run. `make mobile-tunnel-on` refuses a unit `mobile-check` would refuse, or whose address is no longer Terraform's (Imladris redeployed: `make mobile-tunnel-install` writes the new one).
  - `make mobile-check` reads the tunnel too: the unit the model's (no `[Install]`, no link in a `*.wants/`, `RuntimeMaxSec` at most 12 h, the deadline read, one forward on 127.0.0.1 alone, nothing more than the model says, the host key pinned, the address of Imladris Terraform's), then `systemctl --user show` and `ss -Hltnp`: stopped, « ℹ not armed », never a ✗; failing, restarting, enabled, armed past its deadline, for more than 12 h or without its timer, a ✗; armed, since when, until when, and its process listening on 127.0.0.1:7007 alone.
  - `make console-url` gives the console of Imladris's URL with its token when the tunnel is set (the way to it from the phone's terminal), and says when it is not armed. It refuses in a code session, the guard alone first, and so do `make glorfindel-host-url` and `make glorfindel-host-tunnel`: the token never reaches a code session. Every `mobile-tunnel-*` target refuses in a code session too (`bench_shared.session_refusal`, as MOB1's), before make expands the address of Imladris.
  - Said in the threat model: while the tunnel is armed, any local process of the workstation — a code session included — and, WSL sharing its loopback with Windows, a process of Windows reach the console; what guards it then is the console's own gate (`_TokenAuthMiddleware`: the `Host` checked, a change from another origin refused, the token required, a `SameSite=Strict` cookie, a change without its origin refused, JSON required). Without the token, a refusal.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_mobile.py` (on fakes of `systemctl`, `systemd-run`, `ss`, `terraform`, `ssh-keyscan`; the unit's `ExecCondition` run by `/bin/sh` on deadlines past, future and absent), `tests/unit/test_host_console.py` and `tests/unit/test_console.py` (the targets that print the token run from a copy of the operator's checkout); mutants M4776–M4780 (`scripts/mutants/MOB2.py`).
