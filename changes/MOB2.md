### Security
- **The workstation's tunnel to the console of Imladris, armed on demand for 12 hours at most, never at boot, never by a code session** (MOB2 ; `docs/demarrer/mobile.md`, `docs/operations.md`, the threat model's frontier 9). Decision of Jonathan (11/11): drive from the iPhone or the iPad without going back to the desk, and never leave the console reachable on the workstation without a gesture. A user unit of systemd, `infra/poste/eregion-console-tunnel.service`, holds what `make glorfindel-host-tunnel` held by hand: `ssh -N -L 127.0.0.1:7007:127.0.0.1:7007` to Imladris, the port bound to 127.0.0.1 alone; the phone still opens its own SSH tunnel to that 127.0.0.1.
  - `make mobile-tunnel-install [DRY_RUN=1]` writes the unit into `~/.config/systemd/user/` and neither enables nor starts it: no `[Install]` section, nothing for `systemctl enable` to do. The address of Imladris is Terraform's at the install; the host key is pinned: `infra/glorfindel-host/known_host.sh` checks it by `HOST_SSH_FINGERPRINT` (required: never accepted at first contact), the one key copied into the tunnel's own known_hosts, the fingerprint named by the unit (`X-Eregion-HostKey`), `StrictHostKeyChecking=yes`, `GlobalKnownHostsFile=/dev/null`, `UpdateHostKeys=no`; `-F none`, `BatchMode=yes`, `ExitOnForwardFailure=yes`, `ServerAliveInterval=30` × `ServerAliveCountMax=3`, `Restart=on-failure` after 15 s. The unit is checked as `mobile-check` reads it before it is written.
  - `make mobile-tunnel-on` arms it, from the workstation or from the phone in `tmux`; `make mobile-tunnel-off` stops it; `make mobile-tunnel-remove [DRY_RUN=1]` stops it and removes the unit, its known_hosts and its deadline. The gesture writes the deadline (now + 12 h), and the unit alone holds to it: its `ExecCondition` refuses any start or restart without a deadline, past it, or with one more than 12 h ahead (within the window, a `systemctl --user start` by hand does start it, until the deadline); its `ExecStart` runs ssh under `/usr/bin/timeout` for the time left (12 h at most), so a run ends at the deadline (124) and the restart meets the condition; `RuntimeMaxSec=12h` ends any single run. A transient timer of systemd stops the unit at the deadline too (`systemd-run --user --on-active=43200s`, `eregion-console-tunnel-expiry.timer`; without it nothing is armed): a fourth bound the three others do not need. What they do not hold: a process of the same Unix user can write another deadline and start the unit (the guard stops a mistake, not a will); under WSL, a sleep of the host pauses the monotonic clocks, so those 12 h count the time the workstation is awake. `make mobile-tunnel-on` refuses a unit `mobile-check` would refuse, or whose address is no longer Terraform's (Imladris redeployed: `make mobile-tunnel-install` writes the new one).
  - `make mobile-check` reads the tunnel too: the unit the model's (no `[Install]`, no link in a `*.wants/`, no key or section said twice, `RuntimeMaxSec` at most 12 h, the deadline read, ssh under the model's timeout, one forward on 127.0.0.1 alone, nothing more than the model says, the host key pinned, the address of Imladris Terraform's), then `systemctl --user show` — what systemd loaded: its file alone (`FragmentPath`), no drop-in (`DropInPaths`), `RuntimeMaxUSec` at most 12 h, not changed since it was read (`NeedDaemonReload`), nothing that pulls it in (`WantedBy`, `RequiredBy`, `BoundBy`, `UpheldBy`, `TriggeredBy`, `OnFailureOf`, `OnSuccessOf`), else a ✗, armed or not — and `ss -Hltnp`: stopped, « ℹ not armed », never a ✗; failing, restarting, enabled, armed past its deadline, for more than 12 h or without its timer, a ✗; armed, since when, until when, and the unit's processes — its cgroup's, else MainPID and its children, since MainPID is timeout's and its child ssh listens — listening on 127.0.0.1:7007 alone (the port held outside the unit: a ✗; unreadable: a ⚠).
  - `make console-url` gives the console of Imladris's URL with its token while the tunnel runs (the way to it from the phone's terminal); otherwise the local console's, as before (`glorfindel-start` and its neighbours print it), with a line that names `make glorfindel-host-url` or `make mobile-tunnel-on` when the tunnel is set but not armed. It refuses in a code session, the guard alone first, and so do `make glorfindel-host-url` and `make glorfindel-host-tunnel`: the token never reaches a code session. Every `mobile-tunnel-*` target refuses in a code session too (`bench_shared.session_refusal`, as MOB1's), before make expands the address of Imladris.
  - Said in the threat model: while the tunnel is armed, any local process of the workstation — a code session included — and, WSL sharing its loopback with Windows, a process of Windows reach the console; what guards it then is the console's own gate (`_TokenAuthMiddleware`: the `Host` checked, a change from another origin refused, the token required, a `SameSite=Strict` cookie, a change without its origin refused, JSON required). Without the token, a refusal.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_mobile.py` (on fakes of `systemctl`, `systemd-run`, `ss`, `terraform`, `ssh-keyscan`; the unit's `ExecCondition` run by `/bin/sh` on deadlines past, future and absent), `tests/unit/test_host_console.py` and `tests/unit/test_console.py` (the targets that print the token run from a copy of the operator's checkout); mutants M4776–M4780, M4784–M4786 and M4787 (`scripts/mutants/MOB2.py`).
