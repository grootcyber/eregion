### Changed
- **The 360°'s card floats, and its centre follows the estate** (SKY4, #1249; `docs/design/console.md`, « La carte flottante et un centre qui suit le parc »). Jonathan's choice (26/11, on the mockup). At rest the card is closed and the 360° takes the whole width; each satellite says under its name what its module's block says of it (« 6 watched · 3 to judge », « 5 governed · 2 acted on », « 8 protected »), and detection inert is said above the sky. A click opens the card beside what was clicked — the tile itself, a system, a satellite, a module and its whole block —, keeping off Glorfindel's disc, then off what was clicked, then the nearest (twelve places scored), held within the window; where none keeps off the disc (a narrow frame, a small estate's larger centre), and on a phone, under the circle. Measured on 108 targets with the centre at its scale: never on the disc nor on the target. Closed from within, the keyboard's focus comes back to what was pinned. Escape, its ✕ or a click elsewhere closes it. Glorfindel's ring and its satellites follow the estate: 140 % for 5 resources or fewer, 80 % from 35, the size it had at 25. A mode's capabilities and a field of the card keep their own click and Escape.
