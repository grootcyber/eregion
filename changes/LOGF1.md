### Added
- **The watch says whether the workspace's logs still arrive** (LOGF1, #1183; `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/operations.md`, “La fraîcheur des journaux”). A diagnostic setting cut on the tenant or on the subscription, and the rules of Entra ID and of the control plane kept polling, without an error, on empty tables: Glorfindel was blind and did not say so.
  - The tables followed are those the enabled rules bound to Log Analytics read, once per workspace: a rule's table is the first table of its query (never a name a `let` defines), else what its detector knows. A query that reads several tables is followed by its first one only.
  - For each, a query fixed by the code reads when its last log arrived (`max(ingestion_time())`), in a window of twice the threshold, at least 24 h: every 15 min, on the watch's Log Analytics budget (DOS4), never more than half of its minute, one read at least (a budget of 1 still lets one through an empty minute). Never in dry-run. A `;` in a text literal of a query no longer cuts its statements.
  - `~/.glorfindel/log_freshness.json`, written at once; `/api/watch/status` carries it as `log_freshness` (`read_at`, `silent_after_hours`, and per table `last` and `error`), absent when the file cannot be read. A failed read (a 429, a 403, the network) is an `error`, never “no log”: its text in English, its GUIDs and URLs masked before it is cut (never a workspace's, a tenant's or an application's id). The console's route stands when the module raises.
  - Only the tables of the identity plane (Entra ID) and of the control plane open a card (the maintainer's decision of 25/11): Perf, Syslog, StorageBlobLogs stay read and in the field, never on a card. Past `GLORFINDEL_LOG_SILENT_HOURS` (24 h by default, read strictly; a week of measure on the bench before it is tightened), such a silent table makes a card `logs_silent` without a resource, one an episode and per table, closed when the logs come back or when no rule reads the table anymore; not a decision. A failed read makes no card: it is said once in the watch's log. A card the store cannot write is said once and tried again; the pass goes on and the file is written.
  - Nothing reaches the model. The console's tile is IDT1's (#1182).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_log_freshness.py`, `tests/unit/test_watch_loop.py`; mutants M7611–M7620 and M7641–M7647 (`scripts/mutants/LOGF1.py`).
