### Changed
- **The coverage matrix says `fresh` for a proof under 21 days old; `proven` is the catalogue's status only** (FRS1, #904; `docs/design/console.md`, `docs/dagorlad.md`, `docs/glorfindel.md`). One word, one meaning: `proven` is the status of a catalogue entry measured on the bench (STM1g), and nothing else. The matrix's state for a row whose last proof is under 21 days old is now `fresh` — its value in `GET /api/coverage` (`state`, and the summary's key, `fresh` in place of `proven`), on the board's red cards (`GET /api/dagorlad/board`), in `eregion coverage` and `--json`, and its labels: the matrix's row and tooltip, the proof chip of a red card, the counter above the matrix. That counter now says « N fresh », then « M aging » apart; it used to add the aging proofs (21 to 30 days) under « proven ». The overview and `eregion coverage` say « no proof » where they said « never proven ». The value was renamed with its label because only the console reads the matrix — no other component, checked over every tracked file. `aging`, `stale`, `regressed`, `unproven`, `never` are unchanged.
