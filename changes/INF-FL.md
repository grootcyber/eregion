### Security
- **The bench's network flow logs go to a storage account of their own, which no identity reachable from a watched VM can write** (INF-FL, #654 ; `docs/operations.md`, « Le compte des flux réseau (INF-FL) » ; `docs/design/corroboration.md`, CR15). The blobs of the VNet flow logs hold one record per connection, the proof SEC-A3f will read that an address really connected to a VM's port 22. They went to the exfiltration exercises' account, where each enrolled VM's managed identity holds `Storage Blob Data Contributor` on the whole account: a local user on that VM, through IMDS, could forge flows or erase the real ones. Celebrimbor now poses `azurerm_storage_account.flows` (`stcelebrimborflows`, or `stcbrflow<hash>` for a named instance), in the same region, Standard LRS, encrypted as before. No role assignment is given to a VM's identity on it or above it. Its shared key is refused (`shared_access_key_enabled = false`, through `flow_logs_shared_key_enabled`, false by default: the way back, in `terraform.tfvars`, if the bench shows that Network Watcher does not write without it), and public containers are refused. Its one assignment is `Storage Blob Data Reader` for the operator who applies, for the bench's measurements. The flow log's `storage_account_id` targets it, an in-place update; traffic analytics (10 minutes, `NTANetAnalytics`) and the 7-day retention are unchanged. The exercises' account keeps what the exercises need: the enrolled VMs' write, its diagnostic setting and `exfil-target`. The azurerm provider no longer reads any account's data plane with its key (`features.storage.data_plane_available = false`): otherwise an account without a key fails every plan. No account of the module has `queue_properties` or `static_website`. Outputs `flow_logs_storage_account` and `flow_logs_storage_account_id`; `make celebrimbor-flows-plan` and `celebrimbor-flows-up [YES=1]` plan and apply the account, its read and the flow log alone. Written, not yet applied: the plan and the apply are Jonathan's. The history of flows stays in the old account, not a proof. Writing without the key (Network Watcher, traffic analytics) is a hypothesis to be measured on the bench.
