### Changed
- **A detection rule now carries the ceiling of what it may make Glorfindel do: `ransomware-disk-write`, on the write rate alone, leads at most to a snapshot and a card; the isolation is a human's gesture from the card** (RDW1, #994; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « Le plafond par la règle »; README, « Validated TTPs »). On the bench on 20/11 (G1, v0.6.3), Gothmog's benign fault `vm-disk-write`, a backup's sustained writes of about 74 MB/s for 10 min, fired `ransomware-disk-write`, and the model proposed `isolate_vm` at 80 %, above the threshold; only `human_only` held it. The write rate does not tell a backup from an encryption (benign up to 23.2 MB/s, attacks from 26.8 to 91.7 MB/s in 60-s averages), and a higher threshold would miss attacks: safety is now carried by the rule, never by the model's confidence. A rule may declare `ceiling: {allow: [...], lifted_by: [...], why: "..."}` (`glorfindel/rule_ceiling.py`): the autonomous actions its detection may lead to (`snapshot` always among them), the facts that lift it, and what the card says. A guard of `decide` (`_apply_rule_ceiling`), after the gates that judge the model's own choice (the confidence, the signal, the telemetry, the release) and before `never_block`, the attribution and the autonomy mode, brings an autonomous action the rule does not allow back to the snapshot; it never releases another gate (a decision already held stays held, its type and reason kept), the gates after it judge the snapshot (one click in `human_only`, alone in `non_disruptive` and `full_auto`), and every card keeps the model's proposal and its confidence (`ceiling` on the card and in the debug line). Beside it, the ceiling's own card (`ceiling_proposal`, on the model of B3a's floor; Jonathan, 20/11) proposes the model's isolation, approvable in one click in the console and the signed queue under the approval's usual guards, never run alone, whatever the mode; never on a detection that names no VM, never beside the floor's card. The memory keeps the model's proposal: the cycle stored for the retrieval says `isolate_vm`, the action brought back stays in the outcome, the ceiling's note and the debug line. The ceiling is read from the rules on disk, by the rule's name, never put in the signal: nothing the model reads changes, no prompt zone is touched. An `attack_started` resolved by the graph takes the ceiling of the rule it polled. The client's layer may lower a ceiling, never raise it above the shipped one (a client copy keeps the shipped ceiling when it says none; a raise is said at load; an unreadable client ceiling skips its entry, the shipped rule loads); an unreadable ceiling or rule file, or a shipped base gone or without rules, gives the lowest ceiling (fail closed). `lifted_by` is the place of the corroboration: empty for `ransomware-disk-write` until a fact that sets an encryption apart from a backup is measured on the bench, in a later lot. The shipped query does not change; the fingerprint of the whole rule (`base_sha256`) does, so a client copy taken before is set aside and the shipped rule runs in its place. No other shipped rule has a ceiling. The console says both proposals on the card (« rule ceiling — model proposed: Isolate VM (80%) · brought back to: Create forensic snapshot »), escaped, and the ceiling's card is approvable in one click; on the snapshot's card, `glorfindel isolate <group>/<name> --yes` is a comment only (the block runs as a whole), never on an unattributed card; Dagorlad expects « snapshot alone · card » for a wire whose rules are all capped. The price, said: in `non_disruptive`, a real T1486 is no longer isolated alone until the corroboration comes.
