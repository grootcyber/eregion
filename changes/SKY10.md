### Changed
- **A VM's unfolded tile, read from the top** (SKY10, #1290; `docs/design/console.md`, « La tuile dépliée, lue du haut »). Jonathan's choice (27/11), on a mockup, from a card on Imladris: the mode said twice, the addresses twice, the backup at the bottom, a frame and a title that said nothing, a ▾ that folded nothing in the sky. Unfolded, the tile now reads from the top: its priority and its mode as menus (the priority read only until the console can write it, CFG5); its group, subscription and resource id, one click copying each; Backup (its points, the last, Snapshot), Compute, Detection, NSG; the network summed up on the NSG row (its NSGs counted once, its NICs, its addresses, the shared ones, a NIC without NSG) and unfolded in a table — a NIC a row, its NSG and where it sits, its addresses, one click copying each — that scrolls past five NICs and keeps its fold; then, after a rule, what waits on the VM. In the sky, the tile is the card: no title, no frame around it, its ✕ in its corner. The folded tile is unchanged.
