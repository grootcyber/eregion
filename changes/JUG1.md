### Added
- **A disruptive action Glorfindel takes alone makes a card to judge, and everything is said in the VM's Discord thread when the bot is configured** (JUG1, #760; `docs/observation.md`, `docs/glorfindel.md`, « alerting webhook + bot Discord »). On the bench on 12/11, gondolin's autonomous isolation (the T1486, `non_disruptive`) was verified and left no card: TO JUDGE stayed empty, the indicator of the disruptive actions taken wrongly could not count it, and its announcement — and the release's — went by the webhook into the channel, while the bot's cards and the closure of the session cut were in the VM's thread. Now an `isolate_vm` or a `block_suspicious_ip` run alone (`non_disruptive`, `full_auto`) and verified makes a card « exécutée » (`autonomous_action`, `glorfindel/agent.py`, `_record_executed`): the action, the VM, the evidence (a few values of the detection's row), the check read back on Azure and the mode. It enters TO JUDGE, is judged like any other (Right, Wrong, Unsure) and counts among the disruptive actions of `glorfindel report` and of the overview's indicator.
  - **One card per action**, by its key (`escalations.executed_key`: the cycle's signal, the VM, the action, the address), even after a restart of the watch, the card judged or acknowledged since or not; a snapshot or a release makes none; a second action, on the same VM too, has its own card, never folded into another's: each is judged and counted.
  - **It changes nothing of the action nor of the autonomy**: its type is not approvable (no one-click execute, no proposal signed, no Revert or Restore button in Discord); its steps and its command name the usual way back (`glorfindel release`, `glorfindel unblock`). Discord titles it « ✅ VM isolée du réseau — exécutée seule, à juger », with its mode, its check and its evidence, escaped (SEC6).
  - **Where it is said**: the announcement of every autonomous action goes as a notice (`glorfindel/vm_notices.py`, DRN2's form), under the action's key — posted by the bot in the VM's thread, beside the card, when the bot is configured, nothing by the webhook; without the bot, by the webhook as before, the card to judge named, and the card's own notification quiet: one message in the channel. An autonomous action always alerts somewhere: the notices' store refused (a full disk), the webhook still says it — without the bot, or with the bot when its card could not be written either (the bot would have nothing to post); with the bot and the card written, the bot says it in the thread, nothing in the channel. A notice decides nothing: forged under an action's key, it silences the announcement, never the card — without the bot, the announcement is the channel's only message, so a forged notice silences it: a known limit, bounded to whoever writes `~/.glorfindel` and guesses the cycle's signal id.
  - **The ping** (Jonathan, 12/11): the card of an action taken alone pings the role (`DISCORD_PING_ROLE`) in the VM's thread, new or already open, its allowed mentions the role's alone; one ping per action — the announcement and the cut's closure never ping, even when the announcement, read first, opens the thread, and the card is posted once, after a restart of the bot too. Any other card as before: the role pinged when its post opens the thread. Known limits: a bot stopped between the card's post and `bot_posted.json` posts it again at its restart, as every card, and this one with its ping; and, rarely, the card refused (its store in error), the notices' store written: the announcement goes to the thread without a ping.
  - With the bot configured but its container stopped, its card and its announcement wait for the bot (announcements older than 24 h are dropped; the card stays pending in the console) — lifted by JUG2 (#765): the bot mute, the webhook says the action.
  - `glorfindel restore <vm> --yes` without `--before` never takes its moment from the card of an action taken alone (`restore_plan.card_of`): a VM isolated alone, without another card, is still refused (SEC4c: no card, no moment).
  - **The console**: a line of TO JUDGE opens its card (its details, its verdict, a note); a tile that folds several cards (the six cards of an NSG, « ×6 », on 12/11) shows each of them in its details, every one with its own reason, explanation, plan (an identity card's, with its Verify) or steps, proposed query and way to its verdict — before, the first one's only. An action taken alone is never grouped with a card of another type (a held isolation): a group's Ack never acknowledges it unread. `/api/to-judge` carries the card's steps and what an action taken alone did. Everything from outside still goes through `_attr` (`tests/unit/test_console_escaping.py`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_judge_card.py` (the real graph on the in-memory Azure, a fake webhook and a fake Discord thread: the isolation and the block, the verdict in the report, a restart, a second action on the same VM, a snapshot and a release, a mode hold, a check that fails, a card refused, nothing approvable, the bot's card, the webhook without the bot, the thread with it, a forged notice, a notices' store refused, a full disk with the bot, the card's ping in a thread new or open, once per action, a restore without a moment, the escaping), `tests/unit/test_judge_console.py`.
