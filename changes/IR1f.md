### Fixed
- **The plan of an identity card never proposes to cut the defense off, says who is protected, and checks the revocation of sessions against the hour of the event** (IR1f, #828; `docs/design/reponse-identite.md` §3.5, `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/operations.md`). Measured on the bench of 14 Nov on four real consent cards: the operator's gesture giving the watch its four reads of Microsoft Graph. The plan proposed first to disable the watch. Its author, the operator, was not said protected. And the hour of the event was empty, so the check of a revocation of sessions never ran.
  - The client of a consent (`ClientId` outside « Consent to application ») is compared with Glorfindel's identities like its target (`entra.target_ids`): the card is Glorfindel's, critical, and says it is an identity of the defense.
  - An identity of the defense is never cut off by default. That is Glorfindel's, by its marks, or one `never_target` lists that a step would disable or whose sessions it would revoke. Its plan begins with what to check (that the gesture is the operator's) and why. Every other step holds only if it is not, optional, never counted by Verify. Glorfindel's disabling is never in it.
  - An author `never_target` lists is said protected, and nothing cuts it off: neither its disabling nor a revocation of its sessions. What was added to it is still removed, as a human's step with never_target's reason; that covers its own credential, the classic persistence of a stolen one. A predicate that is no list (no `listed`) protects no one. A human's verb on a listed identity says never_target's reason too. An unreadable list names no one: nothing is dropped, and every verb stays a human's step with its reason.
  - `TimeGenerated` is read as `azure-monitor-query` gives it (a `datetime`, its `str()` with a space for the `T`), written again in UTC. Absent or illegible, the plan says it, and Verify says « unreadable », never « held ».
  - The card, the console (ahead of the plan) and Discord (a field of its own) say who is protected and why. They title it « an identity of the defense » for Glorfindel's alone, and « protected » for any other: an operator's account, a listed identity. The check the plan begins with gives the watch's reads of Graph as its example only for a consent given to Glorfindel. Nothing changes in what Glorfindel does alone: the plan stays a proposal to a human, no action, no call to the model, the prompt untouched.
