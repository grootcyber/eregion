### Security
- **What accumulates around the exercise windows is bounded, and their marks held to the injectors' own guard** (SEC-A1b, #642; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, `docs/fichiers-cles.md`). The watch's record of the windows it saw keeps dates and digests only, for windows it believes, with a share per writer and a size bound, and is read by the bounded reader; past them, a window is not believed (its cycles fall toward real). The pull takes only windows, each under 64 KiB, into a bounded directory, and purges what is no window there. The cut past the number of window files read gives each directory its turn, newest first, said once while it lasts. The files of a writer the watch follows are bounded, the newest first, a name gone forgotten. A window marks a card under the injectors' own guard: a fault, or a gesture not declared non-destructive, only inside the bench's perimeter; a technique declared non-destructive within its target's subscription and group, its tags deciding the rest (the production sentinel included, O0's rule holding there); and always in a zone written exactly as the injectors accept it. A window pulled from the channel starts within five minutes of its blob's creation. The local heartbeat is read by the bounded reader, and only the watch mounts its directory in writing. A campaign's manifest is read by the bounded reader when it is published.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_exercise_accumulations.py`; mutants `scripts/mutants/SEC-A1b.py`.
