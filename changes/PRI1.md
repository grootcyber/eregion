### Added
- **Design: a classification of the VMs, its levels and their effects** (PRI1, #1216; `docs/design/classification.md`). Today a VM has one yes or no, `critical: true` on an entry of `autonomy.assets` (PRT2): its grave detections keep part of the model budget's reserve and never wait for the provider's cool-down. The note proposes a real classification. A VM's class is the operator's declaration, deterministic, never an estimate of the model, and it sets Glorfindel's attention, never its guards: a third axis beside the zone (what an experiment may do there) and the mode (what Glorfindel does alone, the decision). Three levels, in words that do not alarm: `essential` (today's `critical: true`), `standard` (the default, nothing changes) and `quiet` (watched by every rule, with less noise and budget for its low detections, a gentler way out than an exclusion). The note inventories every reader of `critical` in the code and states four findings: setting it forces a mode on the entry, and under a `non_disruptive` default the change is classed as a relaxation; the word already names a detection's severity and a class of the queue; the push to the hosted watch copies any section other than the autonomy as it is; a section the change classification does not list is neutral. It sets out the effects level by level: the model's reserve, the rank in the gate at equal gravity, the dossier always composed for an essential VM, a deeper enquiry within the same deadline (0.7), a ping for each card of an essential VM that waits for a human, no ping when a thread opens on a low detection of a quiet VM. A grave detection stays grave at every level; a card's severity never changes. The mode stays the decision: an essential VM only gets a readiness reserve to confirm once. It proposes a section of its own, `priorities:` (`match`, `level`, `reason`), the highest level winning; `critical: true` read as `essential` until 1.0, a contradiction between the two being a configuration error; the watch's reading and the push's held equal by a test, the push never lowering a level. Raising a level tightens and applies at once; lowering relaxes and is confirmed, a `quiet` entry showing in the « Relaxed » view. It drafts what the console shows (the tile, the Config page, the watch's start, the phone), the invariants, the mutants and the bench measure, asks ten questions of the maintainer and proposes seven lots, PRI2 to PRI7 with CRT1, the rest in 0.7 with PRT3. What the model sees does not change. No code changes.
