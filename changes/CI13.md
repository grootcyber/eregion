### Fixed
- **A nightly invariant run cut by its bound now says its seed and where its machine runs** (CI13, #986; `docs/design/preuves.md`, « Une nuit coupée par sa borne dit sa graine et où elle tourne »). On 20/11 the nightly Azure step was cut at its 125 minutes with nothing after `collected 1 item`: no counterexample, no seed, no statistics — the seed was said only at the end of the run, which a cut step never reaches.
  - The run's Hypothesis seed is now said as the run begins, before any test, in a line copied as is (`EREGION_HYPOTHESIS_SEED=<seed>`); the end of the run, each failure and the step's summary still say it.
  - At night only (a seed drawn for the run, or more than a push's 500 sequences), a test that runs an invariant machine has a watchdog. At `GLORFINDEL_INVARIANT_WATCHDOG` minutes after the run began (by default 115 for Azure's machine, under its step's 125; 40 for AWS's, under 45) it writes on the stderr the run began with, never captured: the seed and its replay, the sequences begun, how long the current one has run and the longest before it, and every thread's stack, read by Python under the GIL (safe while the machine's threads run). faulthandler is its backstop, a minute later, only when Python could not speak; a run that set pytest's own `faulthandler_timeout` (the release gate) keeps its own. The run is not stopped. When the machine ends, a line says its sequences and the longest. Pull requests and pushes are unchanged: derandomized, no watchdog.
  - The night of 20/11, tried again on the workstation (one process, 3 000 sequences a pass, seeds drawn, its head and main's, Azure's machine and AWS's): no sequence that does not end, none over 5 s. Not a proof, the night's seed being lost; the steps' durations on GitHub point to a slow runner (AWS's step 2.4 times slower than the workstation that day: Azure's 10 000 sequences, 50 to 70 min on the workstation, take 115 to 165 min at that pace, at the bound or past it). The next nights, which say their seed, are read before any bound is changed. AWS's step took up to 42 min on green nights: its watchdog, at 40, will speak on some of them, its stacks then a slow machine's, not a sequence that hangs.
  - Under xdist, the controller tells its workers that the seed it gives them was drawn (`EREGION_HYPOTHESIS_SEED_DRAWN`): a worker running a machine arms its watchdog as the controller would.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_invariant_watchdog.py` (a sequence that never ends in a pytest of its own, killed by SIGKILL as a bound cuts a step: its log holds the seed and the stack down to the rule that runs; the defaults read against `invariants.yml`'s bounds). Mutants M6800–M6809 (`scripts/mutants/CI13.py`).
