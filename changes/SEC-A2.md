### Security
- **The watch's decision queue is bounded: a burst of forged detections no longer delays a grave one nor drives calls to the model without limit** (SEC-A2, #631; audit of 06/11, V-03; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La file de décision, bornée »). Each detection of the data plane waited for its decision in a first-in, first-out queue per VM with no ceiling, and each decision is at least one call to the model; an aggregated row is identified by its text columns, so values written in log lines by a local account on a watched VM each made a detection. `glorfindel/decision_queue.py` now holds four bounds, before any call: the queue of a VM holds at most 50 detections (beyond, the least grave and newest leaves it, said in the watch's log and counted on a card); the gravest first (critical severity, then the techniques where data is destroyed or leaves: T1486, T1485, T1490, T1041, T1567 — by technique, never by the rule's text); one decision of the model per VM, rule and gravity in a window of 300 s, the other detections of the RulePoller deduplicated without a call; a ceiling of 10 calls to the model per minute for the whole watch, of which the low class takes at most 6, the draws of D-1 counted. A detection held by these bounds becomes a card `undecided_detection`: no action, no investigation, nothing in the memory, one card per VM and rule naming its latest lines and counting them by reason. Cards of the control plane and of identities, decided by the code, are not held. Limits stated in the documentation: a detection held this way goes through none of `decide`'s guards, the floor of a confirmed compromise included (a critical detection is never deduplicated behind a lower one); the counts restart with the watch.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_decision_queue.py`; mutants M4525–M4528.
- **The older lines of a pass are named on a card, none marked sent unsaid** (SEC-A2, #631; audit of 06/11, V-13). For a rule of event lines (sudo, `useradd`, crontab), only the newest line of a pass made the detection and the others were marked sent: a decoy line written just after the real one hid it. The RulePoller still sends the newest line as it is, and now one more signal of the same VM carries the others (one line, or a summary), marked for a card only: the queue makes it a card `undecided_detection` that names them, never a call to the model. What the model reads is unchanged: neither `_build_user_message()`, nor `_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, nor `few_shot_examples.yaml` is touched. auditd's fold (AUD1) is unchanged.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_decision_queue.py`, `tests/unit/test_detection_rules.py`, `tests/unit/test_forged_logs.py`; mutant M4529.
