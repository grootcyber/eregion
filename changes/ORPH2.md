### Added
- **An older version's isolation left on an NSG that governs nothing is said, never taken off** (ORPH2, #1113; `docs/azure.md`, `docs/glorfindel.md`, `docs/design/preuves.md`). A version older than the placements isolated a VM with a deny any/any under two fixed names (`glorfindel-isolation-deny-all`, `-out`) on its NIC's own NSG. With its state lost, then our quarantine with its tags lost, the NIC left bare by someone and the state lost again, the pair stayed on an NSG attached to no NIC and no subnet. The release rightly said `released`, and neither the orphan sweep nor the reset found the pair. Nothing told the operator, and that NSG put back on the NIC cut the VM. The orphan sweep's pass (each minute in the watch, or the posture check when the reassertion does not run) now reads every fixed-name deny on the NSG listing it already shares. On an NSG that governs nothing, it raises a `dormant_isolation` card ("possible older Glorfindel isolation", medium, on the NSG). The card names the NSG and the rules, says why nothing is taken off, and gives the `az network nsg rule show` and `delete` commands to run by hand after a check. Nothing is written on Azure, in any mode: the name alone never proves whose the rules are (another tool, another Glorfindel host). The card is said once, pending or acknowledged. It closes when the rules are no longer on the NSG, or when the NSG is gone, proven by a read by its id: an NSG missing from the listing keeps its card. When the NSG governs a NIC or a subnet again, the card is not closed but replaced by a high one that names what the NSG governs now, because that is when the rules cut. A pair on an NSG that governs something, never said dormant, stays with the paths of its VM. The console labels the new type and keeps it out of "to judge".
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_dormant_isolation.py` (the reviewed world replayed step by step on the in-memory Azure, every closing and replacing case, the posture path, the pinned SDK's NSG listing). Mutants M7206–M7215 (`scripts/mutants/ORPH2.py`).
