### Changed
- **The invariant machine judges a held block on every way to and from the attacker, by port and protocol** (I3Xb, #1035; `docs/design/reaffirmation.md`, §5, E4; `docs/design/preuves.md`). No change to the product. The oracle behind `blocked`, « reposé une fois », « reste bloquée », a true `verify_block_ip` and the drift said of a block read the probes alone: all TCP, none going out to the attacker except on 443. An allow before our deny going out on 22 or on UDP, which the product's model counts as open, went unseen there: a card « reste bloquée » that was false would have passed. The oracle now evaluates the twelve ways of `ATTACKER_WAYS` on the test's own NSGs: in on the threat port (every port when it is unknown), TCP and UDP, and out on every port and protocol. It never reads the code's own verification.
  - The machine also never met that state on its own (two edits of one rule, after a block of both scopes, on a NIC whose subnet's NSG holds both pairs). A new composite rule, `a_way_narrowed_then_turned`, builds it: the VM blocked for itself, on its subnet, for itself again; one of our denies narrowed to port 22, turned to UDP or left whole, then turned into an allow; the claims checked, a pass.
  - Measured: M5331 (I3X) is caught by the machine alone, without the frozen sequences, on 4 seeds of 4 at 200 examples (3 of 6 before). Three new mutants, where the product reads an outbound allow on 22, an outbound allow on UDP or an inbound allow on UDP on the threat port as closed, pass on the old oracle and are caught on the new one. The cost: over four seeds at 200 examples, 277 s before, 281 s after (+1 %); the oracle itself is under 1 % of a run.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_invariants.py`, `tests/unit/test_invariant_regressions.py`. Mutants M7054–M7056 (`scripts/mutants/I3Xb.py`).
