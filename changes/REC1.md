### Added
- **The watch reads on Azure the recovery of a VM it isolated, and lifts the isolation itself** (REC1, #668; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La reprise lue sur Azure »). H0 never restores (SEC17): a restore runs from the workstation, whose `recovery_complete` stays on that host, so H0's watch never released on its own, and its reassertion put back a release made elsewhere — two human gestures on the bench, restore then release. Every `GLORFINDEL_RECOVERY_INTERVAL_S` (60 s), for each isolation of its state, the watch reads through the adapter (`observed_restore`, `glorfindel/providers/azure/recovery.py`), never from another host's file nor from the model: the VM's last restore job since its isolation, finished and `Completed`; the point it restored, named by the job and matched to one of the VM's recovery points, before the start of the incident (the isolation, or the first detection of its pending card when earlier: `restore_plan.incident_start`, the rule of `--before`); the VM running; Run Command v1's word in the instance view, dated after the restore (the restored disk replays the VM model's last command at boot), for `run_command_neutralized`; and the scripts a restored disk may replay. It then builds its own `recovery_complete`, with the same fields as `restore --wait` and vouched for in its process, and hands it to the VM's queue. The release follows as on a workstation: autonomous where the mode allows, a `mode_hold` card in `human_only`, the release precondition unchanged. A failed or cancelled job, a point not before the incident, an unreadable point: one `release_hold` card (action `review_recovery`, nothing to approve in one click), no release. A VM not running 10 min after its job ended: one card, and the release follows once it runs. Once per job (`~/.glorfindel/recoveries.json`, written before the signal leaves). A restore claimed on this host, or a job ended less than `GLORFINDEL_RECOVERY_GRACE_S` (300 s) ago, waits. Reads only, under the Reader role H0 already holds on the bench's group, where the vault is. Unmeasured on the bench: the restore job's property bag, and Run Command's word after a restored disk boots. Either missing gives a card, never a release.
