### Fixed
- **Why the push CI's Linux job on Python 3.12 fell red on the invariant machine, measured and written down** (CI12, #899; `docs/operations.md`, « La CI au bon endroit »; `docs/design/preuves.md`). Among the suite's four processes, Azure's machine at 500 sequences ran 8 to 12 min on 3.12. At 8 min, faulthandler wrote every thread's stack. Its watchdog reads the frames of a running thread without holding the GIL, while CPython frees them: a SIGSEGV in a thread that blocks every signal, so the worker died without a message (four dumps in six on main's pushes of 17–18/11). Reproduced on a workstation. A blocked thread's stack stays safe: the dump still serves a hang. The fix to the workflow is `changes/CI12-workflow.md`.
