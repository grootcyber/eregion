### Added
- **The Discord bot silent, the webhook announces an action Glorfindel takes alone — once, the role pinged** (JUG2, #765; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « alerting webhook + bot Discord »). Since JUG1, with the bot configured, an autonomous action is announced in its VM's thread and nothing goes by the webhook; with the bot's container stopped, nothing said it any more — its card and its announcement waited for the bot, and an announcement older than 24 h was lost. Now the bot writes a heartbeat at each turn of its loop (`~/.glorfindel/bot_heartbeat.json`, `glorfindel/bot_beat.py`, every 5 s once its channel is read) and removes it when it stops; the watch reads it.
  - **Before announcing an action taken alone** (`vm_notices.say`): the beat absent, unreadable, dated more than 30 s in the future or **older than 3 min** (`MUTE_AFTER_S` = 180 s, some 36 turns), the bot is mute. With a webhook set, the notice is recorded `via: webhook` with `relayed` (when, why, whether it pinged) and the webhook says it in the channel — on a Discord webhook by Discord's own endpoint (the `/slack` suffix taken off), the bot's message escaped as the bot escapes it (SEC6), the role `DISCORD_PING_ROLE` first and the only mention allowed (`allowed_mentions`); on another webhook (Slack, Teams), its Slack message, no ping. The watch now receives `DISCORD_PING_ROLE` (an id, no secret; `docker-compose.yml`).
  - **At the passes after** (`vm_notices.relay_pass`, the watch's housekeeping, about every minute): an announcement left to the bot that the bot has not posted (its key absent from `bot_notices_posted.json`) goes by the webhook the same way when the bot reads mute — the bot dead after the action, before its post — or, whatever its beat says, once it is 10 min old (`CONFIRM_S`). Within 24 h, as the bot's own.
  - **Once per action, nothing twice when the bot comes back**: the notice is rewritten before the webhook is called, and the bot never posts it (it reads each notice again just before its post). The card to judge is posted at the bot's return in the VM's thread with its buttons, **without a ping** when the webhook pinged for it; the webhook pings no one when the bot posted the card already. The bot alive, nothing changes from JUG1.
  - **DRN2's boundary holds**: the beat, and the two stores of the bot the watch reads (`bot_notices_posted.json`, `bot_posted.json`), decide only the announcement's path and its ping — never the card, written before any of them is read, nor any state. A beat forged fresh by a writer of `~/.glorfindel` (the console, the bot) only delays the fallback: 3 min once written, 10 min at most if kept fresh; a beat dated in the future reads mute (`docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`).
  - Known limits: without a webhook the announcement still waits for the bot; a bot that posts an announcement and stops before writing `bot_notices_posted.json`, then read mute, has it said again by the webhook; the notices' store refused (a full disk) with the bot mute, the webhook says the action with its ping but nothing records it, so the card pings again at the bot's return. Only the announcements of actions taken alone fall back; a session cut's closure (DRN2) still waits for the bot.
  - JUG1's wording made exact: `.envrc.example` (the role also pinged at each action taken alone), and the two known limits of its ping (`docs/glorfindel.md`, `changes/JUG1.md`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bot_fallback.py` (the real graph on the in-memory Azure, a fake webhook, a fake Discord thread: the bot stopped, stale, alive, back; a bot silent after the action; a card or an announcement it posted already; its read before a post; a beat forged fresh, in the future, unreadable; no webhook; a Slack webhook; a full disk; a role that is not an id; the escaping; Discord's endpoint; the bot's beat written and removed). Mutants M4970–M4979 in `scripts/mutants/JUG2.py`.
