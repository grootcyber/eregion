### Added
- **The Discord bot silent, the webhook announces an action Glorfindel takes alone — once, the role pinged** (JUG2, #765; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « alerting webhook + bot Discord »). Since JUG1, with the bot configured, an autonomous action is announced in its VM's thread and nothing goes by the webhook; with the bot's container stopped, nothing said it any more — its card and its announcement waited for the bot, and an announcement older than 24 h was lost. Now the bot writes a heartbeat at each turn of its loop (`~/.glorfindel/bot_heartbeat.json`, `glorfindel/bot_beat.py`, every 5 s once its channel is read) and removes it when it stops, on a SIGTERM too (`docker stop`: the bot is PID 1 of its container, where Python installs no handler — it is now read as a Ctrl-C); a SIGKILL or a host cut leave it, and the fallback then waits for it to age, 3 min and a pass of the watch. The watch reads it.
  - **Before announcing an action taken alone** (`vm_notices.say`): the beat absent, unreadable, dated more than 30 s in the future or **older than 3 min** (`MUTE_AFTER_S` = 180 s, some 36 turns), the bot is mute. With a webhook set, the notice is recorded `via: webhook` with `relayed` (when, why, whether it pinged) and the webhook says it in the channel — on a Discord webhook by Discord's own endpoint (`/api/webhooks/` or `/api/v<n>/webhooks/`, the `/slack` suffix taken off), the bot's message escaped as the bot escapes it (SEC6), the role `DISCORD_PING_ROLE` first and the only mention allowed (`allowed_mentions`); on another webhook (Slack, Teams), its Slack message, no ping. The announcement's text names the card and the run before the explanation, so that no cut (Slack's 800 characters) loses them. The watch now receives `DISCORD_PING_ROLE` (an id, no secret; `docker-compose.yml`).
  - **At the passes after** (`vm_notices.relay_pass`, the watch's housekeeping, about every minute): an announcement left to the bot that the bot has not posted (its key absent from `bot_notices_posted.json`) goes by the webhook the same way when the bot reads mute — the bot dead after the action, before its post — or, whatever its beat says, once it is 10 min old (`CONFIRM_S`). Within 24 h, as the bot's own; the posts of one pass spaced (`RELAY_SPACING_S`, 0.5 s).
  - **A webhook that fails is never counted as said**: only a 2xx answer is a post, and the notice is then noted `relayed.sent`. Refused, unreachable, or rate limited (a 429 waited for once when it asks no longer than 5 s; else the rest of the pass waits for the next one), the notice goes back to the bot: the next pass tries again, or the bot posts it at its return and its card pings — a rare double rather than a silence. A mark never noted sent (the watch stopped mid-send, its note refused) is taken back after 2 min (`RESEND_AFTER_S`).
  - **Once per action when the bot comes back**: the notice is rewritten before the webhook is called, and the bot does not post it (it reads each notice again just before its post). The card to judge is posted at the bot's return in the VM's thread with its buttons, **without a ping** when the webhook pinged for it and the post was noted sent; the webhook pings no one when the bot posted the card already. The bot alive, nothing changes from JUG1.
  - **DRN2's boundary holds**: the beat, and the two stores of the bot the watch reads (`bot_notices_posted.json`, `bot_posted.json`), decide only the announcement's path and its ping — never the card, written before any of them is read, nor any state. A beat forged fresh by a writer of `~/.glorfindel` (the console, the bot) only delays the fallback: 3 min once written, 10 min at most if kept fresh; a beat dated in the future reads mute. A notice forged under an action's key, relayed and noted sent, silences the announcement and can silence the card's ping too — never the card (`docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`).
  - Known limits: without a webhook the announcement still waits for the bot; a post of the bot in flight while the watch relays (a `thread.send` held by Discord's rate limits) says the announcement twice, and the same race on the card can ping twice; a webhook that delivered but whose answer was lost has it said again; a bot that posts an announcement and stops before writing `bot_notices_posted.json`, then read mute, has it said again by the webhook; the notices' store refused (a full disk) with the bot mute, the webhook says the action with its ping but nothing records it, so the card pings again at the bot's return. A session cut's closure (DRN2) falls back too since JUG2b.
  - JUG1's wording made exact: `.envrc.example` (the role also pinged at each action taken alone), and the two known limits of its ping (`docs/glorfindel.md`, `changes/JUG1.md`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bot_fallback.py` (the real graph on the in-memory Azure, a fake webhook, a fake Discord thread: the bot stopped, stale, alive, back; a bot silent after the action; a card or an announcement it posted already; its read before a post; a webhook that answers 429, 404, 500 or raises, then the bot back; a relay tried again at the next pass; a short 429 waited for; a burst of actions; a pass rate limited; a mark never noted sent; a forged notice and the card's ping; a long explanation on Slack; a beat forged fresh, in the future, unreadable; no webhook; a Slack webhook; a full disk; a role that is not an id; the escaping; Discord's endpoint, versioned too; the bot's beat written, removed, and removed by a real SIGTERM). Mutants M4970–M4979 and M4995–M4999 in `scripts/mutants/JUG2.py`.
