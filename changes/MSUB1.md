### Added
- **Design: one watch per client, over several subscriptions of one tenant** (MSUB1, #1196; `docs/design/watch-multi-abonnement.md`). Today a watch holds one subscription by construction: `AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` is mandatory, every SDK client is built for it alone, 15 methods of the connector and the removal of an orphan rule refuse any target of another one (`WrongSubscriptionError`), and 27 listings of Azure read it alone; yet the discovery, the enquiry and the activity log already see several subscriptions through a shared workspace. The note inventories, from the code, everything that assumes one subscription: the discovery, the isolation, the blocks, the restore and the vaults, the reads of the enquiry and of the control plane, the configuration and the autonomy resolved by a VM's name, the enrolment of O0 and the production rule, the heartbeat for the injectors, the host, its deployment and the Terraform of its roles. It notes two limits of today's watch with a shared workspace: a private address reused by a VM of another subscription, and that subscription's public addresses, unknown to the guard of the client's network. It recommends one identity for the watch, holding in each added subscription the roles it holds in its own, on the enrolled resource groups, declared in infrastructure as code: one identity per subscription bounds nothing more on one host, and a role on the management group widens in silence to any subscription created under it. It proposes three states for a subscription (seen, read, in reach), the declaration and the rights read on Azure both needed, never one without the other; an added subscription inheriting no autonomy, `human_only` by default; no zone given to a subscription, the zone staying each resource's. On a tile out of reach, the console explains the missing role, its scope, the `az` command and the Terraform block; Glorfindel never grants itself a right, and declaring a subscription to act goes through the configuration queue as a relaxation, confirmed. What the model sees does not change. A subscription only read gets connectors read-only by construction, and the guard compares a target with the declared list, never with the connector's own subscription, before any write in another subscription exists. No write is ever made outside a subscription in reach: withdrawing its reach is refused while an isolation or a block is held there, and said. The blast radius is said whole: the response's writes on the declared groups, and Run Command as root on every VM given the session drain, listed VM by VM in Terraform as in the watch's own subscription. It says what would prepare several tenants without doing it, recommends fixing the first limit at once (MSUB0, before the rest), proposes nine lots, MSUB0, MSUB2 to MSUB7, VLT1 and MSUB3b (on need), and asks twelve questions of the maintainer. No code changes.
