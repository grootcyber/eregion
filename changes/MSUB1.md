### Added
- **Design: one watch per client, over several subscriptions of one tenant** (MSUB1, #1196; `docs/design/watch-multi-abonnement.md`). Today a watch holds one subscription by construction: `AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` is mandatory, every SDK client is built for it alone, 15 methods of the connector and the removal of an orphan rule refuse any target of another one (`WrongSubscriptionError`), and 27 listings of Azure read it alone; yet the discovery, the enquiry and the activity log already see several subscriptions through a shared workspace. The note inventories, from the code, everything that assumes one subscription: the discovery, the isolation, the blocks, the restore and the vaults, the reads of the enquiry and of the control plane, the configuration and the autonomy resolved by a VM's name, the enrolment of O0 and the production rule, the heartbeat for the injectors, the host, its deployment and the Terraform of its roles. It notes two limits of today's watch with a shared workspace: a private address reused by a VM of another subscription, and that subscription's public addresses, unknown to the guard of the client's network. It recommends one identity for the watch, holding in each added subscription the roles it holds in its own, on the enrolled resource groups, declared in infrastructure as code: one identity per subscription bounds nothing more on one host, and a role on the management group widens in silence to any subscription created under it. It proposes three states for a subscription (seen, read, in reach), the declaration and the rights read on Azure both needed, never one without the other; an added subscription inheriting no autonomy, `human_only` by default; no zone given to a subscription, the zone staying each resource's. On a tile out of reach, the console explains the missing role, its scope, the `az` command and the Terraform block; Glorfindel never grants itself a right, and declaring a subscription to act goes through the configuration queue as a relaxation, confirmed. What the model sees does not change. It says what would prepare several tenants without doing it, proposes eight lots, MSUB2 to MSUB7 with VLT1 and MSUB3b, and asks eight questions of the maintainer. No code changes.
