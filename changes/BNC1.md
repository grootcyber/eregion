### Added
- **`make bench-preflight`: the bench's step 0 in one verdict** (BNC1, #682; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 0; `docs/operations.md`, « Le banc lisible »). The T1486 replay of 09/11 took about 4 h: every check of the step 0 was typed by hand, and a missing right (`listKeys` on the staging account) was seen only at the restore. One line ✓, ⚠ or ✗ per check, then a summary; exit 1 on a ✗, a ⚠ does not stop the run. Read only, on Azure, H0 and Orodruin, under the operator's `az login` and the pairs of their environment:
  - H0's deployed reference, its containers and its CD, as `make glorfindel-host-status` and `make glorfindel-host-cd-status` say them; no local watch (`docker ps`);
  - Orodruin's contract read on it by `check()`, never published, armed and its cap; deallocated, a warning;
  - the pairs, by `scripts/credential_expiry.py` (RST1): the console's, which restores, is required;
  - the target: its group, its mode and readiness on H0, the quarantine, no isolation already; the age of its newest recovery point (a warning past `POINT_MAX_H`, 24 h);
  - the effective rights: the console under its own pair, by `Microsoft.Authorization/permissions` with the restore's own reader (`restore_rights.py`), the neutralization's Run Command included; H0 and Orodruin by their role assignments and the roles' definitions, wildcards and `notActions` read as Azure reads them, their ids read from Terraform's outputs;
  - the known drifts: « Eregion Glorfindel Restore » that still holds `runCommand/action` (before SEC17) warns; « Eregion Glorfindel Restore Staging » not on the console at the staging account fails.
- **`make bench-report RUN=<run_id>`: the chronology of a run, composed alone** (BNC1; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 8). It reads, on H0 by ssh (`scripts/bench_h0_reader.py`, in the watch's container: the run's report, its cycles, the lifts, the isolation state; never a reasoning nor a raw signal), the target group's activity log, the vault's jobs and the instance view. It prints the form of the 09/11 report — the figures from T0, the `restore_time` in two numbers (the job, the start, and whether the quarantine held it until the lift), the chronology, each Run Command with its caller as a role (the console, the bot, H0, Orodruin, a human, unknown) and its duration — and writes the same in JSON (`runs/bench/<run_id>.json`, or `OUT=`), ready to feed `docs/proofs.json` later. The Dagorlad request of a wire is linked when the run carries it.
  - No identifier is printed by either target: each line says a role, a kind of scope or a public bench name, and goes through one mask (GUID, IPv4, UPN, ARM id) before it is printed or written. `TARGET`, `RUN`, `OUT` and `POINT_MAX_H` reach the scripts through the environment, never expanded by make, checked there.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bench_preflight.py`, `tests/unit/test_bench_report.py`, on a fake `az`, `terraform`, `docker`, `make`, Orodruin and H0 read by ssh (the reader run for real on a fake H0), and a fake Azure Resource Manager; with the shapes measured on 09/11, the preflight sees the missing `listKeys`, an absent pair, a point too old and a role from before SEC17, and the report composes again T0, the detection at 70.3 s, the isolation, the 302 s drain, the 152 s neutralization, the 15 min 17 job, the lift and the agent `Ready`.
