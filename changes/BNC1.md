### Added
- **`make bench-preflight`: the bench's step 0 in one verdict** (BNC1, #682; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 0; `docs/operations.md`, « Le banc lisible »). The T1486 replay of 09/11 took about 4 h: every check of the step 0 was typed by hand, and a missing right (`listKeys` on the staging account) was seen only at the restore. One line ✓, ⚠ or ✗ per check, then a summary; exit 1 on a ✗, a ⚠ does not stop the run. Read only, on Azure, H0 and Orodruin, under the operator's `az login` and the pairs of their environment:
  - H0's deployed reference, its containers and its CD, as `make glorfindel-host-status` and `make glorfindel-host-cd-status` say them; no local watch (`docker ps`);
  - Orodruin's contract read on it by `check()`, never published, armed and its cap; deallocated, a warning;
  - the pairs, by `scripts/credential_expiry.py` (RST1): the console's, which restores, is required;
  - the target: its group, its mode and readiness on H0, the quarantine, no isolation already; the age of its newest recovery point (a warning past `POINT_MAX_H`, 24 h);
  - the effective rights: the console under its own pair, by `Microsoft.Authorization/permissions` with the restore's own reader (`restore_rights.py`), the neutralization's Run Command included; H0 and Orodruin by their role assignments and the roles' definitions, wildcards and `notActions` read as Azure reads them, their ids read from Terraform's outputs;
  - the known drifts: « Eregion Glorfindel Restore » that still holds `runCommand/action` (before SEC17) warns; « Eregion Glorfindel Restore Staging » not on the console at the staging account fails.
- **`make bench-report RUN=<run_id>`: the chronology of a run, composed alone** (BNC1; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 8). It reads, on H0 by ssh, the run's report, its cycles, the lifts and the isolation state; then the activity log of the target and of its NICs, each read apart (a read that reaches its cap is said cut), the vault's jobs and the instance view. It prints the form of the 09/11 report — the figures from T0, the `restore_time` in two numbers (the job, the start, and whether the quarantine held it until the lift), the chronology, each Run Command with its caller as a role (the console, the bot, H0, Orodruin, a human, unknown) and its duration — and writes the same in JSON (`runs/bench/<run_id>.json`, or `OUT=`), ready to feed `docs/proofs.json` later. The Dagorlad request of a wire is linked when the run carries it.
  - H0's reader is read-only by construction: never run inside the watch (`docker exec`), but in a disposable container of the deployed watch's image (`docker inspect` gives its id and its mounts), `docker run --rm --network none --read-only --cap-drop ALL --security-opt no-new-privileges`, with the watch's runs, its state and its configuration mounted `readonly` — no network, so neither IMDS nor a token. It sends back neither a reasoning nor a raw signal. Nothing goes to H0 nor to Orodruin until their host key is verified by its fingerprint. A later step: a read-only sub-command shipped in the image.
  - The isolation, once H0's state is gone, is dated by H0's write on one of the target's NICs; an isolation by an NSG's rules alone does not show there. An assignment at a management group is not read: a missing right says so. The JSON and the Markdown are written side by side, `OUT=x.md` included. A failure of any kind is said in one masked line, never a Python trace.
  - No identifier is printed by either target: each line says a role, a kind of scope or a public bench name, and goes through one mask (GUID, IPv4, UPN, ARM id) before it is printed or written. `TARGET`, `RUN`, `OUT` and `POINT_MAX_H` reach the scripts through the environment, never expanded by make, checked there.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bench_preflight.py`, `tests/unit/test_bench_report.py`, on a fake `az`, `terraform`, `docker`, `make`, Orodruin and H0 read by ssh (the reader run for real on a fake H0), and a fake Azure Resource Manager; with the shapes measured on 09/11, the preflight sees the missing `listKeys`, an absent pair, a point too old and a role from before SEC17, and the report composes again T0, the detection at 70.3 s, the isolation, the 302 s drain, the 152 s neutralization, the 15 min 17 job, the lift and the agent `Ready`. Mutants M4676–M4685, M4706–M4709 and M4730 (`scripts/mutants/BNC1.py`).
