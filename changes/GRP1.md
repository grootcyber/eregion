### Added
- **The infrastructure map grouped by subscription or by resource group, and what is out of reach** (GRP1, #1189; `docs/design/console.md`, « La carte groupée par abonnement ou par resource group »). Jonathan's choice on a mockup (25/11, option B). Several workspaces see VMs of several subscriptions, and the map mixed them without saying so, while a watch acts only in its own.
  - A « group by » selector above the map: none (the map of before, by default), subscription, resource group. The choice is kept in the browser (`glorfindel_group_by`), as the tiles' order: nothing is written on the server.
  - Grouped, a lane each: the tenant, the watch's subscription (« Glorfindel acts here »), the others (« seen, out of reach »), the unknown last; each tile in the order of its slot. A lane is named by the subscription's name when the watch serves it (SUBN1), else by its masked id; two subscriptions of one name carry their masked id beside it. On a phone, a lane is a heading over its tiles.
  - A tile of another subscription than the watch's is out of reach: dashed, an OUT OF REACH · DETECT ONLY chip, no line from PROTECT, no button that writes in Azure, on its tile or in its windows; its cards are still judged, and one that waited for an approval says « out of reach: detect only », never « pending approval ». « Review & turn on » goes with the writes, and no fixed line comes from RECOVER (a vault reads its items by name). The watch's subscription unknown: every tile keeps its actions, and the selector's bar says so. Compared on the masked id of `/api/config`: no new identifier exposed, no new field.
  - The logic in `eregion_console/static/infra_groups.js`, pure, run by the suite as it is.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_groups.py`; mutants M7651–M7658 and M7702–M7705 (`scripts/mutants/GRP1.py`).
