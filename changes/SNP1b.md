### Fixed
- **The vault's job the console names is checked before the bench follows it** (SNP1b, #801; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, « le job du coffre »; `docs/design/banc-autonome.md`, §3.1). Since SNP1, `scripts/bench_snapshot.py` followed a named job with `az backup job show` until `Completed` without reading what job it was, and an `rsv:` name of another vault given by `job_id` alone fell back, in silence, on the job looked for by its time, while the same name given by `vault_job` stopped the run. Now every answer of `show`, from the first, is checked before its status: a job whose VM (`entityFriendlyName`, compared regardless of case) is not the target, whose container (`iaasvmcontainerv2;<group>;<vm>`, when the job names one) is of another group than the target's — a namesake, deleted, its backup left in the vault —, whose operation is not `Backup`, or whose VM or operation does not read, stops the run at that read and says which: it is never followed to `Completed`, and no point is read. An `rsv:` name of another vault or group than the workstation's stops the run whether it comes from `vault_job` or `job_id`, never replaced by the search by time; the stop says which field gave it and what differs, the vault, the group, both, or its shape, without printing any of the names. A read of the job in error is still read again: only three in a row stop the run. The limit: the fields read are those `find_job` already filters on in `az backup job list`, and the container a restore is read by; their presence in `az backup job show`'s answer is read from Azure's job resource, to be seen again at the bench.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bench_snapshot.py` (`bench_snapshot.take`, `az_json` and the clock faked: another VM's job, a namesake of another group, a restore, a configuration, a job whose fields do not read, direct or under FS1, stop at the first `show` — one read, no point read; the VM's name in another case followed; a read in error read again, the errors counted in a row only; an `rsv:` name of another vault, group, both, or a shape not read, from `vault_job` or `job_id`, stops with no `az` call). Mutants M5245–M5249, M5265–M5269.
