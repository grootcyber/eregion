### Changed
- **The bound of the model's spending said right: on Eregion's side, the watch's breaker is its only bound** (DOC9, #1166; `docs/operations.md`, « Le budget du modèle dans la watch », `docs/raisonnement.md` §2.5, `docs/design/enquete-raisonnee.md` §15). The docs and three messages of the watch (its start line, the card of a count lost, the alert's last step) no longer say that the provider holds the hard ceiling, which supposed a limit set there: the breaker is the watch's only bound unless the operator sets a limit at the provider's, and Eregion requires none. The docs say what the breaker does not bound (the harness and the sessions run by hand, each with its own ceiling; Annatar's planner, its own process; a second watch, its own count; a price LiteLLM does not know, 0.05 $ a call; a count file lost, the day from 0), the provider's monthly cap by the organisation's tier, with its source, and its two answers (a 429 `rate_limit_error`, code `enforced_spend_limit_reached`, for the tier; a 400 « You have reached your specified [workspace] API usage limits » for a limit the operator set on the organisation or a workspace), read as the provider's limit by the status and by the words. A limit at the provider's is recommended, against abuse such as a key that leaks, never required; its reverse is said: reached, the whole watch takes the code's path until the next month, or until the operator raises or removes their own limit; without one, a key that leaks is bounded by the tier's cap alone (none in Custom). A count file deleted starts the day from 0 without a card; one unreadable says it on a card. The amounts and the breaker's behaviour are unchanged.
