### Security
- **The threat model names two more boundaries: the watch's Microsoft Graph read and the Stratus binary** (SEC-D, #636; security audit of 06/11, P-02; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md` §5, `docs/design/orodruin-contract.md`). The watch's Graph read (IR1a): four read-only application permissions, set by hand outside Terraform, that apply to the whole tenant and, on H0, to the watch, the console and the bot, which share the VM's identity; what holds it (a GET-only reader, the console never reading Graph, no write permission ever, nothing by default) and what stays open (no start-up contract re-reads the watch's Graph rights; planned: #638). The Stratus Red Team binary launched from Orodruin (R2a): what holds it (closed catalog allow-list, pinned sha256 re-checked before every phase, each phase journaled and counted under the cap from an armed contract, enrolled bench group in zone `bench` only, offline Terraform) and what stays open (no target input, Orodruin's environment and identity inherited, so its reach is Orodruin's RBAC; the binary is not shipped yet; planned: R2b, #608, then R2c). H0's line no longer says its identity holds « nothing else » without naming Graph. §4 now names what the correlation copies from Syslog onto the line sent to the model (`SuccessfulLogins`: an account and a method, 64 characters at most each; `SessionOrigin`), fields that a local user can write under sshd's tag (planned: #632).
