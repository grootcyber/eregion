### Changed
- **A catalogue entry `proven` counts as an `implemented` one in every blue reader** (STM1g, #894; `docs/fichiers-cles.md`, `docs/dagorlad.md`). The catalogue's status is to gain a third value, `proven` — measured on the bench, a notch above `implemented` (STM1). One predicate in the shared core, `eregion_core.experiment.is_ready` (`READY_STATUSES = ("implemented", "proven")`), is now what every reader asks, never the word `implemented` alone: the console's coverage matrix (its summary, the gaps of the proof, the evolution, the stalest proofs, and a `ready` field per row that the matrix page ranks, greys and labels by), the coverage indicator, Dagorlad's board (a card's handle, its order, the count per target type), the plan's judgement of a wire, the scheduled drill's eligibility, Annatar's proposed chain, and the techniques Glorfindel proposes a rule for. A `planned` entry stays out everywhere. The catalogue itself is unchanged: no entry is reclassified.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_catalog_ready.py` (each reader gives a `proven` entry exactly what it gives an `implemented` one, and leaves a `planned` one out; a reader of the code fails on any blue comparison of the status to `implemented` alone, and on a reader that stops asking the predicate). Mutants M6161–M6175, `scripts/mutants/STM1g.py`; M1818, M1985 and M3565 follow the code.
