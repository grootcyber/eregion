### Fixed
- **A control-plane operation whose row names neither its caller nor its resource is a card « Télémétrie incomplète », never a high card, never dropped** (CPT1, #930; `docs/glorfindel.md`, control plane). Seen on Imladris: `role-assignment-write` and `nsg-change-outside-glorfindel` made high cards that read « … par appelant inconnu sur ? », which no one could judge. Such a row (no `Caller`, no caller id from `Claims`, no `_ResourceId` nor `ResourceId`, and no caller named on its operation's lines) now makes a `control_plane_change` card of severity `medium`, which says the telemetry is incomplete without guessing what the event is, and whose first step is to read every line of the operation by its `CorrelationId`. It stays in TO JUDGE, is never approvable, never folded into another card. A grave row (Glorfindel's rules, its identity, its Run Command) stays critical; a summary card stays high; a row that names its caller or its resource keeps its high card.
- **A control-plane card names the caller from the ids the rules draw from `Claims` when `Caller` is empty** (CPT1, #930). It said « appelant inconnu » although the row carried `CallerObjectId` or `CallerAppId`; it now names the object id, or else the application id (said as the tool under a user's token).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_control_plane_incomplete.py`.
