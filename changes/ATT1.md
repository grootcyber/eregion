### Security
- **A card on a detection attributed to no VM is never approvable in one click, whatever gate held it first** (ATT1, #936; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La garde d'attribution prime sur toute retenue antérieure »). Seen live on Imladris: a detection that names no VM, below the confidence threshold, became a `low_confidence` card carrying `isolate_vm`, and the console offered « Approve & execute » — one click would have isolated the VM the card is only filed on (the first watched VM by name). The attribution guard left as soon as an earlier gate (the confidence) had held the decision. It now runs after every gate and before the mode: on an unattributed detection, an action aimed at the VM (`isolate_vm`, `block_suspicious_ip`, `release_isolation`) is always the attribution's card (`unattributed_signal`), whether the confidence, the signal guardrail, the telemetry, the release precondition or never_block held it before, and that earlier reason is kept in the card's text. The mode's hold stays the mode's: a `mode_hold` card there only approves the model's own action when it is not aimed at the VM (a snapshot, which the guard does not hold).
- **Everywhere one approves, an unattributed card's action aimed at its VM is refused** (ATT1, #936). Whatever its type, a card that says it is unattributed and holds such an action is refused by `approval_refusal` (the console's route and the signed action queue), and the watch signs no proposal on it. The console shows neither « ▶ Approve & execute » nor « 📸 Snapshot » on it (nor « 🔄 Restore » on any unattributed card, which its route refuses), the bot neither Restore nor Revert, and the TUI offers no restore nor release. An attributed card keeps its behaviour: below the threshold it stays `low_confidence`, approvable.

### Fixed
- **The suggested commands of an unattributed card no longer name the VM it is filed on** (ATT1, #936). The fallback steps of `glorfindel pending`, the bot's `📋 Command`, the console's `📋 Cmd` and the step the cycle adds to a snapshot's card now say « not attributed », its candidates, and refer the judgement to the CLI, then the ack. They no longer suggest `az vm show -n <anchor>`, `glorfindel restore <anchor>` or `glorfindel snapshot <anchor>`.
- **The watch's return channel is set aside under the shipped `data-exfiltration-blob` only** (ATT1, #936). That rule is recognized by its name and by the fingerprint of its query as the base ships it. An override in the client's layer that would compute a wider `Known` sets nothing aside, and its rows stay cards (`BlobWriters` taken off all the same). An exact copy (WL1) is set aside as the shipped rule is.
