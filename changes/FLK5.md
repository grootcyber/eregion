### Fixed
- **Two intermittent tests steadied, neither weakened: every pass of the watch a test started has ended before the test's redirections are undone, and a decision held on the end of the read alone, never let go by a clock** (FLK5, #1220). No product code changes: both causes were in the tests.
  - `test_bot_fallback.py::test_one_relay_pass_at_a_time` (an error at the end of the CI of ENG1a, 25/11, and of main, 24/11: « the test suite wrote in the repository's folder: runs/glorfindel/config/relaxed.json »). The test before it runs the real loop of `glorfindel watch`; its last housekeeping starts the view « Relaxed » in a thread of its own, right before the loop stops. That thread could still be running once the test's redirections were undone: it then read `relaxed._STORE` again, `runs/glorfindel/config/relaxed.json` relative to the current folder, which is the repository's, and wrote there during the setup of the next test, which HY1 named. The conftest redirects that path for each test; `runs_layout` does not, by design. Reproduced every time by holding the thread until the redirection is undone, and 2 times in 100 by delaying it at random by up to 80 ms; never in 100 runs after the fix. A new autouse fixture, `watch_passes_end_with_their_test` (`tests/unit/conftest.py`), records every pass a test starts (`Solo`, the reassertion, the notices, the view, the dossiers…) and waits for them before `monkeypatch` undoes anything, 10 s at most in all; a pass still running then fails its own test, by its name, instead of writing during another one. `test_repo_hermetic.py` measures it in a throwaway repository: a pass that ends 0.5 s after its test has ended when the next one starts, and one that never ends fails its test at teardown.
  - `test_runs_channel.py::test_the_lines_waiting_for_one_vm_are_bounded` (`3 == 4`). FLK4 held each slow decision until the poll that read the lines had ended, but let it go after 10 s of the real clock, so a red test would never hang. A poll that lasted longer than that, on a stalled runner, brought `3 == 4` back: the first decision ended, gave its room back, and the seventh line found room. Reproduced 8 times out of 8, on this test and on `test_one_search_per_vm_and_technique_and_a_bounded_number_of_them`, by refusals that each take 4 s; green 8 times out of 8 after the fix, and 40 times in a row under `-n 4`. The helper `_run_watch` (`test_signal_origin.py`) now holds a slow decision or search on that event alone, and lets every held one go when the watch returns, so none is left waiting after the test. No run of the CI since FLK4 (24/11) shows this test red: the bound found is the only clock left on that path.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/conftest.py`, `tests/unit/test_repo_hermetic.py`, `tests/unit/test_signal_origin.py`.
