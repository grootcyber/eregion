### Fixed
- **The isolation TTL is read in one place: `check-ttl --ttl 0` escalates everything as the variable at 0 does; a negative or unreadable TTL is refused, and the watch keeps 4 h** (TTL1, #812; `docs/glorfindel.md`). Found by JUG2d's review. The TTL came two ways that did not agree on 0: `GLORFINDEL_ISOLATION_TTL_H=0` escalated every isolation at once, while `glorfindel check-ttl --ttl 0` was silently ignored (`ttl or …` fell back to the variable or to 4 h); a negative value passed (it escalated everything, as 0 did); an unreadable variable raised a `ValueError` at the watch's start, before its loop — the watch did not start. Now one function reads it, for the watch and for `check-ttl` (`config.isolation_ttl_h`): `--ttl` wins when it is given, 0 included; else the variable, 4 h when it is unset or empty. 0 keeps its meaning, « escalate everything », by the option as by the variable (Jonathan's decision, 14/11). A negative value, or one that is not a number (unreadable, `nan`), is refused: `check-ttl` says why and exits 1, nothing escalated (`--dry-run` included); the watch never stops for a TTL — it says it at its start, on the console and in the log (`glorfindel.ttl`), and keeps 4 h. `reassert.ttl_alerts` is unchanged: it escalates, it releases nothing (Q1). `--ttl`'s help, `.envrc.example`, README and `docs/operations.md` say it. Limits: `inf` is not negative and is accepted — the TTL then never escalates anything; `--ttl abc` is refused by click (code 2) before the function.
