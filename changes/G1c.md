### Added
- **The benign noise has its own targeted plan and apply, behind a check of the bench's subscription** (G1c, part of #76; `gothmog/README.md`, « Reste, sur le banc, avec Jonathan »). `make celebrimbor-noise-plan` and `make celebrimbor-noise-up [YES=1]` plan and apply G1's eleven resources alone: the Chaos agent's identity, its Microsoft-Agent target and extension, two capabilities, three experiments and their three roles. The VM that takes the noise carries the agent's identity and changes with them. A full `celebrimbor-up` would take Glorfindel's rules off the shared NSG, and terraform run by hand takes az's active subscription.
  - Before any call to terraform, `CELEBRIMBOR_SUB_GUARD` refuses when `AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` is empty, or when `ARM_SUBSCRIPTION_ID` or az's active subscription is empty or another. The comparison ignores case, and the message names the variable, never its value.
  - On 20/11, an apply run as raw terraform went to another subscription, whose policies refused it. A plan cannot show this: a resource to create is « known after apply ». DCR1's `celebrimbor-dcr-plan` and `celebrimbor-dcr-up` take the same guard. It is written to be reused by the other `celebrimbor-*` targets, in a lot of its own.

  - the tracked config's case touches gondolin alone, and one more assertion proves that no resource of G1 aims at another VM.
