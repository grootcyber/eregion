### Added
- **`glorfindel isolate` and `glorfindel block` say the VM's enrollment zone on one line before the prompt, and never refuse for it** (CMD1b, #811; Jonathan's decision of 14/11; `README.md`, « CLI reference », `docs/operations.md`, `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md` §2). O0's production rule bounds what Eregion injects, not what it contains, and the gesture is reversible (`release`, `unblock`): the zone is information, never a guard. It is read as the console reads it — the VM's tags and its resource group's, by `TagEnrollment` with the host's identity (two GETs through Azure Resource Manager, never a write, no SDK of a provider outside its adapter), judged by `enrollment` (`eregion_core/inscription.py`) — once the guards hold: `bench` and `twin` said as they are; `production` said a real load, with the command that lifts the gesture; a VM not enrolled (its reason), a zone none of the three, tags unreadable (a 403, no credential, an error) each said as such — « nothing says it is the bench's: perhaps a real load » — never taken for `bench`. The line is there with `--yes` too, and with `--dry-run`, which reads the zone and says the real one, in the same cases as a real run (Jonathan's decision of 14/11): a dry run of `isolate` and `block` writes nothing on Azure, it may read — the guards, then the two GETs of the tags, bounded. `block --scope subnet` adds that the zone is the named VM's only: its rule, on the subnet's NSG, reaches every VM of the subnet, whose zones are not read. The read waits 20 s at most (the credential's search has no bound of its own), then the zone is said unreadable; a VM the old chaos tag alone enrolls is said « bench (ancien tag) ». No zone, and no read that fails, stops the gesture or changes its exit code; a gesture a guard refuses (perimeter, anchored card, read-only credentials) reads no tag. What Azure and a tag say is masked (no subscription's id), one printable line, never taken as markup. `glorfindel/gestures.py` (`tags_read`, `TAGS_READER`, `zone_of`); the tests' reader reads nothing (`tests/unit/conftest.py`, `no_tags`).
