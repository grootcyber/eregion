### Changed
- **The console's snapshot names the vault's job, and the bench follows it** (SNP1, #789; `docs/banc-t1486.md`, « le job du coffre »; `docs/design/file-actions-signee.md`). `POST /api/action/snapshot/<vm>` gave only `job_id`, Glorfindel's own record (`snapshot-<vm>-<ts>`), which is no job of the vault: `make bench-run` looked for the job it had asked for by its time, within 2 minutes. The answer now also gives `vault_job`, the vault's job as Azure named it (`rsv:<vault>/<group>/<name>`), `""` when it named none (a dry run); `job_id` is unchanged for those who read it. Under the signed action queue (FS1), the watch's receipt carries the same `vault_job` in its signed `result`, and the console reads its shape again before saying it. `scripts/bench_snapshot.py` follows that job directly; a job named in another vault or group than the workstation's stops the run before Orodruin; only without it (a console or a watch from before this change) does it look for the job by its time, and its line says so. The limit: a watch not yet updated writes no `vault_job` in its receipt, and the bench then falls back on the time.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_bench_snapshot.py` (the console's real route behind the forward, Azure's answer faked: the job named and followed, no job list read; a dry run's id named none, looked for by its time; a foreign vault or group stops, direct or under FS1), `tests/unit/test_action_queue_async.py` (the watch's signed receipt names the vault's job; a receipt that names none, or a shape not read, is answered with none).
