### Added
- **The identity map: who holds what, from where, under which identity — Azure, Entra ID, GitHub, Discord, the workstation, the phone, Imladris and Orodruin** (IDM1, #687 ; `docs/design/identites.md`, `scripts/identity_map.py`). Four mermaid diagrams (the network, the defence's identities, the red team's and the bench's, the workshop), each link saying what crosses it and under which identity. A matrix, one line per identity — 37, among them 4 planned (the Stratus identity of R2/R2c, the restore executor of FS1g, the TOTP secret of TOT1, the dedicated Graph identity of 0.6.2) —: where it lives, where its secret lives or that it has none, who uses it, its rights by kind of scope, what it cannot do, its rotation and expiry, the threat model's boundary that holds it, and whether the repository declares it, Eregion's code draws it, or it is made by hand. A register ties each declaration of the repository to its lines: every role of `infra/identities/`, every Terraform identity or right (role assignments and definitions, user-assigned identities, every `azuread_*` resource, every `identity` and `admin_ssh_key` block), every identity variable of the compose and of the example environments, every secret and write permission of the workflows. `python3 scripts/identity_map.py check` says what is missing, `list` what the repository declares. No real identifier: roles, kinds of scope, generic names. How `make bench-preflight` would compare the live assignments to the map is proposed as a follow-up (§5). The threat model, `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/operations.md` (« Identités Azure ») point to it.
  - Tests : `tests/unit/test_identity_map.py` — the map holds every declaration; one added without its line (a role file, an assignment, a definition, an Entra ID resource, a user-assigned identity, a managed identity or an SSH key on a resource, a pair in the compose, a token, a key, a commented pair, a workflow's secret, a job's OIDC token, a top write permission) fails; a stale row, an unknown line, a « dépôt » line nothing feeds, a planned line already declared, a missing column or boundary, a role not named on its lines fail.
