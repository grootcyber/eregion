### Security
- **No output of the CLI meant for a human shows a subscription's id** (MSK2, #762; IML1c's follow-up; `docs/operations.md`, `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 6, `docs/demarrer/mobile.md`). An ARM id is said by its resource's name and its group (« vm-01 (groupe rg-a) »), a subscription's id left bare (« The subscription '…' could not be found », or the one the environment names) by « <abonnement> » (`glorfindel/masking.py`), at the display only. This holds for Azure's own error text at the CLI's boundary (a 403 cites « over scope '/subscriptions/…' ») and for every refusal; for the refusal of an ambiguous short name, which lists the VMs by their name and group (two of one name and one group in two subscriptions told apart by a rank); for the decision rendered by `respond` and by `restore --wait` without `--keep-isolated` (explanation, reasoning, escalation reason, « Glorfindel agent error »); for `glorfindel release` and `unblock` (their `Resource :` line, their result, what stays after a partial lift), `snapshot` (`Resource :`, `Vault :`, its `glorfindel jobs` hint, its verification), `jobs` (its second step, its error, its poll error), `list`, `door` (its scopes, its unreadable reads; `--json` keeps the ids, asked for), `reset` (what stays), the cards of `glorfindel pending` and `pending --watch` (the reason, masked before it is cut at 120 characters, the model's suggested steps, and its own steps, which name the VM by its short name only when that name comes back to the card's very resource — otherwise, two VMs of that name, a VM no longer known here while another of its name in another group is, a NSG or an account, they ask for the id: the short name would have restored the other VM, `--yes` asking nothing), the rules `pending` proposes (the model's analysis, explanation and KQL query, written with the full id in its prompt; `approve-rule` takes the proposal's id) and the reasons of `glorfindel activate` (`other_subscription` names two subscriptions: the connector's and the VM's are masked even when the environment names neither). The watch's own log lines (`glorfindel watch`, docker logs) are the machine's journal: not masked, Azure's error text keeps its ids there. The model's words are filtered where they are shown, never where they are made: neither `_build_user_message()`, nor `_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, nor `few_shot_examples.yaml` changes, and only `glorfindel/cli.py` imports the masking. The signal, the state files and the machine's journals (JSONL) keep the full id, and every command still takes one.
  - **`glorfindel list --ids`**: the VMs' full ids (this terminal only). Without it, a VM is said by its name and group, and the commands it suggests take the short name; a name two VMs hold, the hint asks for the id (`<id: glorfindel list --ids>`), since every command refuses that name.
  - The guides' reservation of IML1c is lifted for what MSK2 covers; what stays to read again before pasting: the names themselves (VM, group, vault) and, in Azure's message, a principal's object id or a tenant's id, which nothing masks.
  - **The masking** reads an id encoded whole (`%2Fsubscriptions%2F<guid>`), the JSON of an error body (`"subscriptionId": "<guid>"`) and « subscription identifier '<guid>' », with no environment naming the subscription; the « / » before an id stays when a path precedes it (`…/resource/groupe rg`). Known limits, when the environment does not name the subscription (`AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`, mandatory, covers the main one): `"subscriptionId" : "<guid>"` (a separator of 5 characters), `%3D<guid>`, a double encoding (`%252Fsubscriptions%252F`) and a JSON list (`subscriptions: ["<guid>"]`) pass. A step the model wrote, once masked (« glorfindel restore vm (groupe rg) --yes »), no longer copies: the shell refuses it (a syntax error), nothing runs.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_cli_masking.py` (each path, the journal, the card and the signal keeping the full id; the guard that only `glorfindel/cli.py` imports the masking sees every form of import), `tests/unit/test_entries_calls.py` (the ambiguous short name).
