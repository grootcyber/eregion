### Security
- **No output of the CLI meant for a human shows a subscription's id** (MSK2, #762; IML1c's follow-up; `docs/operations.md`, `docs/banc-t1486.md`, step 6, `docs/demarrer/mobile.md`). An ARM id is said by its resource's name and its group (« vm-01 (groupe rg-a) »), a subscription's id left bare (« The subscription '…' could not be found », or the one the environment names) by « <abonnement> » (`glorfindel/masking.py`), at the display only. This holds for Azure's own error text at the CLI's boundary (a 403 cites « over scope '/subscriptions/…' ») and for every refusal; for the refusal of an ambiguous short name, which lists the VMs by their name and group (two of one name and one group in two subscriptions told apart by a rank); for the decision rendered by `respond` and by `restore --wait` without `--keep-isolated` (explanation, reasoning, escalation reason, « Glorfindel agent error »); for `glorfindel release` and `unblock` (their `Resource :` line, their result, what stays after a partial lift), `snapshot` (`Resource :`, `Vault :`, its `glorfindel jobs` hint, its verification), `jobs` (its second step, its error, its poll error), `list`, `door` (its scopes, its unreadable reads; `--json` keeps the ids, asked for) and `reset` (what stays). The model's words are filtered where they are shown, never where they are made: neither `_build_user_message()`, nor `_SYSTEM_PROMPT`, nor `few_shot_examples.yaml` changes, and only `glorfindel/cli.py` imports the masking. The signal, the state files and the machine's journals (JSONL) keep the full id, and every command still takes one.
  - **`glorfindel list --ids`**: the VMs' full ids (this terminal only). Without it, a VM is said by its name and group, and the commands it suggests take the short name; a name two VMs hold, the hint asks for the id (`<id: glorfindel list --ids>`), since every command refuses that name.
  - The guides' reservation of IML1c is lifted for what MSK2 covers; what stays to read again before pasting: the names themselves (VM, group, vault) and, in Azure's message, a principal's object id, which nothing masks.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_cli_masking.py` (each path, the journal and the signal keeping the full id), `tests/unit/test_entries_calls.py` (the ambiguous short name). Mutants M4950–M4959.
