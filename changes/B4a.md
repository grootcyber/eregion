### Added
- **The observation report by confidence: each band and each rule × band with its count, too few said; the share judged right above the gate's threshold; where the cards come from** (B4a, #991, part of #75; `docs/observation.md`, « Par confiance et par provenance »). `glorfindel report` counted the operator's verdicts by action and by rule, never by confidence: the autonomy gate's threshold rested on no measure. It now says, in text and in `--json` (and `GET /api/report`), read from the cards alone (`glorfindel/calibration.py`), with no call to the cloud or the model and nothing the model reads changed:
  - the bands (0, 0.5), [0.5, 0.7), [0.7, 0.8), [0.8, 0.9), [0.9, 1], then each rule × band: right, wrong, unsure, not judged, and how many were judged. Under 10 judged cards a cell says « too few », never a percentage (`too_few`, `right_share: null`). The share is the part judged right among the judged: an unsure verdict is not judged right;
  - above the threshold: the cards at a confidence ≥ the threshold, whatever their action (`confidence >= threshold`, the gate's comparison; the gate itself applies to `AUTONOMOUS_ACTIONS` only), at the threshold as the gate resolves it (`GLORFINDEL_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD`, 0.7 unset). A malformed threshold lets nothing through: said `null`, nothing above it;
  - the confidence counted is the card's `confidence`, not `first_confidence`. It is the one written beside the reason and the explanation the operator judged, and a re-fire rewrites the three together; `first_confidence` is shown by no console, bot or CLI. Two kinds of card are counted apart, never guessed: those without the model's confidence (0, the default of a card written without a decision of the model: a failed cycle, a detection without a decision), and those rewritten after their verdict (`glorfindel verdict` leaves a card pending);
  - the provenance, read from the card's resource id: `bench` inside the bench's perimeter as the injectors' guard draws it (`Scope.check`: the subscription of `AZURE_SUBSCRIPTION_ID`, `rg-celebrimbor` or `rg-celebrimbor-*`), which takes in the pre-observation VM, not enrolled; the criteria before autonomy do not count these cards. `unknown` for everything else: the twin, production, any other park, or a card attributed to no VM (its `attribution`, or the older `unattributed_signal`). A card carries no zone, and the zone is a tag of enrolled VMs only, read through Azure.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_observation.py`. Mutants M6815–M6819, M6855 (`scripts/mutants/B4a.py`).
