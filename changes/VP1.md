### Added
- **A verdict proposed on the expected exercise card of the weekly drill, confirmed in one click** (VP1, #615; `docs/design/drill-banc.md` §8.2 and §12, `docs/observation.md`, `docs/design/console.md`, `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md` section 10). The exercise card that is the detection the drill of the week expects — its rule, on its target, within its delay after the run's T0 and inside the window, the first card of its wire — receives a proposed verdict (`right`), computed every minute by the watch on Imladris from what this host holds: the drill's own copy in its image (read in the shipped layer, never the instance's), its fingerprint equal to the one the request's status announces, the expectations of the image, the run's report and the window the watch believes. Anything that does not agree — another rule, another VM, outside the delay or the window, a second card, two versions of the drill — proposes nothing, and the card says why.
  - A proposed verdict is never a verdict given: a field of its own on the card, the card stays in TO JUDGE, and neither the report nor the indicators count it. Only an exercise card ever carries one. It compares the detection only: the decision stays the operator's to judge.
  - The console (TO JUDGE and the card opened from it) and the Discord bot show it with two buttons: **Confirm** makes it the operator's verdict (and acknowledges the card, as any verdict), **Refuse** erases it for good, the card still to judge. Texts escaped; no inline script.
  - The model's message is unchanged: the decision reads no card (`tests/unit/test_dagorlad_blind.py`).
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_proposed_verdict.py`, `tests/unit/test_proposed_verdict_bot.py`; mutants M5921–M5940 (`scripts/mutants/VP1.py`).
