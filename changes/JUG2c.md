### Fixed
- **Without the Discord bot, neither a session cut's closure nor the reassertion waits for a webhook** (JUG2c, #788; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « alerting webhook + bot Discord »). Found by the second review of JUG2b, older than it: the bot not configured, a cut's closure went by the webhook from `vm_notices.say` while the VM's lock was held (an isolation of the same VM waited for the POST), and every card the reassertion writes went by `escalations._notify` (`requests.post`, 5 s, the name's resolution unbounded) from its thread — the reassertion of every VM waited for that webhook. Now, where no webhook may be waited for (`vm_notices.must_defer`: the thread holds a VM's lock, `locks.holds_vm_lock`, or runs the reassertion's pass, `vm_notices.deferred`), nothing is posted: the notice is queued in the notices' store (`via: outbox`, `queued`), once by its key — the closure's nonce, or `card:<id>` for a card (`vm_notices.queue_card`). The fallback's pass posts it in its own thread, bounded as every post (10 s, the name's resolution included), bot or no bot: at once in the watch (`vm_notices.serve`: a notice queued starts a pass of its own; a pass running starts one more at its end), else at its next pass. Its message is the one it always had, written at its post, never from a payload kept in the store (a card's by `escalations.card_payload`, escaped and masked), and never a ping. A 2xx notes it sent, never said again; refused, unreachable or rate limited, it goes back to the queue — not to an absent bot — with the fallback's tries and their cap (3: then no pass posts it, and the log says it is shown on the console only); a notice posted at once before (`via: webhook`) is never taken up. The store refused (a full disk), a card is posted from a thread nobody waits for. Elsewhere (the graph's cycle, the console, the CLI outside a lock), a card still goes at once. `glorfindel check-ttl` (from cron, without the watch: `make glorfindel-check-ttl`) writes its card under the VM's lock, so queued: it runs the fallback's pass before it ends, outside the lock, bounded — the card said as before JUG2c, and one an earlier run left queued too (the review's round 1); no other command writes a card or a closure under a VM's lock. Limits: a queued notice whose answer was lost (a read timeout, a 5xx) is said again, 3 times at most, then only on the console's card; a notice queued by another process than the watch, and not posted by it, waits for the watch's next pass.
