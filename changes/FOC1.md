### Added
- **The focus in the infrastructure map** (FOC1, #1231; `docs/design/console.md`, « Le focus au survol »). Jonathan's choice on a mockup (26/11): the octopus stays the default view, its lines made readable. A tile hovered, or given the keyboard's focus (each tile now takes Tab), stays sharp; the other tiles and nodes step back to about 30 %, its lines come to the front at 3 px and the others fade, a flowing line included. A backend hovered: its lines and the tiles they reach. About 120 ms of delay, so the map never flickers from one tile to the next, and the focus survives the map's refresh every 5 s without a blink, the keyboard's tile keeping its focus; no focus while a tile is dragged, while a window is open, on a phone, or in another view than the tiles (the table); a click still unfolds a tile; no fade under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Each line now says the tile it reaches and the node it leaves (`data-k`, `data-b`), escaped.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_focus.py`; mutants M7878–M7889 and M7921–M7926 (`scripts/mutants/FOC1.py`).
