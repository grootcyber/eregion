### Security
- **The snapshot of a detection attributed to no VM is never approvable in one click, nor run alone, whatever the confidence** (SEC-Ed, #954; `docs/glorfindel.md`, « La garde d'attribution prime sur toute retenue antérieure »). On such a detection, the answer surest of itself got the most permissive card: a snapshot at a high confidence held by the `human_only` mode stayed approvable in one click (`mode_hold`), and in `non_disruptive` it ran alone on the VM the card is only filed on, while the same snapshot under the threshold was the attribution's card. The attribution guard now holds the snapshot too (`VM_AIMED_ACTIONS`, one set for the guard and the approval): whatever the confidence and the mode, its card is the attribution's (`unattributed_signal`), the reason held before kept in its text. Everywhere one approves, it is refused: the server (`approval_refusal`, the console's route), the signed action queue (a card written before this change and its old proposal included), the bot (a snapshot is no verb it may ask), and the console shows neither « ▶ Approve & execute » nor « 📸 Snapshot » on it. The card says why and that the human chooses the VM to snapshot (its reason, the console's note « the detection names no VM: choose the VM to snapshot », the `📋 Cmd` of the console and of the bot), never a command on the VM it is filed on. Every autonomous action is now held there: the mode holds none, and the floor's reason on such a detection is the attribution's. An attributed detection does not change: its snapshot runs alone in `non_disruptive` and is approvable in one click in `human_only`.
