### Security
- **The root-compromise floor proposes the isolation, it never runs it; the decision queue bounds its critical class** (SEC-E, #846; `docs/glorfindel.md`, the gates of `decide` and « La file de décision, bornée » ; `docs/design/correlation.md`, B3a ; `docs/design/modele-de-menace.md`, sections 3 and 4). Until its two facts are proven by a source the VM does not write, the floor (a brute force that succeeded, then root, on the same VM) puts a card `floor_proposal` that proposes `isolate_vm`, approvable in one click, whatever the autonomy mode — never an isolation it runs.
  - The floor now plays last: the gates and the agreement of the draws judge the model's own choice, never an action raised; the model's own action, when it runs alone, runs as its gates judged it, and the floor's card sits beside it. The confidence kept is the model's in its own choice, said as such.
  - The floor's facts are dated and live for a TTL of their own (900 s, absolute): no later signal of the VM prolongs them, nor does a detection the decision queue held. A whole release of the VM's isolation forgets them — `glorfindel release` (the console's and the bot's included), `glorfindel reset`, a request of the action queue, the watch's own release after a restore — never a release the model chose on a detection.
  - A detection that does not name the VM (unattributed, or a call from behind an appliance) keeps its card, never approvable in one click: the floor is only said in its reason.
  - The decision queue: a critical detection counts as a grave one in the ceiling of calls to the model and waits there; it is deduplicated by VM and rule whatever its address (the address is named on the card); at most three distinct addresses per VM and window call the model; a detection of a grave technique never leaves a full queue for another one; the pair of the floor is no longer exempt from the dedup once a decision opened its window. The severity a line of the runs/ channel writes no longer sets its class — its technique does; what the model reads is unchanged.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_root_floor.py`, `tests/unit/test_decision_queue.py`; mutants M5721–M5740, M5910–M5911 (`scripts/mutants/SEC-E.py`).
