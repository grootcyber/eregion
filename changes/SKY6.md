### Changed
- **On a desktop, the 360° without its column: To judge, the feed and the legend in the sky; a click opens a bubble where the target is** (SKY6, #1268; `docs/design/console.md`, « Sur ordinateur, les listes dans le ciel »). Jonathan's choices (27/11, on a mockup). The sky fits the window, width and height. The lists go in the room the circle leaves: the feed at the top left and To judge at the top right, at the edges, the legend at the bottom left as wide as the circle lets it; in a window nearly square, in its corners; in a tall one, under the circle — never into the orbit's labels. The column's own nodes are moved into the sky, never copied: To judge keeps JUG3's boxes, « select all », its bar and its confirmation by rule. « group by » goes into the INFRASTRUCTURE line, the bands to the sky's top. A click on a line of To judge or of the feed, or on a target, opens its bubble beside it, off the lists and off its target: its state, what waits on it with its tile's own buttons and guards (read-only, out of reach), its cards to judge with all the list says of them (the IP a block targets, its time unreadable, its exercise, the verdict a drill proposes), their explanation, a note and their verdicts; as tall as its room, its content scrolling; « open its card ▸ » to the card. The bubble opens by itself for a card that comes to judge or to approve, a minute by the server's clock, never while something is pinned nor under an open window. The corners' lists are as tall as they may be, then scroll; the lists keep where they were read at each refresh. The table and a phone are unchanged.
