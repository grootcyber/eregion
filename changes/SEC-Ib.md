### Security
- **The readiness audit reads a VM by its resource id, never by its host name** (SEC-Ib, #910; `docs/glorfindel.md`, the attribution of a line; `docs/fichiers-cles.md`, `audit.py`). The two telemetry checks of `glorfindel audit` (« Disk write sampling », « Syslog ») filtered `_ResourceId =~ <id> or Computer =~ <name>`: a host of the same workspace named like the VM made a VM whose telemetry is absent look present, and the rules that read it covered — a blind VM passed for ready. They now read the VM by the resource id only, as the rules route a row.
  - A VM whose lines carry no resource id (some agents, a hypothesis still to measure on the bench) is said absent, its rules blind — they are blind for it too —, never present nor unknown; the lines under its host name without a resource id are counted and said, with what to look at (the agent, not only the DCR), never taken for the VM's: they may be a namesake's. That count is read only when the VM is absent; unreadable, the check stays absent, its reason unsaid.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_audit.py` (a workspace in memory, its rows selected as the queries are written); mutants M6301–M6310 (`scripts/mutants/SEC-Ib.py`).
