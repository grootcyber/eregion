### Added
- **The bench in one gesture: the calm and « restore first » verified by the preflight, the clean point taken by the 📸 route, the preflight replayed** (BNC3b, #770; `docs/design/banc-autonome.md` §3 rows 3 and 5, §3.1, §3.2; `docs/banc-t1486.md`). `make bench-preflight` reads the target's activity log over 89 days and the vault's jobs, under the operator's `az login`: the calm (started 10 min ago at least, no Run Command for 10 min) is a ✗ that says the minutes left, never a wait; « restore first » refuses a VM that carries any Run Command since its last restore, whatever its caller, but a session drain of Imladris within [−2 min, +7 min] of a verified isolation of the watch, and a run of the current Orodruin whose technique reads as non-destructive (its journal in the channel, then its report, against the catalogue). A read that fails, a read cut at its bound, a last restore beyond the horizon, a restore in progress: a ✗, never a ✓ (`scripts/bench_history.py`). Once the request is confirmed, `make bench-run` keeps the newest recovery point only when it was taken after the VM's last restore and outside every quarantine over every run (`scripts/bench_h0_reader.py history`, in its disposable, network-less container); otherwise it asks for one by `POST /api/action/snapshot/<vm>` of Imladris's console, through a short port forward it opens itself (host key pinned, no multiplexed master, closed whatever the end), the console's token read in `host.env` after `CONSOLE_GUARD` and handed by the environment only, as a Bearer, without a proxy; under FS1 its receipt is followed (`not_taken`, `untrusted`, `refused`, `failed`, `unknown`, expired stop the run); the vault's job is followed to `Completed`, 60 min at most, before anything goes to Orodruin; then the preflight is replayed just before the attack (`scripts/bench_snapshot.py`). Nothing is read for the point, nor written, before the confirmation. Only a restore proven in place counts as the last restore — its job's detail (`az backup job show`; the field is a hypothesis, to measure on the bench) and a write on the VM during the job (the swap of its disks): a « Restore disks » or a new VM made by hand counts for nothing. One line of Orodruin's journal answers one Run Command; a Run Command without its own line is destructive. The calm counts a write on the VM and the end of its last restore as a start, and is unreadable when no start reads and the VM's creation does not. A partial history of Imladris (a line or a file unread, a mount missing, no cycle file) is no quarantine absent. The forward is a bounded remote command, closed on SIGTERM too; the console's token is taken out of every child's environment once read. Limits, written: a managed Run Command, a Custom Script extension or an SSH session are not seen; the activity log's ingestion delay is a hypothesis to measure. Verified on fakes; not yet measured on the bench.
