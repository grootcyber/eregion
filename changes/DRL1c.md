### Added
- **The weekly drill's scheduled start in Terraform: a Logic App that wakes Orodruin before the drill, shipped disabled, and Orodruin shut down every day at 23:00 UTC** (DRL1c, #613; `docs/design/drill-banc.md` §5.1, §14). `infra/orodruin/drill_autostart.tf` deploys an ARM template (`drill_autostart.json`): one Logic App (Consumption) whose one trigger is a weekly Recurrence in UTC at `drill_autostart_at` (default `tue 18:40`, the shipped drill's schedule less 20 min), and whose one action starts Orodruin's VM by the Logic App's own managed identity, without following the operation. The template carries the state `Disabled` itself and Terraform follows only the deployment, never the Logic App's state: the arming enables it (`make orodruin-drill-arm`), the disarming and the kill disable it, and no apply enables it — an apply that redeploys it puts it back to `Disabled`, a re-arming then expected. The azurerm trigger and action components were not used: they rewrite the whole workflow without its state. The Logic App's identity gets a custom role of one action (`Microsoft.Compute/virtualMachines/start/action`), defined and assignable in Orodruin's resource group alone, assigned at the scope of Orodruin's VM alone; Orodruin's own identity gains nothing, and its contract is unchanged. Orodruin's DevTest schedule deallocates it every day at 23:00 UTC. The output `drill_autostart_id` is the contract DRL1a fixed. Nothing is applied: the plan, the apply, the measurement and the arming stay the operator's.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_drill_autostart.py`, `infra/orodruin/tests/drill_autostart.tftest.hcl`. Mutants M5881–M5900 (`scripts/mutants/DRL1c.py`).
