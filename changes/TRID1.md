### Fixed
- **A tile is its asset, never its name** (TRID1, #1203; `docs/design/console.md`, « Une tuile par ressource »): two VMs of one name in two subscriptions folded into one tile — their cards mixed, the other subscription's card under the watch's VM. Now each tile is keyed by its resource id, in lower case (or the name it is filed by without one), two full ids compared without their case — an isolated VM keeps its place; the page's ids, folds, drags, windows, Cmd and lines follow the key, and the order dragged before, kept by name, is read again as keys. What Glorfindel closed is shown on its own VM only; a restore in course and a VM's decisions, read by name, never on a tile out of reach. A VM out of reach no longer counts among RECOVER's unprotected. The map's construction is taken out of `renderInfra` (`_tileMap`) and run by the suite. A VM's actions still reach the server by its name.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_console_tile_ids.py`; mutants M7727–M7740 and M7781–M7788 (`scripts/mutants/TRID1.py`).
