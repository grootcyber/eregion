### Added
- **The infrastructure map as a table, beside the tiles** (TBL1, #1230; `docs/design/console.md`, « La carte en tableau »). Jonathan's choice on a mockup (26/11), for large estates and the phone: a toggle « VIEW · tiles · table » next to « group by », kept in the browser; with no choice, the table on a phone whatever the estate's size (Jonathan, 26/11), and on a large screen the map at 360° (SKY1). A row per tile, in the lanes of « group by », its three modules as pills: DETECT `watched`, `to judge` or `a card`; PROTECT `ready`, `isolated`, `blocked`, `isolated · blocked`, `to approve` or `isolated · to approve`; RECOVER `protected`, `restoring`, or `not read` while the vaults' inventory is unread; « — » where a module does not reach. A VM's priority beside its name (CRT1). Rows open each on its own, several at once, and an open row shows the tile in full, unfolded, with its mode and its buttons. What asks for the eye opens by itself; a row the operator folds stays folded until its next incident after a return to calm, as a tile's fold. The totals filter the rows, and a filter whose total falls to 0 goes; « Open all · Fold all »; no line onto a row. On a phone, a row is 44 px and its modules three dots.
