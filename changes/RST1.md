  - « Eregion Glorfindel Restore Staging » (`infra/identities/glorfindel-restore-staging.json`): `listKeys` alone, given to the console and the bot on the staging account only — never on the group, which holds other storage accounts, never to the watch nor to H0's identity.

- **`make azure-pairs-expiry`: when the secrets of the workstation's three pairs end** (RST1; `docs/operations.md`, « Faire tourner un secret »). It reads `az ad app credential list` under the operator's `az login`, the end date only. It warns under 14 days, says « aucune paire » for an absent pair, prints neither a secret nor a client id, and exits 1 on an unreadable date or when no secret is valid; an expired secret beside a valid one is said as an old one to remove. `check-azure-env` and Glorfindel's start never call Graph. The bench's step 0 checks the console's pair three ways (`docs/banc-t1486.md`), and `.envrc.example` says the workstation that restores needs it.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_credential_expiry.py`, on a fake `az`.

### Fixed
- **A restore reads its rights before it touches the VM, and says Azure's refusal whole** (RST1, #680; `docs/azure.md`, « Le restore depuis la console »). On 09/11 the CLI neutralized the last Run Command and deallocated the VM, then met the refusal; the VM stayed stopped three hours, unsaid, and the refusal, cut at 300 characters, lost the action Azure named.
  - `Microsoft.Authorization/permissions` is read on the vault, the VM and the staging account, before the neutralization and the deallocation. The rights read: the vault's restore, `deallocate` and `start` on the VM, `listKeys` on the staging account. Wildcards and `notActions` are read as Azure reads them. A missing right refuses the restore early, naming it and its role, the VM untouched. Run Command missing on the VM is no refusal (SEC17). Unreadable rights are said, and the restore goes on as before: a hypothesis to measure on the bench. Deny assignments, locks, conditions and PIM are not returned by the API.
  - An `AuthorizationFailed` or `LinkedAuthorizationFailed` refusal names each missing action and the kind of its scope, never an id; another refusal is said with its ids masked; the snapshot's too. Past the deallocation, every failure says the VM is deallocated and the gesture: start it again after a refusal of authorization or validation (a 4xx other than 409: no restore left); after a 5xx, a 409, no answer or no job, read the vault's jobs first. A refused trigger leaves no trace in the activity log: only the CLI's output keeps it.
  - `glorfindel restore` says which kind of identity it runs under (`Identity :`): the console's pair, the bot's (in their containers, by `GLORFINDEL_AZURE_PAIR`, a constant of the compose; the watch gets none), the watch's, another, or no complete pair, when `DefaultAzureCredential` silently takes another identity. Never an id.
- **No `restoreDiskLunList` on a restore in place** (RST1). Azure now refuses the field on `OriginalLocation`, even complete: the job failed in 4.5 s, code `400625`. The same payload passed twice on 05/10; Azure hardened its validation between the two dates. Every disk of the point is restored.
- **A job that fails at once is read with its error** (RST1). The search after a trigger (`_find_backup_job`, also the watch's snapshot) kept an InProgress job only, for 45 s: a job failed at once read « not found after trigger », which pushed to trigger again. It now keeps a finished job too, says its `errorDetails`, and does not take a finished job started more than 30 s before the trigger (a previous attempt's). The search lasts by operation: 2 min for a restore (8 × 15 s), still 3 × 10 s for the watch's autonomous snapshot, which a ransomware's signal waits behind. Without a job it says to read the vault's jobs before triggering again, and, for a restore, that the VM is deallocated. A `--wait` ends on `CompletedWithWarnings` too.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_restore_console.py`, on a fake Azure (the permissions API per scope, the VM's writes in order, the trigger's answer, the vault's jobs). Mutants M4654–M4663 (`scripts/mutants/RST1.py`).
