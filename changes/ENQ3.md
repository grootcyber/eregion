### Added
- **The enquiry's knowledge base and its first sheet, T1486, read by nothing in production yet** (ENQ3, #1077; `docs/raisonnement.md` §10.1, `docs/architecture.md`, « L'enquête, et son catalogue de lectures » ; design: `docs/design/enquete-raisonnee.md` §2.3, §3). `glorfindel/enquiry/knowledge.py` holds the sheet of a technique, its hypotheses, their sense (confirms, infirms, neither), their effect (tightens, relaxes, neither — it must follow the sense), their states (proposed, supposed, measured, contested, withdrawn) and the pure rules by which a lesson changes them. The seed, `glorfindel/enquiry/fiches/T1486.yaml`, is the design's sheet: nine hypotheses on ransomware, three of them measured at the bench, each with its reads, its sources and its limit; it is to be reviewed by the maintainer before merging. Its validator refuses a sheet with an unknown key, a key given twice, a read neither in the catalogue nor announced (the dossier's reads and the new ones, which enter the catalogue one by one, measured), a sense and an effect that disagree, a state a seed cannot carry, or a `grave` that contradicts the grave techniques of the decision queue; a refused sheet is said, and its rule keeps today's enquiry. A seed folder that cannot be read, or holds no sheet, is an error, never an empty base.
  - The weights: the bench 1.0, a human verdict 0.8, the dossier 0.5 (a fact of the platform or of Glorfindel) or 0.2 (a fact of the guest), the model 0.1 — always drawn from the source, never read back from the store. What tightens applies alone; what relaxes never applies without a human's accord. Only the bench and a human verdict change a state; the model never counts, and a hypothesis it proposes is never shown to it; what the model may be shown of the others is built field by field, never a lesson that waits for an accord or a PR. The sense and the effect are never learned: a lesson asking to change them waits for a PR. The base never lifts a rule's ceiling.
  - `glorfindel/enquiry/evolutions.py`: the store of the evolutions, `~/.glorfindel/enquiry/knowledge.sqlite`, one SQLite table of events appended and never rewritten nor erased, 0600, never through a link; a file that is not a database raises. It is empty, and nothing writes it yet.
  - Nothing the model sees changes: neither the watch, nor the graph, nor `investigate` imports the base (a test); the prompt, the user message, the few-shot examples and the decision tool are untouched, ENQ2's sha256 unchanged. No end-to-end run is required.
  - Tests: `tests/unit/test_enquiry_knowledge.py`. Mutants M7108–M7113 and M7120 (`scripts/mutants/ENQ3.py`).
